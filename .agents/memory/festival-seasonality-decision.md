---
name: Festival seasonality decision
description: Confirmed treatment of Diwali-aligned seasonality evidence and the resulting forecasting policy.
---

Use a fixed calendar-month curve for company-level seasonality. Do not replace it with a Diwali-relative curve.

**Why:** The confirmed three-year territory-register analysis did not show a recurring Diwali-relative peak. March remained the company, PTMT, SWR, and Sanitaryware peak; CP moved contrary to the Diwali-date hypothesis. CPVC, CP, and Sanitaryware have materially different monthly shapes, so a company curve is not a segment forecast.

**How to apply:** Keep the current company monthly curve for broad company planning and explain its scope. When a feature forecasts or compares individual CPVC, CP, or Sanitaryware performance, prefer that segment's own curve rather than inheriting the company allocation. Treat within-month month-end timing as a separate concern from monthly seasonality.

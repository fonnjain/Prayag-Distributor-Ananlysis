---
name: Secondary period achievement basis
description: Company-level secondary achievement must use recorded-months plan as denominator, never the full period plan.
---
Rule: for secondary performance totals, the achievement numerator (recorded sales) and denominator (plan) must cover the SAME set of fully-recorded closed months. Never divide period sales by the full period plan. A period with a plan but zero fully-recorded months is an explicit "no actuals recorded yet" state — never 0% and never an unexplained dash.

**Why:** A full-FY selection early in the year includes future months that carry a plan but no sales; dividing by the full plan showed ~24.5% instead of ~78%. Matches the QA glossary rule that secondary YTD stops at the last recorded month.

**How to apply:** Any surface showing a secondary achievement % for a selected period must reuse the server-computed recorded-months figure rather than recomputing sales/plan, and label the figure when the period contains unrecorded months.

description: Evidence boundaries and proposal rules for history-grounded schemes.

Treat stored scheme definitions, slabs, raw item-group labels, and territory groups as structural precedent only. They do not establish qualification, payout, attributed orders, actual cost, control-group lift, or measured outcomes.

**Why:** The available scheme history contains definitions but no qualification ledger, payout ledger, or order-to-scheme linkage. Raw item-group rows also are not an independent canonical category universe.

**How to apply:** Copy only observed scheme structures. Label every figure by source, state unavailable outcomes and controls explicitly, and never claim never-covered canonical groups from raw mappings alone. A generated proposal must remain non-persisting, respect held/unknown-margin guards, use clearly labeled modeled economics, and include a numeric breakeven or refuse generation.

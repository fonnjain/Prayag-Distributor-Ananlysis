## 3. Regional Picture

Retail sales are heavily concentrated in the **East and Northeast**.

| State | Sales Head | Retailers | Retail Sales |
|---|---|---|---|
| West Bengal | Sandeep ji | 755 | ₹10.49 Cr |
| East U.P. | Rizvi ji | 609 | ₹4.17 Cr |
| Bihar | Sandeep ji | 295 | ₹3.06 Cr |
| Odisha | Sandeep ji | 196 | ₹3.00 Cr |
| Maharashtra | Lalan / Rizvi | 588 | ₹2.48 Cr |
| Jharkhand | Sandeep ji | 227 | ₹2.45 Cr |

**West Bengal alone is the anchor market**, delivering more than double the next state and holding the largest retailer base. At the individual level, one head — **Sandeep ji — accounts for ~56% of all retail sales** across 1,621 retailers, with Rizvi ji contributing a further ~20%. This is a strength (deep regional dominance) but also a concentration risk.

**Whitespace:** Mizoram, Goa, and Delhi NCR are effectively untapped (near-zero sales). Western and Southern India remain significant expansion opportunities relative to the eastern stronghold.

## 7. Strategic Priorities

1. **Protect the core.** PTMT + CP + Plumbing generate ~88% of revenue. Secure supply, pricing, and inventory for these lines above all else.
2. **De-risk the concentration.** With one head at ~56% of sales and one state (West Bengal) as the anchor, build depth in the second tier (East U.P., Bihar, Odisha, Maharashtra) to reduce single-point dependence.
3. **Convert coverage into sales.** The network reaches 31 states but earns in ~6. Prioritize activation in high-population, low-penetration markets (Western and Southern India, Delhi NCR).
4. **Ride the Q4 seasonality.** Demand concentrates sharply in the final quarter — align production planning, distributor stocking, and working capital to that curve.
5. **Sustain order momentum.** The FY2026-27 pipeline is running ahead of the prior-year pace; keep monitoring monthly bookings against the FY2024-25 base to confirm the growth is durable.

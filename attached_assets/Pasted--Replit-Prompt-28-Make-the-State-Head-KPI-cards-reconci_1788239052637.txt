## Corrections to my earlier statements

- **M. Raman is not in `noTarget`.** The band tests
  `sp.plan != null OR targetSecondary != null`, not `targetSecondary > 0`. His
  `secondaryPlan` is ₹12,30,000, he is in `below25`, and he is one of the 35 Low
  Performers. I read the band logic wrongly.
- **Low Performers is not hiding a quarter of the roster.** Only 2 of the 52
  `noTarget` members have a non-zero plan. The other 50 genuinely have none.

## Worth keeping in view

- The fallback fires only on **null**, never on zero. So 17 members holding
  ₹5.90 crore of `targetSecondary` contribute nothing to the KPI Target while the
  Data table shows them a target. The card and the table disagree by design.
- `achBand()` returns `noTarget` whenever `achievementPct` is null, so the band
  means "no target" *or* "no computable achievement" *or* "no fully recorded
  month" — three situations under one label.

GOAL
The seasonal curve is calibrated on FY2025-26 alone and has never been
validated. Make it rebuild from EVERY completed fiscal year, equally weighted,
with each rebuild recorded and never overwritten.

It is the denominator of every forward projection, so it must never change
without a trace.

============================================================
1 - VERSIONED, NOT OVERWRITTEN
============================================================
seasonal_curve
  id
  fiscal_years_used        array of the years that built it
  month_weights            12 values, summing to 100
  quarter_weights          4 values, summing to 100
  month_share_stddev       12 values - the spread across source years
  built_at · built_from    auto_rebuild | manual
  is_active
  source_rows              row count per source year, for reconciliation
  source_net               net per source year

Never overwrite. Each rebuild INSERTS a new version and flips is_active. Prior
versions are retained permanently.

Store what it was BUILT FROM, not only the result. When someone asks in two
years why a projection moved, the answer must be readable from the table.

============================================================
2 - EVERY COMPLETED YEAR COUNTS EQUALLY
============================================================
No recency weighting. Every frozen fiscal year contributes equally.

    for each year:   month share = that month's net / that year's total net
    curve weight  =  the mean of that month's share across all years

With three years that is (FY23-24 + FY24-25 + FY25-26) / 3. With six years it
is the average of six. The curve stabilises as history accumulates rather than
swinging with whatever last year happened to be.

AVERAGE THE SHARES, NOT THE RUPEES.
FY2025-26 is Rs 361 Cr; an earlier year may be Rs 280 Cr. Pooling rupees would
let the larger year dominate the shape - which is recency weighting arriving
through the back door. Each year gets one equal vote on the SHAPE, regardless
of its size.

Normalise so the twelve months sum to exactly 100%.

Do NOT add a recency weight, a decay factor, or a "last N years" window. If the
business ever wants one, that is a deliberate decision at the time, not a
default sitting in code.

============================================================
3 - WHEN IT REBUILDS
============================================================
Automatically, on the first rebuild after 7 April, matching the existing freeze
rule. A year that has just frozen is complete and safe to calibrate on.

Only FROZEN years enter the calculation. A year still open or partially loaded
must never contribute.

FY2023-24 has no day-level dates and no invoice numbers - month-only, 137,619
rows. That is fine here: this needs monthly totals only. Include it.

Log every rebuild: which years, the resulting weights, and the delta against
the previous version.

============================================================
4 - SHOW WHICH VERSION A PROJECTION USED
============================================================
Every figure derived from the curve states its basis. On the Momentum run-rate
card, one line:

    "Seasonal curve v2 - built from FY2023-24 to FY2025-26"

If a projection changes because the curve was rebuilt rather than because sales
moved, the reader must be able to see that.

============================================================
5 - MAKE THE VARIANCE VISIBLE
============================================================
Store the standard deviation of each month's share across the source years.

Where a month varies by more than 3 points between years, that month is
unstable and any projection resting heavily on it should say so. One line is
enough:

    "Q4 share has varied 31-35% across three years."

Do not build a confidence interval or a range forecast. Just make the spread
readable.

============================================================
DO NOT
============================================================
- Do not change any frozen year's data. This READS them; it never writes.
- Do not rebuild from an open or partially loaded year.
- Do not apply the curve to institutional business - territory and retail only,
  as today.
- Do not delete or overwrite a prior curve version.
- Do not add recency weighting.

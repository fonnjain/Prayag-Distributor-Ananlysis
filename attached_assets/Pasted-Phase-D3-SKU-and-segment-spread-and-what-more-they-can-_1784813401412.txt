Phase D3 - SKU and segment spread, and what more they can sell
PROMPT - Phase D3
Prayag app - Distributor Deep Dive, Phase D3 of 4. Same global rules.

Goal: SKU and segment spread per distributor, and a ranked list of what they
could sell but do not.

Closed years (FY2021-22 to FY2025-26), from secondary_register_line grouped by its
Distributor column:
- distinct item codes (Cat. No.), segment coverage out of the FULL Prayag segment
  set (state the denominator on screen), NET (Sub Total) by segment, top SKUs by
  NET, segment concentration, and average distinct segments per retailer as a
  cross-sell depth measure.

Whitespace - rank by ease of conversion, easiest first, and name the evidence
behind every suggestion:
  1. Range depth - item codes the distributor does NOT sell inside segments it
     ALREADY sells. Easiest, because the shelf and the relationship exist.
  2. Lost SKUs - item codes bought last year and absent this year.
  3. Peer whitespace - segments and SKUs that comparable distributors sell and
     this one does not. Comparable = same state, similar NET band. Name the peer
     distributors behind each suggestion so it is empirical, not aspirational.

Live year (FY2026-27): no item-code data exists, because there is no FY2026-27
secondary register. Show the SKU and segment panels as 'populates when a FY2026-27
register exists' - not as zeros. Do NOT substitute the dealer 'Assigned Segment'
attribute for actual sales by segment: it records what a dealer is assigned to,
not what was bought.

Acceptance:
  for a closed year, a distributor's segment coverage and NET by segment render
    and foot to that distributor's register NET total, using Sub Total
  segment coverage shows both numerator and denominator (for example 7 of 15)
  every whitespace suggestion names its evidence - the segment already sold, the
    prior-year purchase, or the specific peer distributors
  the live year shows the unavailable state, never zeros

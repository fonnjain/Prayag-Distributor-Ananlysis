## Section A — Sales People shows unavailable as 0.00%

On the Sales People table, several members show **Sales as an em dash** and
**Sales/Plan% as 0.00%** on the same row.

| Name | Plan | OB | Sales | Sales/Plan% |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| M. Raman | ₹12.30 L | ₹2.99 L | — | **0.00%** |
| Manish Kumar Trivedi | ₹9.35 L | ₹3.45 L | — | **0.00%** |
| Aditya Dasgupta | ₹8.48 L | ₹1.87 L | — | **0.00%** |

If Sales is unavailable, the ratio built on it is **unavailable, not zero**. A
member with no sales data and a member with genuinely zero sales look identical,
and the second is a performance problem while the first is a data gap.

```
Code change. Display only.

A1. Print the query behind the Sales column. Is the em dash "no value
    returned" or "value is null"?

A2. Where Sales is unavailable, Sales/Plan% must be unavailable too -
    blank-grey with a reason, never 0.00%.

A3. Where Sales is genuinely zero and Plan is non-zero, 0.00% is correct
    and should show.

A4. How many of the 229 members are in each state - sales available, sales
    unavailable, sales genuinely zero? If most of the dashes are
    unavailable, the headline Sales figure of Rs 96.63 Cr covers fewer
    members than the table implies, and the card should say so.

A5. The cards show 139 members with plan out of 229. Does the Sales card
    cover all 229 or only the 139? State the basis on the card.
```

## Common rules for all four exports

```
Already established elsewhere; apply them here.

  - Indian number format: >= 1 Cr as Rs x.xx Cr, >= 1 L as Rs x.xx L,
    below that grouped with no decimals
  - percentages as percentages, with numerator and denominator shown
  - THREE STATES on every figure: value, blank-grey-with-reason, genuine
    zero. Never a zero standing in for unknown
  - order booking never labelled sales
  - every export honours the page's active filters exactly
  - an Info sheet on every one: scope, filters, sources, coverage,
    provisional months, data-read timestamp
  - freeze panes, sensible widths, no sheet wider than it needs

AFTER EACH, print the sheet list with row counts and the Info sheet as
generated.
```

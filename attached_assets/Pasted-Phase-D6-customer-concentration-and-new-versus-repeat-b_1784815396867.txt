Phase D6 - customer concentration and new versus repeat business
PROMPT - Phase D6
Prayag app - Distributor Deep Dive, Phase D6. Same global rules. Depends on D1
only. Do not change any other phase.

Goal: answer two questions - where is the business actually coming from, and is
the salesperson generating new business or recycling the same customers.

1. Top customers. For the selected salesperson, distributor or state head, show
   the top 5 and top 10 customers by order booking with the amount, the percentage
   of the total, the running cumulative percentage, the visits each received, and
   the channel each sits in (distributor name, or direct dealer). Flag it when a
   large share of the top-line runs outside the distributor channel.

2. Classify every customer on last year versus this year. Four states, and keep
   them distinct:
     RETAINED    - bought last year AND this year
     REACTIVATED - nothing last year, bought this year (a revived dormant account)
     AT RISK     - bought last year, nothing yet this year
     NEVER       - no business in either year
   Show for each: retailer count, order booking this year, order booking last
   year, visits received, and business per visit.

3. Do NOT call the AT RISK group 'lost'. The live year is partial - it runs only
   to the data cutoff established in Phase 3-C. An account that has not ordered in
   three months may still order. Label the group with the cutoff date and the
   months elapsed, and say what it is: not yet converted this year.

4. Keep two different definitions of 'new' apart, because they measure different
   things and the dashboard already carries the first:
     NEW RETAILER  - newly onboarded onto the roster (dashboard 'New Retailers',
                     and 'New Party Order Booking' for its value)
     REACTIVATED   - an existing retailer that was dormant last year and has
                     bought this year
   Report both. A salesperson can score zero on one and well on the other.

5. Effort versus return. Show the share of visits and the share of business for
   each of the four states side by side, plus business per visit for each. The
   comparison is the finding: it shows whether visit effort is going where the
   money is.

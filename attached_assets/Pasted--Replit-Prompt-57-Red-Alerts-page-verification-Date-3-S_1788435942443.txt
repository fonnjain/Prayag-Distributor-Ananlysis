## Notes

- **Section B first when it comes back.** A badge reading "OPEN 99 PERIODS"
  against a two-month window is either wrong or means something entirely
  different from what it says. Reformatting the page around it would make a
  possible defect look intentional.
- **C2 is the one that could invalidate the alerts.** If Cumulative Target is a
  full-year figure and Cumulative OB is two months, then every achievement
  percentage on this page is comparing a partial numerator against a whole
  denominator — and 25.78% would be arithmetically correct and completely
  misleading.
- **D1 is worth thinking about rather than dismissing.** Both visible members have
  roughly doubled their prior achievement. An alert firing on someone improving
  fast may be technically right and practically wrong, and that is a threshold
  question rather than a bug.
- The formatting change is straightforward and can follow immediately. It is
  worth doing second so it does not lock in the appearance of something that
  turns out to be broken.

# Replit Prompt 39 — Excluding Mohan Impex from analysis

**Date:** 1 September 2026
**Scope:** Section A is read-only and must come back first. Section B is
implementation, and only after the scope decisions in A are settled.

**Standing rule:** every figure states which database or source it came from.

## The decision

**Mohan Impex is to be excluded from all analysis in the app. The data stays
stored; it just stops feeding any analytical figure.**

That is a business decision, already taken. This prompt exists to scope it
correctly before anything is built, because "excluded from analysis" can mean
three different things and the wrong reading would break a frozen year.

## Why it matters

Mohan Impex holds **₹35,73,37,364.63 across 1,210 rows** in FY2025-26, all with
`state_canon`, `head_canon` and `state_raw` NULL, spread across all twelve
months. That is **74.5% of the year's unattributed value** and **9.9% of the
entire FY2025-26 register**.

Excluding them changes the analytical baseline:

| | With Mohan Impex | Without |
|---|---:|---:|
| FY2025-26 rows | 145,613 | **144,403** |
| FY2025-26 value | ₹360.9954 Cr | **₹325.26 Cr** |
| Unclassified value | ₹47.98 Cr | **₹12.25 Cr** |
| Unclassified share | 13.3% | **3.8%** |

## Notes

- A4 and requirement 4 are the same point. Excluding a customer from one year of
  a comparison and not the other would manufacture a movement, which is precisely
  the artefact this decision is meant to remove.
- A5 is the one that could bite. If the anchor verification and the analytical
  aggregation run through the same query, excluding Mohan Impex would make a
  frozen year fail its own check. That has to be established before anything is
  built.
- Requirement 3 is the one I would not compromise on. A stated policy and a
  silent one look identical in the database and completely different to whoever
  reads the screen in three months.

## What this does not solve

**₹12.25 crore across 244 customers remains unclassified** in FY2025-26 — 3.8% of
the reduced baseline. Of those, 51 customers worth ₹4.18 crore have unique state
evidence in FY2026-27 history or `customer_master` and could be classified as a
derived reading. The other 194, worth ₹8.07 crore, have no evidence at all.

The state-comparison artefact shrinks from 13.3% to 3.8%. It does not disappear.

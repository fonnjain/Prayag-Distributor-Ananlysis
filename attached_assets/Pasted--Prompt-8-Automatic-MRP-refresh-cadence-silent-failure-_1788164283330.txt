# Prompt 8 — Automatic MRP refresh: cadence, silent failure, and exposure

**Date:** 31 August 2026
**Adds to:** Replit_Verification_Prompts_v2_2026-08-29.md, Prompt 7
**Scope:** Read-only. No code change, no cache refresh, no scheduler change, no load.

**Why this one is time-sensitive:** the MRP cache refreshes itself every 24 hours
in production. Nobody has to load anything. When Prayag publish the 01 September
prices to prayag-price.com, the app will pull them automatically, and because the
current-price lookup is not date-aware, Apr-Jul figures derived from MRP will
move without anyone acting or being told.

Send this before the September prices appear on the site, not after.

  fraction of a percentage point, this can wait. If it is several points across
  Rs 31 Cr, it cannot.
- Do not let anything in Section E be built off the back of this reply. The
  point of E is to know the size of the fix before the situation forces one.
- Separately, and not for Replit: Prayag should be told that codes 20, 25 and 32

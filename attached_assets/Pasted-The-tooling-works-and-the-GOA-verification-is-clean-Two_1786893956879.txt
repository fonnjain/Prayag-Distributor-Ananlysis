The tooling works and the GOA verification is clean. Two problems with the
suggestion rule.

1. 100% COVERAGE IS THE WARNING, NOT THE RESULT
Amey Deodhar covers ONE assigned customer in Goa. That makes him the majority
by default, and the rule suggested him for all three unassigned. That is not
evidence - it is the only name in an almost-empty set.

J. Kamal Kishore covering 70 in Andhra Pradesh is a real majority. Both appear
in the same format on the card, so an operator accepting in bulk cannot tell
them apart.

Add a CONFIDENCE BAND to every suggestion and show it prominently:
    strong    the suggested person covers >= 20 assigned customers in that
              territory AND >= 50% of them
    moderate  covers >= 5
    weak      covers < 5 - which is most of the long tail

Then:
  a. Print the count of the 3,376 in each band.
  b. "Accept all" must NOT be available on weak suggestions. Those need
     picking one at a time, because the rule has nothing behind them.
  c. Show the band on the card in words, not just a number. "covers 1 of 4 in
     this territory" reads differently from "covers 70".

2. THE RULE IGNORES THE DEPARTED PERSON
I asked for the replacement for THAT PERSON - who now holds the largest share
of the departed salesperson's former customers. You built territory majority,
which does not look at the departed person at all.

Those differ. If Shanker Singh's 741 customers are now split between two
people, whoever took most of HIS book is a better suggestion than whoever
happens to be largest in the state.

The source data carries the previous salesperson name on most of these rows.
Add it as rule 0, ahead of territory majority:
    rule 0  the active person who now holds the most of THIS departed
            person's former customers
    rule a  territory majority (existing)
    rule b  state head (existing)

Print the count that resolves under each rule, and 10 examples showing the
previous person, the suggestion, and which rule fired.

3. ALSO SHOW THE PREVIOUS ASSIGNEE ON THE CARD
"Previously: Shanker Singh (departed)" tells an operator why the row is blank
and lets them sanity-check the suggestion. Right now the card shows only the
suggestion with no history.

GOAL
Add an MRP Calculator to the MRP page. It works BACKWARDS from the price that
matters commercially - what the retailer pays - to the MRP that produces it.

Today MRP is an input and the net price is whatever falls out. That is the
wrong way round. MRP is a printed number; the net price is the commercial
reality. This calculator makes MRP the output.

THE CHAIN - two steps
  target retailer buying price
    / (1 - distributor margin)   -> distributor buying price
    / (1 - primary discount)     -> BACK-CALCULATED MRP

Both steps are user-editable with a stated default. Show the value at each
step, not just the final MRP.

What the retailer charges a walk-in customer is out of scope. Do not model it,
do not ask for it, do not display a consumer price anywhere.

THE PRIMARY DISCOUNT - confirmed source
Use the Discount column from the GP MARGIN workbooks (margin_fact.
discount_frac), matched per item_code, volume-weighted over the TRAILING 12
MONTHS.

  weighted_discount = SUM(discount_frac x qty) / SUM(qty)   over 12 months

That column is a FRACTION of MRP given away, not a percentage. Verified on
code 144: MRP 216.40, discount 0.5353, AVG SALE 100.55, and
216.40 x (1 - 0.5353) = 100.56. Confirm this identity holds on load for a
sample and report any code where it does not.

Show the register's realised MRP discount BESIDE it as a cross-check. If the
two differ by more than 5 points, flag it - the margin workbook is the plan,
the register is what happened, and a gap between them is a finding.

NO DISCOUNT DATA
Where a code has no margin_fact rows in the trailing 12 months, display
"no discount data" and require the user to enter one. Do NOT substitute a
segment average, a company average, or the last known value. A silently
inferred discount produces a confidently wrong MRP.

THE DISTRIBUTOR MARGIN
Default from the secondary register discount where the code has secondary
activity. User-editable. Store the SOURCE of the value alongside it -
assumed | survey | user - and display it. The Market Survey page will supply
better values for this later; building the source field now means that swap
needs no rework.

WHAT THE CALCULATOR SHOWS
For a chosen item code, side by side:
  current MRP (from mrp_history, with its effective date and segment)
  back-calculated MRP
  the difference, in rupees and percent
  the value at every step of the chain
  bom_cost and the resulting gross contribution at the NEW price
  the same at the CURRENT price, so the margin consequence is visible

If bom_cost is unavailable for that code, say so. Never show a contribution
figure computed from a missing cost.

BOM cost is FACTORY cost - no freight, overhead or SG&A. Label every derived
figure GROSS CONTRIBUTION, never "profit" or "margin" unqualified.

AMBIGUOUS CODES
56 codes exist in two segments with different products and different MRPs
(CNS-15 is a Concealed Stop Cock in PTMT at Rs 860 and a different item in CP
at Rs 1,020). The calculator must resolve on (item_code, segment), never on
code alone. If a code is ambiguous and no segment is given, refuse and ask -
do not pick one.

IT PROPOSES, A HUMAN APPROVES
The calculator NEVER writes to mrp_history. It produces a proposal. Writing a
new MRP requires an explicit separate action with a confirmation step showing
what changes and the margin impact. No auto-apply and no bulk apply in this
task.

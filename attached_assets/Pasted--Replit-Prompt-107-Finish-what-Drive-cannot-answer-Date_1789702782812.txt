```
Read-only.

399 distributor names exist; 130 have no code. We cannot create codes, but
we can propose matches.

C1. For each of the 130 uncoded names, search the coded population using
    UPPER(TRIM()) plus parenthetical stripping, then edit distance.

    The normalisation that worked for P008 and for normSecKey applies here:
    strip "(Off Roll)", "(JAMSHEDPUR)", "M/S", punctuation and case.

C2. Print proposed matches with supporting evidence - same state, same
    head, overlapping customers, similar order pattern.

C3. Print the residue: names with no plausible match, with their sales
    value.

That turns "create 130 codes" into "confirm these N matches and create the
rest".
```

## Notes

- **Section A is the priority.** It is the only open item with a measured
  commercial impact on live sales — ₹5.41 crore where the discount is being
  computed against the wrong price.
- **F2 is a correction that must land before anyone acts on P4.** The register
  currently holds three WCT-to-WT candidate mappings, and the monthly data shows
  they are different products. Acting on them would merge a live ₹1.15 crore line
  into another.
- **Section D carries the Q19 lesson forward.** Edit distance suggested WCT was a
  rename of WT and the sales pattern proved otherwise. The same discipline applies
  to 824XD.
- **Sections B and C are the biggest effort savings for Prayag** — they convert two
  open-ended requests into confirmation lists.

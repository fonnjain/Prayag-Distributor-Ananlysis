**Date:** 17 September 2026

**Standing rule:** every figure states which database or source it came from.

**Purpose.** 33 questions are open with Prayag, some since 31 August. Several are
answerable — or narrowable to a single decision — from data already in the

## Q1 is already answered. Here is how.

Recomputing PTMT cost from the **34,369 component rows** in the `ANUJ SIR BOM..`
tab of the June 2026 workbook, and comparing against each month's reported BOM
cost across 230 common codes:

| Reported month | Recomputed ÷ reported |
|---|---:|
| Dec-25 | 1.37× |
| **Jan-26** | **3.15×** |
| **Apr-26** | **2.77×** |
| **Jun-26** | **1.07×** |

**June agrees within 7%. January is out by more than three times.**

Prayag's own component bill supports the **higher** cost level. January to April
are understated; the surrounding months are not overstated.

That is the method to apply across the rest.

## Section A — questions answerable from held data

```
Read-only. For each, run the stated test and report: ANSWERED,
NARROWED, or NEEDS PRAYAG.

A1. Q1 - PTMT COST, JANUARY TO APRIL 2026

    Reproduce the test above and extend it:
      - recompute from ANUJ SIR BOM.. for every month Nov-25 to Jun-26
      - do the same from the OLD BOM, NEW BOM and NEW BOM APR 2026 tabs
      - which tab reproduces which months?

    If NEW BOM APR 2026 reproduces the low months and ANUJ SIR BOM..
    reproduces the normal ones, the cause is a BOM version switch, and the
    question to Prayag becomes "which version is current" rather than
    "what happened".

A2. Q2 - THE 64 PEA CODES, 1 FEBRUARY OR 10 AUGUST?

    The sales data knows. If the price rose on 1 February, sales from
    February onward realise against the new MRP; if on 10 August, they do
    not.

    For each of the 64 codes, compute realised price per piece by month
    from sale_line FY2025-26 and FY2026-27. Find the month the level
    steps.

    PEA-44B rises Rs 479 to Rs 1,528 - a 219% change should be visible in
    realised prices, not just in the master.

A3. Q19 - ARE WCT AND WT THE SAME PRODUCT?

    Three tests:
      - do WCT-3LL-* and WT-3LL-* sell to the SAME customers?
      - do they sell in NON-OVERLAPPING periods? A rename shows one
        stopping as the other starts
      - are their realised prices similar per piece?

    All three pointing the same way would settle it.

A4. Q20 - THE SIX AMBIGUOUS CODES

    824XD, 924XD, 824SD, HD140B, HD160B, HD200B.

    Same tests - customers, periods, realised price - against each
    candidate catalogue code. Rank the candidates by evidence rather than
    by edit distance.

    Do not auto-match. Produce a ranked recommendation Prayag can confirm
    in one line.

A5. Q13 - WHICH OF THE 58 NAMES ARE FORMER EMPLOYEES?

    A former employee has a last order date and nothing after it. Print
    for each of the 58: first and last order month, total value, and
    months since last activity.

    Anyone with no activity for six months is almost certainly gone.
    Anyone still ordering is not.

A6. Q30, Q31, Q32 - DISCONTINUED AND -VB CODES

    The eight PS codes, the 70 discontinued codes still selling, the 15
    -VB codes.

    For each: last sale date, quantity trend over FY2025-26 to date, and
    whether sales are falling or steady.

    A code with steady sales is not discontinued whatever the master says.
    A code with no sales for eight months effectively is.

A7. Q25 - RAVI UPADHYAY AND SHIV KUMAR

    No plan, no order booking, no sales for any month of 2026-27. Check
    FY2025-26 - did they ever have activity? If they did and it stopped,
    that is a leaver question, not a plan question.

A8. Q27 - SEPTEMBER SAP, 604 AGAINST ~700

    Check whether the missing invoices have since arrived. If the gap has
    closed, the question is answered and can be resolved.
```

    That turns "create 130 codes" into "confirm these 40 matches and

    classes with counts is a decision.

B4. Q17 - COLOUR PRICES ON 123 PRODUCTS

## Notes

- **A1 is already half-done and is the most valuable.** If a specific BOM tab
  reproduces the low months, the question changes from "what happened" to "which
  version is current" — and that is answerable in one line rather than an
  investigation.
- **A2 is the neatest test on the list.** A 219% price rise is unmissable in
  realised prices. The sales data knows the effective date even if the master file
  disagrees with its own note.
- **B1 is where the most effort is saved.** Turning "create 130 codes" into
  "confirm 40 matches, create 90" halves the work and makes it something someone
  can finish.
- **B3 matters because 870 is an unanswerable number.** Four classes with counts
  and values is a decision; a list is a task nobody starts.
- **Section C is the point of the whole exercise** — a short list where Prayag's
  answer is genuinely required, each a single decision.

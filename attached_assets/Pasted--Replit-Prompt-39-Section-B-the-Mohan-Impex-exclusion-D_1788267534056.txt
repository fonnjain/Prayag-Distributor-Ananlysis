## Two things I checked myself and corrected

**The scheme engine is NOT exposed.** I warned that the Nudge engine accepts
`is_territory IS NULL` and could therefore include Mohan in slab positions. It
does accept that clause — but `resolveSchemeForState` opens with
`if (!stateCanon) return null;` and the caller does `if (!schemeId) continue;`.
Mohan's NULL state drops him two steps later, at both call sites, lines 490 and
1028. **No scheme, slab, achievement or ROI figure moves.**

So this is purely a reporting change. Nothing commercial is affected.

**The Growth number moves ten points, in the direction that matters.** Including
Mohan flattered FY2025-26 by 1.16 points and now depresses FY2026-27 by 10.48
points. Same account, opposite distortions. That inconsistency is the reason to
exclude, not the improved figure.

## Notes

- Requirement 2 is the one that could break something. If the exclusion leaks
  into the anchor path, a frozen year starts failing verification and it will
  look like data loss rather than a filter.
- Requirement 5 is the one I would not compromise on. A total that quietly moves
  from ₹360.9954 Cr to ₹325.2616 Cr is indistinguishable from a bug. Showing both
  figures also protects whoever quoted 2.46% yesterday.
- Requirement 4's aside matters: two surfaces already disagree about Mohan by
  accident. Making the exclusion explicit resolves that as a side effect, and
  leaving it would mean the dashboard and the AI report keep giving different
  answers for reasons nobody chose.

## Separate from this change, and more important

**A ₹35.73 crore account went to zero and nobody has raised it.** Mohan Impex
appears in November 2024, grows from ₹30.36 Cr to ₹35.73 Cr, and stops in March
2026. That is the largest single commercial event in the data and the exclusion
would bury it.

Somebody should find out why — contract ended, moved to a competitor, one-off
project, or now booked under a different name. "Impex" suggests an export or
trading house, which would also confirm whether excluding him is the right
permanent policy or a temporary fix.

Worth adding to the Prayag pack as a plain question: **who is Mohan Impex, who
serviced them, and why did they stop in March 2026?**

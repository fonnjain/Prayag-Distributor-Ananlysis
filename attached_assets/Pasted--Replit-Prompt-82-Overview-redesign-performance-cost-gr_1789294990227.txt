# Replit Prompt 82 — Overview redesign: performance, cost, growth

**Date:** 13 September 2026
**Scope:** Section A read-only. Section B is the design. Section C is the build.

**Standing rule:** every figure states which database or source it came from.

---

## Remove

**Retailers**, **Channel Partners**, **Secondary Retail Reach**, **Sales &
Dispatch**, **Product Mix**. Those live better on Sales Deep Dive, Distributor
Deep Dive and SKU Deep Dive, where they can be explored.

## What the page is for

Three questions a management team asks every month:

**Are we performing?** · **What is it costing?** · **Where does more sales come
from?**

The page can be long. It should carry charts, not only cards.

---

## The story the current data already tells

This is what a good Overview would surface, and today's page does not.

**The year inflected in June.** Not "+7.2% YTD" — the shape underneath it:

| | FY26-27 | FY25-26 | Change |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Apr | ₹13.11 Cr | ₹15.06 Cr | **−13.0%** |
| May | ₹28.28 Cr | ₹29.62 Cr | −4.5% |
| Jun | ₹31.43 Cr | ₹29.87 Cr | +5.2% |
| Jul | ₹32.10 Cr | ₹26.34 Cr | **+21.9%** |
| Aug | ₹30.42 Cr | ₹25.36 Cr | **+20.0%** |

**Apr–May −7.4%. Jun–Aug +15.2%.** A single YTD percentage hides a turnaround.

**Gross margin varies by a factor of nearly two across categories** — PTMT 76.2%,
CISTERN 48.7%, C P 40.0%. Revenue mix is therefore a margin decision, and nothing
on the Overview shows it.

**Revenue is highly concentrated** — the top 100 SKUs are 45.9% of revenue, 560
codes reach 80%.

**Order booking leads dispatch**, and is up 14.0% on a like-for-like Apr–Jul
basis. It is the forward indicator and it is not on the page.

**And the month is heavily back-loaded** — 30% of a month's orders by day 19, 44%
in the last six days. Any mid-month read of the current month is not a trend.

## Section B — the proposed page

```
Design for comment. Build nothing yet.

BLOCK 1 - HEADLINE, four cards only

  Sales YTD          value, vs LY, growth %
  Order booking YTD  value, vs LY, growth %   (the forward indicator)
  Achievement        actual vs target to date, both sides shown
  Gross margin       weighted %, and the range across categories

  Every card shows its comparison inline. No card without a comparison.

BLOCK 2 - THE TREND. One chart, the most important on the page.

  Monthly sale LY vs TY, bars or lines, twelve months, growth % labelled.
  Order booking overlaid as a leading line.

  Beneath it, one sentence stating the shape - currently: "April and May
  were behind last year; June to August ran 15.2% ahead."

  Provisional months marked.

BLOCK 3 - WHERE GROWTH IS COMING FROM

  By state head: LY, TY, difference, growth %, share of company growth
  By state: same
  By category: same, six masters

  Sorted by CONTRIBUTION TO GROWTH, not by size. A large flat state matters
  less than a small state growing fast.

BLOCK 4 - MARGIN AND MIX

  Category mix by value, with gross margin % beside each.
  A chart showing revenue share against margin, so a shift toward a
  low-margin category is visible.

  This is the block that does not exist today and should. Margin varies
  nearly twofold across categories.

  Where margin data is missing - July onward, and PTMT Jan-Apr - say so on
  the block rather than omitting the category.

BLOCK 5 - COST AND PRODUCTIVITY

  Company cost ratio: total CTC + T.A. against sales, with both sides shown
  and the denominator type named
  Cost per order, sales per rupee of cost
  By head: cost ratio, with the company figure as a reference line
  Active members, orders, avg orders per member

BLOCK 6 - CONCENTRATION AND RISK

  Cumulative revenue by SKU rank - the Pareto curve
  Top 10 SKUs with share
  Revenue at risk: codes with no MRP (Rs 9.64 Cr), discontinued codes still
  selling, dormant retailer value

BLOCK 7 - REALISATION

  Weighted discount off MRP, company and by category
  Movement versus last year if computable
  Flag categories realising worse than last year - that is margin leaking
  through discount rather than cost

BLOCK 8 - COVERAGE ROLL-UP

  Company level only: retailers visited vs not, parties giving business,
  new parties, dormant count and value
  Not a list. A number with a trend and a link into Sales Deep Dive.

RULES FOR EVERY BLOCK
  - every percentage shows numerator and denominator
  - every figure names its source
  - provisional months marked, per the open-window rule
  - three states: value, unavailable-with-reason, genuine zero
  - order booking never labelled sales
  - six master categories throughout
  - the intra-month curve means a partial current month is NOT a trend -
    state that wherever the current month appears

Comment on this before building. Say which blocks are cheap, which need
work, and which cannot be served yet.
```

## Notes

- **Block 2 is the single most valuable change.** A YTD percentage of +7.2% tells a
  management team almost nothing. The monthly shape tells them the year turned in
  June, and that is actionable.
- **Block 4 does not exist anywhere today.** Margin ranges from 40% to 76% across
  categories, so mix shift moves profit more than volume does — and nothing on the
  Overview shows it.
- **Block 3 sorted by contribution to growth, not size**, is the difference between
  a report and a decision aid.
- **The removed cards are not lost.** Retailers, channel partners and retail reach
  belong on the deep dives where they can be drilled. The Overview should carry
  one roll-up number and a link.

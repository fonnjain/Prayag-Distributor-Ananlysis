GOAL
A new Market Survey page. A Prayag salesperson, on a visit, records what a
retailer is currently buying from a competitor and at what price.

This is the missing input. The competition data we hold is 150 codes, one
brand, one snapshot from March 2026, and its "net price" is a guess - a flat
40% discount applied to MRP. The survey supplies the real number.

WHO FILLS IT IN
A Prayag salesperson or pre-sales member, during a visit. Not the retailer.
The respondent is the person being asked about, not the person typing.

THE FORM
1. RESPONDENT
   - Existing Prayag buyer, or new prospect (dropdown, required)
   - If existing: pick from the customer master, searchable. Store the
     customer id, not a typed name.
   - If new: free text name, plus state and district from the same state
     picker used elsewhere. Do NOT create a customer master record from a
     survey - flag it for review instead.
   - Who recorded it: the logged-in member, captured automatically.

2. WHAT THEY BUY
   - Segment (dropdown, canonical segments)
   - Product (dropdown, filtered to that segment, from mrp_master)
   - Competitor brand: FREE TEXT with autocomplete against brands already
     recorded. Do NOT restrict to a fixed list - we currently know of one
     competitor and the field will find more. Normalise on save (trim, case)
     and show a count of how many surveys already name that brand.
   - Competitor's product name or code: free text, optional.

3. THE PRICE - two ways in, one stored value
   Either:
     (a) enter the NET PRICE the retailer pays, or
     (b) enter MRP and DISCOUNT %, and the net computes
   Show the computed value live in both directions. Store net_price as the
   canonical figure, and store mrp and discount_pct as entered when they were
   the entry route, so the working is preserved.

   Net price means WHAT THE RETAILER PAYS. Not what the shop charges a
   walk-in customer - that is out of scope and must not be asked for
   anywhere on this form.

   Also capture: unit (piece, box, carton), and pack size if the competitor
   sells in a different pack.

4. CONTEXT (all optional, all useful)
   - Why they buy it there: price, availability, credit terms, relationship,
     scheme, quality (multi-select)
   - Approximate monthly volume
   - Free-text note

SCHEMA
  market_survey
    id · surveyed_at · recorded_by (member) · customer_id nullable ·
    prospect_name nullable · state · district ·
    segment · prayag_item_code nullable ·
    competitor_brand · competitor_product nullable ·
    net_price · mrp nullable · discount_pct nullable · entry_mode ·
    unit · pack_size nullable ·
    reasons[] · monthly_volume nullable · note nullable ·
    created_at

Index on (segment, competitor_brand), (prayag_item_code), (state, district).

WHAT THE PAGE SHOWS BACK
This is the part that makes people fill it in. A survey form with no output
gets abandoned.

  - Recent submissions by this member, editable for 24 hours then locked
  - Per Prayag item code: our MRP, the median surveyed competitor net price,
    the number of surveys behind it, and the spread
  - By competitor brand: how many surveys, which segments, price range
  - Coverage gaps: segments and states with fewer than 5 surveys

NEVER show a single survey as though it were a market price. Always show the
count. One response is an anecdote; twenty is evidence. Where n is below 5,
label it "indicative only".

DO NOT
- Do not create customer_master records from survey entries.
- Do not write to the competition app. This is read-only with respect to it.
- Do not feed survey prices into any MRP calculation yet. That comes later
  and needs its own review.
- Do not touch margin_fact, sale_line or anything scheme-related.

VERIFY - produced values, and a hash that resolves
1. Submit one survey each way - net price direct, and MRP plus discount.
   Print both stored rows showing net_price, mrp, discount_pct and entry_mode.
   Confirm the computed net matches the arithmetic.
2. Submit a survey naming a competitor brand not previously seen. Confirm it
   saves, and that the brand then appears in the autocomplete for the next
   entry.
3. Submit for an existing Prayag customer and for a new prospect. Confirm the
   first stores customer_id and the second does not, and that no
   customer_master row was created either way.
4. Print the per-item summary for one code with at least 2 surveys, showing
   our MRP, the median surveyed net, and n.
5. Confirm a code with n=1 renders as "indicative only".
6. Confirm customer_master, margin_fact and sale_line row counts are
   unchanged.
7. Commit hash, git cat-file -t, git merge-base --is-ancestor main with exit
   code.

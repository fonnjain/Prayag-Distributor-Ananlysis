## Notes

- Section B is the one that could change the answer rather than refine it. If
  Data-tab Column A is a member list rather than a head list, then the vote is
  not heads competing and Karnataka's attribution is not a tie-break gone wrong
  but a category error.
- Section C4 and C5 exist because we are about to ask Prayag to confirm 34
  existing DIST# codes rather than create new ones. That is a far easier request
  to get actioned, but only if the evidence holds. Check it before we send it.
- Section F is last on purpose and nothing in it should be built yet. The
  attribution change and the state-vocabulary work from Prompt 10 both touch
  head and state resolution, and merging them into one change would be
  impossible to localise.

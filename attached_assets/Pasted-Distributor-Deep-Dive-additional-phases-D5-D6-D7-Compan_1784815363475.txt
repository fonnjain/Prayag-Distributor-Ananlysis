Phase D5 - territory whitespace and channel overlap
PROMPT - Phase D5
Prayag app - Distributor Deep Dive, Phase D5. Same global rules. Depends on D1
only. Do not change any other phase.

Goal: show where Prayag has retailers but no supply route, and where channels
overlap. This is the highest-value output on the page.

1. Separate the two kinds of gap. They have different fixes and must not be
   merged into one 'uncovered' number:
     COVERAGE GAP   - the district has retailers but NO distributor at all.
                      Fix: appoint a distributor. Strategic, slow.
     ASSIGNMENT GAP - the district HAS a distributor operating, but retailers in
                      it carry no Assigned Distributor. Fix: assign them to the
                      existing distributor. Administrative, immediate.
   Report each district as: retailers, distributor-served, direct dealers,
   unassigned, prior-year order booking, current order booking, visits spent, and
   the distributors operating there.

2. Size the prize, do not just count rows. For every gap show the PRIOR-YEAR order
   booking of the affected retailers - that is proven demand with no route to
   market - and the visits already being spent on them. Rank gaps by prior-year
   value, not by retailer count.

3. Direct-dealer overlap. Flag direct dealers operating inside a district that has
   a distributor: that is structural channel conflict. Direct dealers in a district
   with NO distributor are NOT conflict - they are the only channel, and must be
   labelled differently. Never merge the two cases.

4. Tie it back to dormancy. For each unassigned retailer show visits received and
   business booked. A retailer with visits and no distributor cannot order; that is
   a supply-mapping failure, not a sales-effort failure, and the page should say so.

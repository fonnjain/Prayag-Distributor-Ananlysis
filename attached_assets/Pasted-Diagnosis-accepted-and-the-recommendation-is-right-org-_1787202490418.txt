1. THE SIX CASE-DUPLICATES ARE INERT, NOT SPLIT
All 22 people are on the uppercase side; zero on the title-case side. So

Delete them as part of the migration, not as a separate task. Confirm no
person_territory row references them first.

2. state_hierarchy HAS ITS OWN GAP
You report 37 rows covering all 33 register values, but the state filter work
found four NON-GEOGRAPHIC values in it: GEM, JJM, Non-territory / Project /
Govt, and HITESH. Those were deliberately kept in the hierarchy for arithmetic
but EXCLUDED from the picker.

If org territories point at state_hierarchy, those four must not become
assignable territories. A person cannot cover "GEM". Confirm they are excluded
from anything a person can be assigned to, and that HITESH - 374 register rows
that are a rep's name in a state field - is flagged rather than inherited.

3. MULTI-HEAD NEEDS A DATA MODEL, NOT JUST A NOTE
Eight territories have more than one head; WEST BENGAL has four. person_territory
links a person to a territory with NO head dimension, so the app cannot express
"Sandeep covers this part of WB and Rizvi covers that part".

Before building, say how you would model it:
  a. Is the register's split value the unit of coverage - so UP ( A ),
     UP (AS) and UTTAR PRADESH are three separate territories, each with one
     head?
  b. Or is a territory genuinely shared, needing a head dimension on
     person_territory?

Print, for each of the eight multi-head territories, the net per head. If each
head's business sits in a distinct register value, (a) is the answer and the
model stays simple. If two heads both trade under the SAME state_canon value,
(b) is required.

That distinction decides the schema. Do not build until it is answered.

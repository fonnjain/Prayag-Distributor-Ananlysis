# Prompt 75 Section B — Fix WIDEN and ACTIVATE

**Date:** 12 September 2026

**Scope:** One code change, two metrics, in `aiGrowthReport.ts`.

**Standing rule:** every figure states which database or source it came from.

## What Section A established

**The defect is not latent.** Across ten production distributors in FY2025-26,
the mixed value runs **18.6% higher** than the matched-population value. AI
narrative has been overstating the widen opportunity, consistently and in one
direction.

**The cause is precise.** A primary-sales customer median (**₹19.31 L**) is used
to monetise a secondary distributor segment gap, where the correct baseline is
the secondary distributor median (**₹16.28 L**). Different entity, different
population, different source.

**There is a second occurrence.** ACTIVATE monetises a secondary dormant-retailer
count with the same primary customer median. Same builder, same wrong median.

**And a period mismatch sits on top of it** at company and state scope — a
selected-period primary median against whole-FY segment breadth.

## Notes

- **Item 2 needs care rather than copying.** ACTIVATE counts dormant *retailers*,
  not distributors. The matched basis is a retailer-level secondary median, not
  the distributor median that fixes WIDEN. Using the distributor median there
  would swap one entity mismatch for another.
- **Item 3 is half the distortion at company scope.** Matching populations while
  leaving a selected-period numerator against whole-FY breadth would look fixed
  and remain wrong.
- **Item 5 matters because these are opportunity figures.** An estimate that could
  not be computed and was filled in reads exactly like one that was — and there is
  no way for a reader to tell.
- **Item 7 is worth confirming rather than assuming.** These are already
  half-to-full ranges on a 40% uptake assumption. The correction grounds them; it
  does not turn them into forecasts, and the narrative should not start reading as
  though it has.

## What changes for anyone reading the reports

Widen opportunities fall by roughly **18.6%** on the sample, and ACTIVATE will
move too. Anyone who has quoted a widen figure from an AI report has quoted an
overstated one.

Worth mentioning to whoever uses these reports, rather than letting the numbers
quietly drop.

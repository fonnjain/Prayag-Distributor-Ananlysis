## What this batch is really testing

**Every guard fires here.** Eleven zero-target members across five heads, two heads with no business at all, one member with 8 working days, one team with a single retailer. The large teams never produced these — they were all comfortably measurable.

**Sulinder Pal has three of five members with no target.** His 45.6% headline rests on two people. That is the like-for-like rule doing real work rather than being a footnote.

**Nasir Hussain Khan runs 46% direct dealer** — the highest of any head, against a company average near zero. If his territory genuinely runs on a parallel channel rather than a distributor network, that is worth knowing before anyone applies distributor-based recommendations to J&K.

**And J&K plus Himachal are the strongest test of the Q4 seasonality assumption.** Deep winter stops construction there, which is exactly when the rest of the country peaks. If any territory breaks the pattern, it is one of those two — and that would mean the push list needs per-territory banding company-wide.

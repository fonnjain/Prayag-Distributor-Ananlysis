Turn the Organisation and Customer pages into the editable master for people,
hierarchy, territories and customer assignment. Today both derive from Google
Sheets that are read-only, and most distributor State Heads read "Guessed"
with no value. After this, the app holds the truth and a person can change it.

Attached: Prayag_Master_Seed.xlsx. Nine tabs, each mapping to one table.
Import it ONCE. After that the app is the master.

BUILD IN THREE PHASES. Report verification after each and STOP. Do not begin
the next phase until I confirm. This is the largest change in the project and
it touches attribution.

EFFECTIVE DATING IS NOT OPTIONAL
Every assignment carries effective_from and effective_to. Reassigning a
customer must NEVER change historical attribution.

One customer reclassified between years already broke every year-on-year
territory comparison in this system - Rs 35.73 Cr appearing as a total
collapse. If users can reassign freely without effective dating, every
historical figure becomes unstable and no report can be trusted.

Reports read the assignment IN FORCE during the period being reported, not
today's.

CONFIDENCE LADDER
  confirmed          a human set it in the app          highest
  assign_user_chain  derived from the named salesperson  (what the seed uses)
  state_lookup       derived from the state alone
  guessed            fallback - what most rows show today

Editing a value promotes it to 'confirmed'. Editing IS the confidence upgrade.
Show the confidence on every row, as the Customer Data page already does.

PHASE 2 — THE ORGANISATION PAGE
============================================================

A tree view of the hierarchy, expandable, with an edit panel per person.

EDIT A PERSON
  Designation   dropdown from the designation table, PLUS "add new
                designation" which asks for a name and a rank
  Reports to    dropdown of any active person. Must reject a cycle.
  Territories   multi-select from the territory table, showing splits
                indented under their parent
  Active        toggle. Deactivating must ask what happens to their
                customers and their direct reports - see below.

ADD A PERSON
  Name, employee code, designation, reports to, territories.
  Employee code is OPTIONAL and must not be the identity key - 62 of 179
  active people have an implausible code (mobile numbers, "+" characters,
  20-digit values). Key on person_id.

INSERT A MANAGER ABOVE AN EXISTING TEAM
  Explicit action, not a series of edits. Pick the people who will report to
  the new person, and who the new person reports to. The system re-points the
  selected reports in one transaction.

MOVE A PERSON TO A DIFFERENT HEAD
  Changes the rollup for that person AND everyone below them.
  MANDATORY IMPACT PREVIEW before saving:
      "Moving Shanker Singh from Pawan Kumar Sharma to Anant Singh.
       Affects 3 people below them and 741 customers.
       Historical attribution before <date> is unchanged."
  Nothing saves until the preview is confirmed.

DEACTIVATING A PERSON
  Refuse to leave customers orphaned. Ask, in one flow:
      their direct reports -> reassign to whom
      their customers      -> reassign to whom
  A person may be deactivated with customers still attached only if the user
  explicitly chooses to leave them - and they then appear in the reassignment
  queue.

DIVERGENCE FROM THE SHEET
  The HR sheet keeps updating. Where the app value differs from the latest
  sheet value, flag it visibly - the page already says "App overlay - Google
  Drive is read-only; divergences are flagged". Keep that. The app value wins;
  the sheet value stays visible beside it.

PHASE 3 — THE CUSTOMER PAGE
============================================================

A hierarchy view: State -> Distributor -> the secondary customers beneath.

  State (splits shown separately, parent selectable)
    State Head
      Distributor  (and Direct Dealer)
        Retailers / sub-dealers linked to that distributor

RETAILERS LINK TO MORE THAN ONE DISTRIBUTOR
Over a third do. A strict tree cannot represent that. Show a retailer under
EVERY distributor they link to, with a marker showing they have others, and
the count. Do not pick one and hide the rest - the current app already loses
half the assignments that way.

EDIT A CUSTOMER
  State Head          dropdown, promotes confidence to 'confirmed'
  Salesperson         dropdown of active people
  Type                distributor / direct_dealer / retailer / sub_dealer /
                      project / govt / other
  Territory           from the territory table, splits selectable
  Distributor link    for a retailer: add or remove links, many allowed

MOVE A CUSTOMER
  Same mandatory impact preview as moving a person:
      "Moving DIST#61201 from Pawan Kumar Sharma to Anant Singh.
       FY2026-27 value to date Rs X. Historical attribution before <date>
       is unchanged. Year-on-year comparisons spanning this date will show a
       basis break and will be flagged."
  That last sentence is not decoration. A reassignment IS a basis break and
  comparison surfaces must know.

ADD A CUSTOMER
  Goes to the existing Review Queue as pending, not straight into the master.
  Approving there creates the customer record. Keep that gate.

BULK REASSIGNMENT - build this, it is the first real job
404 departed salespeople still hold customers: 3,290 active retailers and
3,167 distributors and dealers. Reassigning those one at a time is unusable.

  Select a departed person -> see all their customers -> select all or some
  -> reassign to a named active person -> single impact preview -> one
  transaction.

Seed tab "Reassignment queue" lists all 404 ranked by urgency.

TYPE CHANGES ARE THE DANGEROUS ONE
Changing a customer's type - distributor to project, say - is exactly what
broke year-on-year comparison before. Require a reason on that edit
specifically, record it in change_log, and effective-date it so history holds.

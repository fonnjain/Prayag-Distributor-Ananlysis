Fix the duplicate accumulation in sale_line. Read this whole thing before
starting - it has an order, and step 1 must land before step 2 is useful.

THE PROBLEM, RESTATED

line_uid hashes the amount. ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING only ever inserts. So when
someone edits a rate in the sheet - as happened on 18 and 20 July, rounding
490.92 to 491 - the row hashes differently, gets inserted as new, and the old
version stays forever. The DB reports the sum of every version.

This is not a July problem. Every month carries it: Apr 75, May 169, Jun 214,
Jul 592. It is structural and it will keep growing.

The sheet is already correct. Nothing needs recovering. The DB holds too much,
not too little.

STEP 1 - STORE COLOUR

SALE SHEET 26-27 has a Color column at position 9, zero-indexed 8. It is read
and discarded at mapping. Add it to sale_line and populate it on every load.

This must come first. Without colour, white and ivory are indistinguishable -
the documented case is invoice 12600285, item 188, qty 88, Rs.25,867 as serial
1802 WHITE and 1803 IVORY, two real products. That is the whole reason
serial_no was pressed into the key, and serial_no is generated in the sheet and
changes whenever anyone sorts it. Storing colour is what lets the key become
stable.

Backfill colour for existing rows where it can be matched from the sheet.
Report how many rows get a colour and how many cannot.

STEP 2 - VERSIONED UPSERT

Split identity from mutable attributes.

  IDENTITY - stable, survives edits:
      fy, invoice_no, item_code, color, qty
      This is the new line_uid.

  MUTABLE - can change when someone edits the sheet:
      rate, amount, serial_no, and any derived field

  STATE - new columns:
      version_status   'current' or 'superseded'
      superseded_at    timestamp, null while current
      superseded_by    the id of the row that replaced it

On sync, for each sheet row:
  - no matching identity          -> insert as current
  - identity matches, values same -> touch sheet_confirmed_at, nothing else
  - identity matches, values differ -> mark the existing row superseded with a
    timestamp, insert the new values as current

NOTHING IS EVER DELETED. The rate-rounding episode becomes visible history
instead of silent inflation.

EVERY reported figure filters to version_status = 'current'. Audit views may
show superseded rows; no total may include them.

STEP 3 - RECONCILE WHAT IS ALREADY THERE

One-off pass over existing rows. Group by the new identity. Within each group,
the row with the latest ingested_at becomes current; the rest are marked
superseded with superseded_at set to the timestamp of the row that replaced
them.

Do not delete anything. Report per month: rows examined, marked superseded,
remaining current, and the total value before and after.

The July total should land near the sheet's Rs.6,28,33,045 across 626 invoices.
Apr, May and Jun should barely move - their duplicate counts are 75, 169 and
214.

STEP 4 - PROVE IT

  a) Per month for FY2026-27: current-row count and total, against the live
     sheet's row count and total. They should now agree.
  b) Q1 must still be Rs.73.09 Cr. IMPORTANT: Q1 currently carries 458
     duplicate rows AND matches its anchor exactly. Those two facts are hard to
     hold together. Either the anchor was computed from equally inflated data,
     or those 458 are something other than superseded edits. Report what Q1
     becomes after the pass and whether the anchor still holds. If Q1 MOVES,
     say so plainly - do not adjust anything to preserve it.
  c) Idempotency: re-run the sync twice with no sheet change. Zero inserts,
     zero supersessions on the second run.
  d) Edit simulation: take one row, change its rate, re-sync. Confirm exactly
     one supersession and one insert, and that the current total moves by the
     difference, not by the whole row.

STEP 5 - THE STANDING CHECK

SAP is a separate system carrying the same invoices and the same money, which
makes it a genuine external auditor rather than an internal cross-foot.

Add a reconciliation comparing SUM(amount) of current rows per invoice against
SAP's Taxable Amount per Document Number, for the SAP Combined tab
(19Oj6P2cSZmXNGfDro9K_ubfrfPp_03K14vOTrq_gdyI).

Caveat: the Drive copy of SAP is a periodic export and lags - last modified
18 July against the sheet's 20 July. Rows newer than the last export will
legitimately have no SAP counterpart. Report those separately by date rather
than as failures.

Also flag any invoice where sheet and SAP disagree on value. The rate rounding
means the sheet may now differ from what SAP actually billed. Rs.490.92
becoming Rs.491 is one paisa, but across 626 invoices it accumulates, and it is
worth knowing whether that rounding was a tidy-up or a correction.

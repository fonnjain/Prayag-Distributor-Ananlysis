# Replit Prompt 96 — Pending Orders: drill through team member

**Date:** 15 September 2026
**Scope:** A is read-only and decides feasibility. B is the change.

**Standing rule:** every figure states which database or source it came from.

---

## What is wanted

Pending Orders currently drills **State Head → Party**. Sandeep Ji expands
directly into 45 parties.

It should drill **State Head → Team Member → Party**.

## The thing that will break it

The page says the source is the *"Factory pending sheet — balance quantity by
state head and party."* So the sheet carries **head and party, not member**.
Member has to be derived from the customer-to-member assignment.

And that assignment is incomplete. The Customers page shows **3,376 unassigned**
customers, and there is an Attribution Conflicts page in the sidebar — so
multi-member and disputed assignments exist too.

**If any of Sandeep's 45 parties do not map to a member, the member rows will not
sum to 1,68,445 pieces**, and the head total will appear to be wrong when the
attribution is what is missing.

## Section B — the change

```
Code change. Pending Orders page. Show the diff before applying.

Add a TEAM MEMBER level between state head and party.

  State Head  ->  Team Member  ->  Party  ->  (existing product group
                                                columns)

1. MEMBER ROWS MUST RECONCILE TO THE HEAD TOTAL.

   Sum of member rows plus the unassigned bucket equals the head total,
   exactly. Print the reconciliation on screen the way Company Reports
   does - parent total, sum of children, difference.

2. UNASSIGNED IS AN EXPLICIT ROW, never a silent omission.

   Label it plainly - "Not assigned to a member" - with its party count and
   piece quantity. It is not zero and it is not hidden.

   If it is large, that is a finding worth seeing, not a defect to smooth
   over.

3. DISPUTED ATTRIBUTION gets its own treatment.

   A party mapping to more than one member is shown once, in a
   "Disputed attribution" row, NOT counted under either member and NOT
   silently assigned to the first.

   Link it to the Attribution Conflicts page.

4. DEPARTED MEMBERS still show.

   A member marked LEFT with pending stock keeps their row, marked LEFT.
   Pending stock against a departed member is an ownership gap and hiding
   it does not close it.

5. THE BREADCRUMB follows the Company Reports pattern already built -
   clicking a row expands in place or opens the next level with a visible
   path back. Use the same behaviour; do not invent a second one.

6. COLLAPSED BY DEFAULT at member level, as the head level is now. A head
   with 45 parties across 12 members should not open into 45 rows.

7. THE EXPORT follows the same hierarchy - head, member, party - with the
   unassigned and disputed rows included and labelled.

DO NOT CHANGE any figure. The head totals, the balance quantity of 2,98,679
pieces, the 147 parties and the cross-check block must all be identical
before and after.

AFTER APPLYING, print:
  - Sandeep Dadheech expanded to member level, with the reconciliation
  - the unassigned and disputed rows with their quantities
  - the company totals before and after, which must match
```

## Notes

- **A3 is the check that decides this.** If a meaningful share of Sandeep's 45
  parties do not map to a member, the drill will look broken when the attribution
  is what is missing. Better to know the number first.
- **Requirement 2 is the same principle applied everywhere else in this app.** An
  unassigned bucket stated plainly is information. Parties silently dropped from a
  member view is a total that does not add up.
- **Requirement 3 matters because of the winner-take-all problem** found earlier in
  distributor attribution — a state's distributors going entirely to whichever
  head had most rows, decided in one case by a margin of six. A disputed party
  should be visible as disputed, not resolved by a tiebreak nobody sees.
- **The page is already careful** — it states that water tanks are in pieces not
  litres in this source, and the cross-check block explains that derived pending
  and factory pending are independent measures in different units that should not
  be added. Keep both.

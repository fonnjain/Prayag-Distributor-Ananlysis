# Apply the FY2025-26 state attribution

*The validation is what makes this safe. One check before applying, and one thing deliberately left undone.*

---

## Why this is approved

**376 customers compared, ₹300.02 Cr of derived attribution, zero state-vs-state disagreements.**

That closes the concern that FY2025-26 state was inferred rather than sourced. Every state figure in the frozen year now has a source behind it — West Bengal at ₹76.64 Cr included.

## What moves

| | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| No state at all | ₹47.98 Cr | **₹3.93 Cr** |
| Non-territory / Project / Govt | ₹15.21 Cr | ~₹51.7 Cr |
| Named states | ₹313.02 Cr | ~₹320.6 Cr |
| **Total** | **₹361.00 Cr** | **₹361.00 Cr** |

**The anchor does not move. Only the labels do.** That is the safest possible change to a frozen year.

## The principle worth keeping

**We just replaced inference with a source, and the source agreed with the inference on 376 of 376 customers.** That is a good outcome for the backfill as well as for the files.

The temptation now is to close the last 1.1% with a name-parsing rule. **Don't.** An honestly unattributed ₹3.93 Cr is worth more than a tidy total built on `(UP)` in a customer name — and the business can resolve most of it in one conversation.

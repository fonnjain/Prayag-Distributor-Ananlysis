## Notes

- **Section D is the reason for the audit.** Building a fourth page that recomputes
  peer penetration differently from SKU Deep Dive would create two answers to one
  question, and this project has spent weeks removing exactly that.
- **E6 is the one I would check first.** The Resolution register exists and enforces
  holds at the API layer. If the deep dives predate it and read `margin_fact`
  directly, they may be showing held PTMT months.
- **A2 has been open since the first Deep Dive review** and is a small, visible
  arithmetic failure — 46 + 18 = 64 against a stated 59. It undermines confidence
  in everything beside it.
- **B is the thinnest section because Distributor Deep Dive has had the least
  scrutiny.** That is itself worth knowing.

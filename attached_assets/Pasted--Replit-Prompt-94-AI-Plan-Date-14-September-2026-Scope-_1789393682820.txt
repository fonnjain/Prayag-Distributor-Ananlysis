# Replit Prompt 94 — AI Plan

**Date:** 14 September 2026
**Scope:** A is a defect. B is persistence. C is the page. D is completion
tracking. E records the source-authority finding.

**Standing rule:** every figure states which database or source it came from.

**A new page: `/ai-plan`.** The third of the AI family — `/ai-targets` proposes
targets, `/ai-schemes` proposes schemes, `/ai-plan` proposes where to go.

---

## What already exists, and it is more than I credited

`visitPlan.ts` is a real planner, not a pro-rating. It anchors monthly capacity
on **last closed FY actual visits**, splits it into maintenance and development
budgets, and selects named retailer targets by:

```
priority = businessPlan / max(distanceKm, 5)
```

**Business potential over distance.** That is one term away from the breadth
logic in `/ai-schemes`, and it already ranks dormant retailers by what they are
worth against what they cost to reach.

Three target types, correctly differentiated — **Maintain** (active, top five by
order booking), **Develop** (inactive, assigned, untouched, ranked by
plan-over-distance and removed from later pools so nobody is scheduled twice),
**Reduce** (visited, inactive, zero order booking).

## What is missing, and it is one thing

**The plan is never persisted.** Computed in memory from connected sheets, cached
in process, never written to the database or back to Sheets.

So there is no plan ID, no approval, no planned-versus-completed comparison, and
no history. **The app can tell a member where to go and can never check whether
they went.**

That single gap is what blocks the feedback loop for visit planning *and* for
scheme proposals — because without it, nobody can measure whether a visit
produced an order.

## Section A — the planner coerces unavailable to zero

```
Code change. Fix before persisting anything, or the defect gets written to
the database.

From the analysis: "Missing retailer visit values are commonly coerced to
zero in VisitPlan sums, so unavailable and no recorded visit can converge
in calculated totals."

This is the empty-versus-unknown failure inside the planner, where it
decides who gets scheduled.

A1. Print every place VisitPlan coerces a missing value to zero -
    totalVisit, visitsRequired, businessPlan, distanceKm.

A2. Three states, as everywhere else:
      recorded value
      unavailable, with the reason
      genuine zero

    A retailer with no visit record and a retailer recorded as visited zero
    times must not converge. The first may need a visit; the second has
    been tried.

A3. THE DEFAULTS ARE DOING REAL WORK AND MUST BE VISIBLE.

      businessPlan defaults to Rs 50,000 when missing
      distanceKm defaults to 20 km when missing
      visitsRequired defaults to 12 when missing

    So priority = 50,000 / 20 = 2,500 for any retailer missing both. That
    is a manufactured rank, not a measured one.

    Print how many targets are currently selected on defaulted inputs. Mark
    them on the plan. A target ranked on two defaults is a guess wearing a
    score.

A4. Confirm no total that reads as a count is actually a sum of defaults.
```

## Section B — persist the plan

```
Code change. After A.

TABLE: visit_plan
  id, member, state_head, fy, month
  generated_at, generated_from - the source snapshot and its timestamp
  capacity, working_days, maintenance_budget, development_budget
  status: proposed | approved | superseded
  approved_by, approved_at
  pool_exhausted flag
  excluded_count and reason - retailers without a distributor

TABLE: visit_plan_target
  plan_id, retailer identity, district, distance_km
  priority_type: maintain | develop | reduce
  priority_score, and WHICH INPUTS WERE DEFAULTED
  business_plan, order_booking, visits_done
  reason - why this retailer was selected
  status: planned | visited | not_visited | superseded
  visited_on, order_value_after - filled by Section D

RULES

1. A PLAN IS A PROPOSAL until approved. Same rule as engine_targets and
   the scheme proposals. Generating is not committing.

2. REGENERATING SUPERSEDES, never overwrites. The previous plan stays,
   marked superseded, with its targets. Otherwise there is no way to ask
   what was planned last month.

3. RECORD THE SOURCE SNAPSHOT. Connected sheets change. A plan generated
   on Monday from a sheet edited on Tuesday must be traceable to what it
   read.

4. Do not write back to Google Sheets. Read-only there, as everywhere.
```

## Section D — completion tracking

```
Code change. After B and C. This is the piece that makes the rest worth
having.

D1. When a plan is approved, its targets are status planned.

D2. On the next sheet sync, match completed visits back to planned
    targets. A planned retailer whose totalVisit increased is visited.

D3. Record order_value_after - did the visit produce business?

    This is the feedback loop. Today nobody can answer "did the visits we
    planned produce orders" for any member, any month.

D4. Report, per member and per priority type:
      planned, visited, not visited
      visited-with-order, visited-no-order
      value produced per visit

    The Deep Dive already shows "VISITED - NO BUSINESS" as a count. This
    turns it into a plan-level measure.

D5. HONESTY REQUIREMENT. Matching by "totalVisit increased" is inference,
    not evidence. A member may have visited a retailer not on the plan, or
    the sheet may be updated late.

    Label it inferred. Do not present it as confirmed attendance.
```

## Section E — record the source-authority finding

```
Not code. Operating rules, and one register entry.

E1. RECORD IN THE OPERATING RULES, verbatim from the analysis:

    "There is no single visit authority. Dashboard Data owns aggregate
     headline visits, visited-retailer coverage and working days. Member
     sheets own retailer-level visits, required visits and VisitPlan
     analytics. HR/SFA owns only its separately labelled field-activity
     counters.

     Any published figure labelled only 'Visits' is ambiguous unless it
     also identifies aggregate visits, unique visited retailers, or
     retailer-row visits."

E2. RESOLVE P41 in the Resolution register - "which is authoritative for
    visits" - as ANSWERED, with the matrix from the analysis as its
    resolution note. It is not a conflict; it is three sources owning three
    things.

E3. ADD a PENDING entry:

    P45  VISIT FIGURES LABELLED ONLY "VISITS"
         reason  Aggregate visits, unique visited retailers and
                 retailer-row visits are three different measures shown
                 under one word in several places.
         action  Audit every surface showing a visit figure and label
                 which measure it is.
         owner   internal
         priority medium
```

## Notes

- **Section A before Section B, without exception.** Persisting a plan built on
  coerced defaults would write the defect into the database and make it look
  authoritative. A priority of 2,500 from a ₹50,000 default over a 20 km default
  is a manufactured rank.
- **B2 is what makes the page worth building.** Without supersession there is no
  way to ask what was planned last month, and therefore no way to learn.
- **Section D is the feedback loop that both other AI pages lack.** `/ai-targets`
  proposes and never learns; `/ai-schemes` will do the same. This is the first
  place the app could measure whether its own suggestion worked.
- **D5 matters because the inference is weak.** "totalVisit increased" is not
  attendance. Labelling it inferred is the difference between a measure and a
  claim.
- **Tab 3 is where the three AI pages meet.** A retailer with no distributor cannot
  be scheduled and cannot receive a scheme. Same population, two consequences, and
  currently counted in neither place.

THREE QUANTITIES, NOT TWO

For one tank line there are now three numbers:
  DB      500     per-tank litres        - the loader bug, wrong
  Sheet   9,500   total litres           - pieces x 500, COMPUTED in the sheet
  SAP     19      PIECES                 - the actual billed quantity

The sheet's 9,500 is not a source figure. It is SAP's 19 pieces run through the
WT-LTR lookup inside the sheet. SAP is the billing master. 19 is what was
invoiced.

THE QUESTION THAT DECIDES THE FIX

What is sale_line.qty SUPPOSED to hold for a tank - pieces or litres?

Every non-tank row holds PIECES. A tap is qty 1, not "litres of tap". If the
column is pieces, then the correct tank value is SAP's 19 pieces, and litres is
a DERIVED view for volume reporting only - adopting the sheet's 9,500 litres
would make qty mean something different for tanks than for everything else, a
mixed unit in one column. That mixed unit is exactly what caused the
litre-vs-piece confusion running through this whole project.

If the column is deliberately litres for tanks, then 9,500 is right and we adopt
it.

I am not going to guess this. Settle it against SAP before touching a row.

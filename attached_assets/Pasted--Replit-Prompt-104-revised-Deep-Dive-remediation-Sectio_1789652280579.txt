## Section C — coverage arithmetic

```
Code change.

Total Old Retailers and Non-Visited come from member and order-sheet
populations. Visited comes from HR/SFA visit evidence. They sit together
with no reconciliation rule, so 59 / 46 / 18 remains structurally possible.

C1. LABEL, DO NOT RECONCILE. Name the source on each card and state that
    the three are not additive.

    Forcing genuinely different populations into one number would hide a
    real measurement difference. Naming the sources is honest; making them
    add up would not be.

    what it measures. Use a name reflecting its actual source.

C3. Confirm no other card on this page sums across populations that do not
    reconcile.
```

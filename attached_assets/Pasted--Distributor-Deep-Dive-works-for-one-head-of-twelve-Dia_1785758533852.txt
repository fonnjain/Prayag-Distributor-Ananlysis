# Distributor Deep Dive — works for one head of twelve

*Diagnose before fixing. And one question matters more than the fix.*

---

## The message does not match the roster

> *"0 member sheets loaded, 2 not yet mapped"* — for **Biju C.O**, who has **12 members**.

If the page knew about 12 and had mapped none, it would say 12. **Saying 2 means it is reading a member list that holds only two** — not the roster, and not what SD9 onboarded.

## Only the first head works

| Head | Members | Sales DD | Distributor DD |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Anant Singh** | 13 | works | **works** |
| Sandeep Dadheech | 74 | onboarded SD1 | **fails** |
| Syed Aqil Rizvi | 32 | onboarded SD3 | **fails** |
| Lalan Kumar | 15 | onboarded SD6 | **fails** |
| Biju C.O + 7 others | 48 | onboarded SD9 | **fails** |

**Anant Singh was the first onboarding, wired directly in the D-series. Every head onboarded through the SD-series fails.**

## The likely cause

The Distributor Deep Dive reads **the same retailer rows** as the Sales Deep Dive — just grouped by Assigned Distributor rather than by member. If Sales works for Sandeep and Distributor does not, **the two pages are reading different member-sheet maps.**

That would be the **fifth** service built locally and then needed everywhere, after the identity registry, the period resolver, the primary period service and `stateCanon`.

---

# The question that matters more than the fix

**SD4, SD7 and SD10 were reported complete, with acceptance figures.**

SD4 required Rizvi's blank group to reconcile to **₹61,57,806 across exactly seven members**. SD7 required Lalan's blank group to be **empty**. SD10 required Nasir's to reconcile to **₹39,73,417**.

**If the page cannot load their sheets, none of those acceptances can have passed against real data.**

That needs answering before anything is rebuilt, because it determines whether this is one broken lookup or three phases that reported success without producing it.

## Why the shared resolver matters more than the immediate fix

Wiring Biju's twelve sheets into the Distributor page would make this screen work today. **It would leave the same fault waiting for the SKU Deep Dive, the Warning System's new distributor tab, and the AI payload** — all of which need the same rows.

Every one of the four previous local-service extractions was found by a bug rather than by design. This is the fifth, and it is now a predictable pattern rather than a surprise.

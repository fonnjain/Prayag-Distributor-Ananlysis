GOAL
prayag-price.com becomes the authoritative source for MRP and competitor
prices. It is the same application as prayag-competition-analysis.replit.app
on a custom domain — COMPETITION_API_KEY in Secrets already authenticates to
it.

WHY THE REVERSAL
We previously agreed Distributor Analysis would hold the MRP master and the
competition app would read from us. That was based on it holding 150 codes in
two categories. It now holds 6,213 codes across seven divisions with better
provenance than ours:

  mrp_sources          registry of source sheets per division, with sheet IDs
  mrp_load_batches     SHA256, file size, row count, loaded_by
  mrp_price_history    review_status, review_reasons, batch lineage
  catalog_products     division, series, size, UOM

File hashing and per-price review status are two things we do not have, and
the second is exactly what our 56 "ambiguous codes" needed.

ITS MODEL IS BETTER THAN OURS ON AMBIGUITY
We store CNS-15 twice, once per segment, resolved on (code, segment). It
stores each code ONCE with a multi-division string:
    "Ceramic Sanitaryware | PTMT & Plastic Fittings"        50 codes
    "CP Fittings / Faucets | PTMT & Plastic Fittings | ..."  3 codes
One code, one MRP, every division it belongs to named. Adopt that. Do not try
to split it back into per-segment rows.

============================================================
BUILD A SYNCED CACHE, NOT A LIVE READ
============================================================
Read from prayag-price.com on a schedule and store locally. Reasons:
  - The MRP page, the calculator and the Price Shrinkers tab must stay fast
  - A network failure must not take out the calculator
  - prayag-price.com stays authoritative; we hold a dated copy

mrp_synced
  item_code · product_name · division_raw (the full multi-division string)
  series_range · size · uom
  mrp · price_in_force_since · previous_mrp · change_pct
  status            revised | unchanged | new | discontinued
  colour variants where present (Ivory, White with Jet)
  source_batch_id · source_review_status · source_review_reasons
  synced_at

Carry source_batch_id and source_review_status THROUGH. A price we cannot
trace back to a batch on their side is a price we cannot defend.

Never write to prayag-price.com. Read only.

DIVISION MAPPING
Their divisions are not our segments:
    Pipes & Fittings · PTMT & Plastic Fittings · CP Fittings / Faucets ·
    Hardware · Ceramic Sanitaryware · CP (QUAA / FERN)
Map to our segment vocabulary, store the raw string unchanged, and report
anything unmapped. Do NOT overwrite division_raw with the mapped value.

For a multi-division code, map to ALL the divisions named — a code can belong
to more than one segment and that is the point of their model.

============================================================
WHAT HAPPENS TO OUR EXISTING MRP MASTER
============================================================
mrp_master and mrp_history have 5,750 rows and 8,949 history rows, and the
calculator, MRP page and Price Shrinkers tab read from them.

Do NOT delete them. Repoint the readers at mrp_synced, keep the old tables
until the new path is proven for one full sync cycle, then retire them in a
separate task.

Report the overlap: how many of our 5,694 codes appear in their 6,213, how
many of ours do not, and how many of theirs are new to us. The 817 register
codes with no MRP are the interesting case — check whether they exist there.

============================================================
COMPETITOR PRICES
============================================================
Same source, same sync. Keep the existing rule: their net price is DERIVED
from MRP less an assumed discount, not observed. Label it derived wherever it
appears beside a Market Survey figure, which IS observed.

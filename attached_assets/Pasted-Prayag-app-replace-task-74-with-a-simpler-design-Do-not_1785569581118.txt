Prayag app - replace task #74 with a simpler design. Do not build the occurrence
index; it inherits the same weakness as serial when the sheet re-sorts.

1. NIGHTLY FULL REPLACE OF THE OPEN MONTH.

   Once a night, for the current open month only:

     read every row for that month from SALE SHEET 26-27
     verify the read (see the guard below)
     in ONE transaction: delete that month's rows, insert the read in full
     record rows read and rows written

   NO identity key. NO dedup. NO tombstone. NO supersede. NO revive.
   If the sheet holds two identical rows, BOTH are written - the sheet is the
   truth for a live month, and it is not the loader's job to decide a row is a
   duplicate.

   ACCEPTANCE, one line: rows written must equal rows read, exactly.
   Any difference is a bug with nothing to interpret.

2. THE ONLY GUARD NEEDED - verify before deleting.

   Read first, count, and only then delete. If the read returns fewer rows than
   the last successful read of that month by more than a small tolerance, ABORT
   and leave the existing data alone. A failed read after a delete would empty
   the live month.

   Store the last good count per month in the database, not in memory - an
   in-memory baseline is lost on restart and the guard lapses for one cycle.

   Because this is a full replace inside a transaction, a partial write cannot
   occur. Either the whole month lands or nothing changes.

3. FREEZE A MONTH ON THE 7th OF THE FOLLOWING MONTH.

   A closed month gets 7 days of grace for late entries and corrections, then
   freezes permanently:

     Apr-26  closed 30 Apr  ->  frozen 7 May
     May-26  closed 31 May  ->  frozen 7 Jun
     Jun-26  closed 30 Jun  ->  frozen 7 Jul
     Jul-26  closed 31 Jul  ->  frozen 7 Aug

   Derive the freeze date from today's date - never a config list, so it advances
   on its own.

   A frozen month is skipped entirely: no read, no write, no replace. Record its
   row count and total as an anchor and assert them on every startup. If a frozen
   month's figures change, something wrote to it that should not have - fail
   loudly with the month named.

   Writing to a frozen month requires an explicit unfreeze with a recorded reason.

   NOTE: under this rule April, May and June should ALREADY be frozen. Freezing
   them would have prevented the May and June churn entirely.

4. THE SAME RULE ALREADY APPLIES TO CLOSED FISCAL YEARS.
   FY2023-24, FY2024-25 and FY2025-26 are loaded by straight copy and frozen.
   This extends the identical logic from years to months.

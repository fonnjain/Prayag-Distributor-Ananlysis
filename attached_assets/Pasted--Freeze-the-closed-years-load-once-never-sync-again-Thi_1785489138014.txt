# Freeze the closed years — load once, never sync again

*This removes several open problems at once rather than solving them one at a time.*

---

## The reasoning

FY2023-24, FY2024-25 and FY2025-26 are **closed**. Their figures will not change. Yet the system currently treats them as live sources to be re-read, re-matched and re-tombstoned on a schedule — and every problem of the past week comes from exactly that.

Load each once, freeze it, and these all disappear together:

| Problem | Why freezing removes it |
|---|---|
| FY2023-24 has no INVOICE NO or COLOR, so identity keys collide | identity only matters for re-sync matching; a one-time load needs only row position |
| Historical years were ingested under the old `lineUidKey` | no future sync means no mismatch |
| A truncated read could tombstone a closed year | no read, no tombstone |
| Schema differs per year — 10, 11 and 20 columns | handle it once at load, not on every tick |
| `REGISTER_SYNC_PAUSE` as a workaround | replaced by a real freeze |

## What this also settles

**The `lineUidKey` guard I asked for is no longer needed** for historical years. Once frozen, they are never synced, so the old-key mismatch cannot fire. The guard is only needed for FY2026-27, which has already been re-ingested under the new key.

**And FY2025-26's anchor gets corrected in the same pass.** ₹359.52 Cr has been quoted as exact in the head-merge verification, the Overview, the SKU denominators and both handovers. Freezing at ₹361.00 Cr fixes it permanently rather than leaving a known-stale figure in circulation.

# T2 — Engine targets per salesperson and State Head

*The engine works. It now needs to produce a target per person, which requires changing the basis it computes on.*

---

## The problem underneath the request

The engine baseline is **₹361 Cr from `sale_line` — that is PRIMARY**, and its customers are **distributors** (572 in the old-SKU population).

**Salespeople do not sell to distributors.** They book **secondary** business from **retailers** — the only one of the four measures that carries a salesperson name at all.

| | Primary | Secondary |
|---|---|---|
| Flow | Prayag → distributor | retailer → distributor |
| Customers | 572 distributors | 11,338 retailers |
| **Carries a salesperson?** | **no** | **yes — the only one** |
| Includes project/govt | yes, ₹20.65 Cr | no |

So a salesperson target cannot be cut from the primary baseline. **The three populations have to be rebuilt on the secondary register** — which is loaded for FY2025-26 at 379,441 lines.

That is also the more meaningful basis: **a retailer is the salesperson's own book; a distributor is not.**

## One thing to decide

**Should a State Head's target be the sum of their members, or set independently?**

They are not the same thing. A head also carries **primary** targets in `primary_state_targets`, which is a different measure entirely — Prayag to distributor, including project business their team never touches.

My suggestion: **show both, never merge them.** A head's secondary target is the sum of their team; their primary target is set separately. Presenting one as the other has caused confusion on every page where it has been tried.

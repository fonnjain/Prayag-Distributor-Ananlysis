Prayag app - AI Analytics, Phase A4 of 7. Same global rules. Same payload, same
numeric guard, same no-arithmetic constraint for every artifact below.

1. STATE HEAD REPORT (PDF). Team-level narrative: aggregate position, ranked member
   performance, spread of achievement across the team, which members carry the
   business, and which data-quality flags affect the aggregate. Where members carry
   NO_MEMBER_SHEET, say so in the report - an aggregate built on partial detail must
   declare it.

2. PRESENTATION (PPTX) at both levels. Charts are rendered NATIVELY by the app from
   payload figures - Claude supplies titles, ordering and commentary only, never chart
   data. Keep decks short: position, coverage, effort versus return, risks, actions.

3. SUGGESTIONS AND ACTIONS (PDF). Ranked, specific and tied to a figure. Each
   suggestion states the metric that motivates it and the expected effect. Rank by
   ease of execution first, since the easiest actions are what actually get done -
   for a salesperson that ordering is typically: assign the unassigned retailers,
   redirect effort away from non-converting accounts, then range expansion.

4. TRAVEL AND VISIT PLAN (PDF). Built from the existing visit-plan logic - do NOT
   have Claude invent a schedule. The app computes the month-by-month plan, the
   capacity and the shortfall; Claude writes the covering explanation only.
   State plainly on the document that batching is by district and distance from base,
   NOT route optimisation - the sheets carry district, city and distance, but no
   coordinates. Do not imply precision that the data cannot support.

5. PERFORMANCE REVIEW (PDF) - MANAGEMENT ONLY. Additional constraints, all mandatory:
     - Mark every page 'Management only - not for distribution to the individual'.
     - Mark the document 'Draft - requires human sign-off' and require an explicit
       action before it can be exported or shared.
     - Every data-quality flag that bears on the assessment MUST appear in the
       assessment itself. A member whose dormant retailers cannot order, whose sale
       column may be unmaintained, or who has 11 working days in the period must not
       be characterised as underperforming without that context stated alongside.
     - Distinguish clearly between what the person controls and what they do not.
     - No comparison to named colleagues; rank within the team is permissible, naming
       another individual's figures in someone's review is not.

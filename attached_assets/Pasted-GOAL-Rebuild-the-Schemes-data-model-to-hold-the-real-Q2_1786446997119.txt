MODEL - these fields are what the real data requires

scheme
  scheme_id
  title                  e.g. "Quarterly Scheme - CP / Sink / Sanitaryware"
  audience[]             ARRAY: distributor | direct_dealer | sub_dealer |
                         super_distributor
                         Annual Scheme carries TWO: direct_dealer AND
                         sub_dealer. A single flag cannot hold this.
  settlement             primary | secondary | pass_through
                         primary  = we pay the customer
                         secondary= we fund the distributor to pay
                         pass_through = distributor runs it, we fund via
                         extra discount (Free Quantity Scheme)
  qualification_basis    cumulative_value | single_bill_value |
                         single_bill_quantity
                         THIS IS CRITICAL. Cumulative accrues across the
                         quarter; single-bill resets every invoice; quantity
                         ignores value entirely. The existing Extra Earn
                         formula assumes cumulative and is WRONG on the
                         other two.
  territory_group        the raw scope string from the sheet, verbatim
  product_scope          the raw product string from the sheet, verbatim
  period_from, period_to period_to NULLABLE for open-ended schemes
  period_note            e.g. "Till further change", "onwards"
  audience_source_term   the sheet's own word, e.g. "SUB-DEALER"
  funding_note           for pass_through, e.g. "8% additional discount in
                         bill; 3% extra CN above 1000 pcs"

scheme_slab
  scheme_id, slab_order
  threshold_from, threshold_to    NULL upper bound = "& above"
  unit                            value | pieces | master_cartons
  rate                            numeric, NULL where no cash rate exists
  alt_reward                      text, e.g. "Trip to Goa - 1 person, 3N/4D"
  free_goods                      text, e.g. "110 Pcs", "1 PPR welding machine"
  reward_status                   ok | needs_clarification
  raw_text                        the slab's original text, always stored

TRIP-OR-PERCENTAGE SLABS
Where a slab reads "Trip to X OR N%", store rate = N and alt_reward = the
trip text, reward_status = ok. Extra Earn and nudges then compute off rate
exactly as today, with no new engine. The trip is a display label.

THE TWO AMBIGUOUS SLABS - do not interpret them
  1. PTMT-CP, A.P. AND TELENGANA, "On Billing of Rs.15,00,000 & Above"
  2. Annual Scheme, "Rs.25.00 Lac to Rs.49.99 Lac"
Set reward_status = needs_clarification, rate = NULL, store raw_text
verbatim, and render the original wording on screen. EXCLUDE them from every
nudge, Extra Earn and achievement calculation. Never substitute a guessed
rate. Show a visible "wording under review" marker so the gap is obvious
rather than silent.

TERRITORY GROUPS - map, do not parse
Scope strings carry person and market qualifiers inside the state list:
  "Delhi/ NCR/ W.UP/ UK/ RAJ/ HR/PB/ HP/ GUJRAT/ MAH (Mr. Lalan)"
  "E.UP/MP/CHTS/MAH (WAHID MKT.)"
Create a territory_group table mapping each group to its member states, with
the raw string retained. NOTE: Maharashtra appears in TWO groups with
DIFFERENT slabs - the mapping must be group-to-states, never state-to-group.
The same product family has three different slab tables by territory (CP
starts at Rs.30,000 / Rs.40,000 / Rs.50,000 depending on group).

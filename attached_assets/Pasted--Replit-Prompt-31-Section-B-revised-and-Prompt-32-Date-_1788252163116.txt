## Why B changed

A5 did not merely fail to settle east versus west. **It disproved both maps.**

All 14 `UP ( A )` customers attribute to **Anant Singh** — in transaction data,
in `person_state_coverage`, and in `customer_assignment`. The scheme territory
table has **WUP under Lalan** and **EUP under Wahid**. Neither is Anant Singh.

So fixing the spacing alone would take ₹2.44 crore of Anant Singh's customers and
route them into **Lalan's** territory schemes. They would begin qualifying for
the wrong head's schemes. That is worse than the current exclusion, and my
original instruction to fix the spacing independently was wrong.

**The UP fix is withdrawn until the territory question is answered.** Only
Uttarakhand proceeds.

## Notes

- The revised B is safe because Uttarakhand is unambiguous: the correct value is
  registered, nothing stores the misspelling, and no head assignment depends on
  it. The UP case is the opposite on every count.
- Question (a) in B is not optional. "Broad fallback behaviour" means Uttarakhand
  customers are being handled some way today that will stop when the join starts
  working. Fixing a broken join can change behaviour as much as breaking one.
- Prompt 32 question 5 is the one with consequences. A year-on-year state
  comparison against a baseline missing 13.3% produces growth that is partly an
  artefact of classification, not performance.
- Prompt 32 ends with an explicit instruction not to backfill. FY2025-26 is
  frozen, and the reflex here would be to fill in the states.

## Still needed before the UP fix can proceed

The blocking question is not east versus west. It is: **what territory
abbreviation should Anant Singh's UP customers carry?** WUP belongs to Lalan and
EUP to Wahid, and the production data says these customers are Anant Singh's.

Until there is an answer, ₹2.44 crore across 14 customers stays excluded from
scheme qualification entirely — including from schemes open to all states, since
the "All States" fallback is only reached after a state resolves.

That is a question for the business, not for code. Worth adding to the Prayag
pack alongside the Maharashtra query, or asking internally if the territory
structure is your own rather than theirs.

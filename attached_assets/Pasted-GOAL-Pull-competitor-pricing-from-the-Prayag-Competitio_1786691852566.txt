GOAL
Pull competitor pricing from the Prayag Competition Analysis app and show it
alongside our MRP and the Market Survey figures.

Read-only. This app never writes to the competition app.

THE SOURCE - know its limits before you build
  Base URL     https://prayag-competition-analysis.replit.app/api
  Auth         Bearer token, already in Secrets as COMPETITION_API_KEY
  Endpoint     GET /v1/comparison

What it actually holds today:
  Competitor brands   ONE - Sparsh Pearl
  Rows                150, of which 140 carry a price
  Categories          CPVC and uPVC only
  Snapshot            a single date, 5 March 2026
  Versioning          NONE - re-uploading a price sheet replaces the rows

So this covers roughly 150 codes in two categories against one competitor, at
one point in time five months ago. Our margin data covers about 2,000 codes
across eight segments monthly, and the survey will grow. Build the UI so this
reads as one thin input among several, not as authoritative market pricing.

Its "net price" is also NOT observed - the competition app computes it by
applying a flat 40% default discount to MRP. Label it as derived, never as a
street price. The Market Survey captures real net prices and the two must
never be presented as equivalent.

WHAT TO BUILD
1. A client that calls GET /v1/comparison with the bearer token, on a
   schedule (daily is ample given the data changes rarely) and on demand.

2. Store a LOCAL SNAPSHOT with a fetched_at timestamp. Do not query the
   competition app on every page load - it is a separate Replit app and will
   be slow or asleep.

   competitor_price
     competitor_brand · competitor_code · competitor_name · category
     mrp · net_price_derived · discount_pct_assumed
     source_fetched_at · prayag_item_code nullable
   Index on (prayag_item_code), (competitor_brand, category).

3. MAPPING TO OUR CODES - do not guess
   The competition app has no mapping table linking a competitor product to a
   Prayag code. Do NOT infer one from name similarity.

   Leave prayag_item_code NULL on import. Add a small mapping UI where a
   person links a competitor row to one of our codes, stored explicitly with
   who mapped it and when. Unmapped rows still display; they just cannot sit
   beside one of our items.

4. WHERE IT APPEARS
   - MRP page: for a Prayag code with a mapped competitor row, show the
     competitor's MRP beside ours, with the brand and the fetch date.
   - Market Survey page: on the per-item summary, add a competitor-MRP column
     beside our MRP and the surveyed median net.

   Every competitor figure carries its brand, its fetch date, and the word
   "derived" on the net price. A number without those three is misleading.

5. FAILURE BEHAVIOUR
   If the competition app is unreachable, show the last good snapshot with
   its age - "Competitor data from 5 March 2026, last checked <date>". Never
   show stale data as current, and never show an empty column that looks like
   "no competitor sells this".

   Distinguish three states explicitly: no competitor row exists for this
   code · a row exists but is unmapped · fetch failed.

DO NOT
- Do not write to the competition app, in any form.
- Do not feed competitor prices into the MRP calculator or any pricing
  recommendation yet. That comes later and needs its own review.
- Do not put COMPETITION_API_KEY anywhere in the client bundle. Server-side
  only.
- Do not touch margin_fact, sale_line, market_survey or mrp_history.

VERIFY - produced values, and a hash that resolves
1. Fetch once. Print rows imported, distinct brands, distinct categories, and
   the count with a price versus without. Expect roughly 150 rows, 1 brand,
   2 categories, 140 priced.
2. Print 5 imported rows in full, showing mrp, net_price_derived and the
   assumed discount, so the 40% assumption is visible.
3. Confirm prayag_item_code is NULL on every row at import. Print the count.
4. Map two rows by hand through the UI. Print both, showing who mapped them
   and when, and confirm they now appear beside our MRP on the MRP page.
5. Simulate the competition app being unreachable - block the call or use a
   bad URL. Confirm the page shows the last snapshot WITH its age and does
   not render an empty or zero price. Paste the rendered text.
6. Confirm the three states render distinguishably: no competitor row,
   unmapped row, fetch failed. Paste all three.
7. Confirm COMPETITION_API_KEY does not appear in any client-side bundle.
   Print the grep result.
8. Confirm margin_fact, sale_line, market_survey and mrp_history row counts
   are unchanged.
9. Commit hash, git cat-file -t, git merge-base --is-ancestor main with exit
   code.

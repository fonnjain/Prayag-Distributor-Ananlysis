**Date:** 1 September 2026
**Follows:** Prompt 28, which is applied and working
**Scope:** Section A is read-only. Section B is a code change, gated on A.

**Standing rule:** every figure states which database or source it came from.

**Confirmed working:** the Plan card moved from ₹159.09 Cr to ₹137.59 Cr,
September's ₹21.51 Cr plan is out, and the ₹107.48 Cr denominator is on screen.

**What it exposed:** three levels, two denominators.

| Level | Denominator |
|---|---|
| Company card | recorded-month plan, ₹107.48 Cr |
| **Head rows** | **full-period plan, August included, ₹137.59 Cr** |
| Member rows | recorded-month plan |

**78.74%**. A 17.23 point gap on one screen, and the head rows are the only level
using the odd basis. ₹30.11 Cr of plan sits in head denominators and not in the
company one — ₹14.85 Cr of it Sandeep's, ₹15.27 Cr shared across the other nine.

## Notes

- A2 is the whole point of Section A. If the ranking holds, this is a
  presentational correction. If it moves, heads have been compared against each
  other on a distorted basis, and that is a different conversation.
- A5 matters before B. If Comparison or the AI reports use the same full-period
  basis, fixing one surface makes them disagree with each other, which is worse
  than a consistent error.
- Section B keeps the Plan column as-is deliberately. The head's full-period plan
  is a real figure and should stay visible; only the denominator changes.
- C3 is the part worth internalising. Two individually correct decisions,
  combined, bias every head's percentage downward.

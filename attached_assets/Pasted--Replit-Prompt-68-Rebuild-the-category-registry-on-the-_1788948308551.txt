## Why the registry changes

Phase 1 seeded from `sale_line.group_canon` — 17 flat categories, derived from
ERP Item Type. That was the best available at the time.

**Prayag have now supplied their own two-level taxonomy.** `Master_and_Sub_category.xlsx`
maps 33 sub-categories to **6 real master categories**, and `Products_Grouping_Master.xlsx`
maps every item code to a sub-category through its Item Type.

Joined against FY2026-27 sales, coverage is **complete — zero unmapped codes,
zero unmapped value**:

| Master | Codes | Value | Share |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| PLUMBING | 1,039 | ₹44.78 Cr | 33.3% |
| PTMT | 1,221 | ₹42.60 Cr | 31.7% |
| C P | 1,126 | ₹33.17 Cr | 24.7% |
| SANITARYWARE | 235 | ₹6.86 Cr | 5.1% |
| SINK | 198 | ₹6.10 Cr | 4.5% |
| HARDWARE | 162 | ₹0.87 Cr | 0.7% |

Three categories carry 90% of the business. The flat 17 showed CPVC, UPVC, SWR,
AGRI and water tanks as separate peers rather than as **PLUMBING**.

## Three defects in the source file, to handle at load

**`C P` and `CP` are the same master.** One holds CP ACCESSORIES and GLASS, the
other CP ALLIED and TEFELON TAPE. Merge to a single `C P`.

**A header row leaked in** as `Master Category ← Sub Category`. Exclude it.

**`CONECTION` and `CONNECTION`** are both sub-categories under PTMT — a spelling
variant, not two things.

Handle these in the loader with the corrections **recorded**, not silently
applied. And tell Prayag so the source file can be fixed.

## Notes

- **A4 is the interesting one.** The old flat registry created a 7.84% overlap
  because a code could sit in three categories. Under master/sub a code has one
  Item Type and therefore one sub-category and one master — so the tabs should
  sum exactly to All. That removes the most confusing part of the current page.
- **The source corrections must be recorded, not silently applied.** `C P` versus
  `CP` is obvious to a human and invisible in data. Recording it means Prayag can
  fix the file, and the next loader does not have to rediscover it.
- **Superseding rather than deleting** keeps the Phase 1 mapping auditable. It is
  the first thing that read the registry, and its figures are already on screen.

# Replit Prompt 27 — Four cards, one label, four periods

**Date:** 1 September 2026
**Scope:** Read-only. No code change, no data change.

**Standing rule:** every figure states which database or source it came from.

**The headline:** the Target card shows ₹159.09 Cr and Sales Received shows
₹84.63 Cr. Dividing those two visible figures gives **53.19%**. The Achievement
card shows **78.74%**, computed against ₹107.48 Cr — a denominator that appears
nowhere on the page. That is a 25.5 percentage point gap and ₹51.62 crore of
difference between what a reader can derive and what is displayed.

## Notes

- Section A is the one that will cost credibility fastest. Everything else is a
  labelling problem a careful reader could work around. This one produces a
  number that cannot be checked, on a page whose whole purpose is being checked.
- B2 deserves a real answer rather than a period explanation. Sales exceeding
  bookings over a shorter window may be entirely legitimate — receipts against
  prior-year orders — but nothing on screen says so.
- C3 is the structural finding. Fifty-two members, 27.5% of the roster, cannot
  appear in Low Performers no matter how they perform. A tab that silently
  excludes a quarter of the population is worse than one that shows nothing.
- Section E decides a question going to Prayag. Query D2 in their pack currently
  asks them to confirm PLAN 26-27 is the target source. If the dashboard plan is
  already fed from those sheets, that query needs rewording; if plans are typed
  straight into the dashboard, it needs rewording differently. I am holding the
  revision until E3 comes back.

GOAL
Two new tabs on Market Survey, beside the three entry tabs (Existing SKU /
New SKU / New Customer).

============================================================
TAB 4 - SUBMISSIONS
============================================================
Everything recorded so far, grouped by survey type in this order:

  1. Existing customer, existing SKU   (survey_type = existing_sku)
  2. Existing customer, new SKU        (survey_type = new_sku)
  3. New customer                      (survey_type = new_customer)
  4. Unclassified                      (pre-rebuild rows)

Group header shows the count and the combined value where volume was recorded.

Columns:
  submitted   date, newest first within each group
  recorder    the self-declared name - label it as self-declared, since it is
              not authenticated
  retailer    with a marker where it is a pending prospect not yet approved
  state · district
  segment · Prayag item code · our MRP at the time
  competitor brand · competitor product
  net price · unit · pack size
  entry mode  net_direct or mrp_discount, so the working is visible
  reasons · monthly volume · note

MULTI-LINE SURVEYS
Rows sharing a survey_id were entered in one visit. Group them visually under
one retailer heading rather than repeating the retailer on every line - a
salesperson recording four items at one shop should read as one visit.

FILTERS
State head, state, segment, competitor brand, recorder, date range. Export to
xlsx.

EDITING
A row stays editable for 24 hours by the recorder, then locks. Show the lock
state. Do not allow silent edits to older rows.

============================================================
TAB 5 - ANALYTICS
============================================================
Read-only. Everything computed from stored survey rows, never recomputed from
sales data.

THE SAMPLE-SIZE RULE - build this first, it governs every figure
Every number on this tab carries n. Below n=5, label it INDICATIVE ONLY and
render it visibly weaker - muted, not bold. Never show a median from one or
two responses as though it were a market price.

The tab already has 7 surveys in the system. Almost everything will start
below n=5 and that is correct - the tab should look sparse until the field
has done the work.

WHAT IT SHOWS

  1. PER PRAYAG ITEM CODE
     our current MRP · surveyed competitor net, median · n · the spread
     (min-max) · the implied gap as a percentage
     Sort by the largest gap where n >= 5, then everything else.

  2. BY COMPETITOR BRAND
     surveys · segments they appear in · states · price range · median net
     Which brands are actually being met in the field, not which are in the
     competition database.

  3. WHY RETAILERS BUY ELSEWHERE
     Count each reason from the multi-select - price, availability, credit
     terms, relationship, scheme, quality - overall and by segment.
     This is the most actionable output on the page. If credit terms outrank
     price in a state, that is a different conversation from a pricing one.

  4. COVERAGE GAPS
     Segments and states with fewer than 5 surveys. Name them.
     A salesperson looking at this should be able to see where to go next.

  5. NEW SKU OPPORTUNITY
     From survey_type = new_sku: which Prayag codes are being bought from a
     competitor by customers who already buy from us. Grouped by code, with
     the retailers named.
     This is a push list built from field evidence rather than peer inference.

  6. COMPETITOR PRICE VS OURS, WHERE BOTH EXIST
     Where a code has both a survey net price and a competition-app row, show
     both. Label the competition-app figure DERIVED - it is MRP less an assumed
     40%, not an observed price. The survey figure is observed. Never present
     them as equivalent.

============================================================
DO NOT
============================================================
- Do not feed any survey price into MRP calculations, the MRP calculator, or
  any pricing recommendation. That remains a separate, reviewed step.
- Do not compute a median, an average or a gap without showing n beside it.
- Do not change the three entry tabs.
- Do not write to customer_master, mrp_history, margin_fact or sale_line.

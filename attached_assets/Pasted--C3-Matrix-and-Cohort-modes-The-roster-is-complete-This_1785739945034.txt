# C3 — Matrix and Cohort modes

*The roster is complete. This is the phase where the page starts producing decisions rather than figures.*

---

## Why the matrix matters more than the first two modes

Trajectory tells you a member is at 45%. Peer tells you he is below his team. **Neither tells you whether he is climbing or sliding** — and those are opposite conversations with the same number.

**Level and direction together is the whole point.** A member at 45% and rising needs support; one at 55% and falling needs a different discussion this week.

With 182 members across twelve heads now onboarded, this is the first phase that can look at the whole company at once.

```
Prayag app - Comparison Deep Dive, Phase C3 of 4. Same global rules.
Add Mode C (matrix) and Mode D (cohort). NO NEW COMPUTATION - both call
POST /api/comparison and render what it returns, including refusals.

The twelve guards from C1 apply unchanged. The basis strip and the full
channelLabel remain above the data.

1. MODE C - THE MATRIX. Many entities x many periods.

   Enable the second axis that C2 deliberately disabled.

   LAYOUT: entities down, periods across, ONE measure at a time. A matrix of
   many entities x many periods x many measures is unreadable - make the
   measure a selector, not a third axis.

   EACH CELL CARRIES: the value, and the change from the previous period in
   that row. Not a colour alone - a reader must be able to take the number.

   THE TWO DERIVED COLUMNS ARE THE POINT:
     LEVEL      the latest period's value
     DIRECTION  the trend across the selected periods, stated as a slope on
                the normalised measure, not eyeballed from colour

   Then the quadrant view, which is what turns this into a decision:
     high level, rising      performing and improving
     high level, falling     the early-warning quadrant - most valuable
     low level, rising       recovering, leave alone
     low level, falling      the intervention list

   THE FALLING-FROM-HIGH QUADRANT IS THE ONE NOBODY SEES TODAY. A member at
   70 per cent who was at 85 raises no warning anywhere in the app - they are
   above every threshold. Surface that quadrant first.

   GUARDS IN A MATRIX:
     a cell whose period is partial or has no actuals shows so, never a zero,
       and is EXCLUDED from the direction calculation
     an entity flagged TENURE is shown but not given a direction - too few
       periods of comparable tenure to establish one
     a period where the roster changed names the joiners and leavers, because
       a head's direction can move purely from membership. Excluding three
       leavers moved one team 51.7 to 58.4 per cent with no performance change
     cross-year money cells show real terms with the segment index named

2. MODE D - COHORT. Group against group.

   A cohort is a RULE, not a hand-picked list, so it re-evaluates as data
   changes. Support at minimum:
     retailers WITH an assigned distributor  vs  WITHOUT
     members above vs below a stated achievement band
     distributors by tier A / B / C
     customers retained vs reactivated vs at risk vs never
     segments in season this quarter vs out of season
     members with a mapped working sheet vs without

   Report per cohort: the population count, the measure, and the difference
   WITH ITS SAMPLE SIZE. A difference between cohorts of 3 and 800 is not a
   finding.

   THE ASSIGNMENT COHORT IS THE ONE TO GET RIGHT. It is the strongest
   relationship in the project and now computable across all twelve heads:
     Anant Singh   96.0 per cent active with a distributor, 5.0 without,
                   member correlation -0.90 (n=10)
     Sandeep       100 versus 16, correlation -0.33 (n=66)
   With 182 members loaded, report the company-wide figure and state plainly
   whether the retailer-level gap holds and what the correlation is at full
   scale. A weak correlation reported honestly is worth more than a strong one
   assumed.
   CARRY BOTH READINGS as always - a retailer with no distributor may have no
   route to order, OR '--' may be written when a retailer goes dormant. The
   data cannot distinguish them.

3. WHAT NEITHER MODE MAY DO
     no composite score across measures
     no colour without the number beside it
     no direction computed across periods of differing completeness
     no cohort difference reported without both population counts
     no entity silently dropped from a matrix - a guard-flagged row is shown
       with its flag, or listed below as excluded with the reason

4. EXPORT carries the full basis block, as in C2, plus the cohort rules used.

  a partial period cell says so and is excluded from direction
  a head whose roster changed between periods names the joiners and leavers
  a cross-year money matrix shows real terms per segment
  the assignment cohort reports company-wide across all 182 members, with the
    retailer-level gap, the member-level correlation, and the sample size
  a cohort with a small population states the count and suppresses the
    correlation
```

## The output I would look at first

**The falling-from-high quadrant.** Every alerting mechanism in this app is threshold-based — the Warning System fires below 50%, low performers below 50%. **A member who has gone from 85% to 70% triggers nothing**, because they are above every line.

That is the most useful thing a matrix can surface, and nothing in the app can currently see it.

## After C3

**C4** is the suggestion layer — ranked observations with the evidence behind them, reusing the existing push list and whitespace rather than computing anything new. That is the phase that turns the page from something a manager reads into something that tells them where to look.

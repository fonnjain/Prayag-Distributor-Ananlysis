## What makes this territory a useful test

**Direct-dealer business is 5.0% of order booking** — against 0.4% for Sandeep Dadheech and 6.2% for Anant Singh. So the parallel branch is material here, and the reconciliation to ₹61,57,806 across seven members is a real check rather than a formality.

**The geography is different.** East U.P, Madhya Pradesh, Chattisgarh and Maharashtra — a different climate and construction cycle from Sandeep's east and south. **If the Q4 seasonality pattern holds here too, it is a company-level fact.** If it does not, that is more useful still, because it means the push list must band per territory rather than nationally.

**And the unassigned finding gets its third test.** −0.90 on ten members, −0.33 on sixty-six. Thirty-two sits between them, and a third data point will settle whether the member-level correlation means anything at all.

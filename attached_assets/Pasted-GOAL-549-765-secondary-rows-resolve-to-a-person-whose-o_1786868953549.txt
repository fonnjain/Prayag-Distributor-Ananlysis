549,765 secondary rows resolve to a person whose own state_head is NULL in

Two phases. Report after the first and STOP.

============================================================
PHASE 1 - POPULATE state_head
============================================================

DO NOT DERIVE IT FROM THE STATE
A state can have several heads - Karnataka two, West U.P three, Rajasthan two.
A state-to-head lookup will attribute members to the wrong head and do it
silently. That is worse than leaving the column NULL.

RESOLVE THROUGH THE PERSON'S OWN CHAIN, in this order:

  1. The person IS a state head        -> state_head = themselves
  2. Walk reports_to upward until you reach someone with is_state_head = true
     -> that person. This is deterministic and it resolved 99.9% of active
     retailers when the customer master was built.
  3. The CRM dashboard roster gives a direct member-to-head mapping for 178
     members. Use it where the chain does not resolve.
  4. Still unresolved -> leave NULL and report it. Do not guess.

Record HOW each row was resolved in a new column, state_head_source:
    self | reports_to_chain | crm_roster | unresolved

That column is the difference between a value you can trust and one you
cannot. When a figure looks wrong later, the first question will be where the
head came from.

GUARD AGAINST THESE, all of which have already caused defects here:
  - Cycles in reports_to. Cap the walk and report anything that hits the cap.
  - Managers who have LEFT. Eight people report to three deactivated managers
    (Suresh Kumar Nair, Mahendra Kumar Jain, Karuppasamy R). Keep walking
    THROUGH them to the head above; do not stop at an inactive person.
  - Name variants. Sulindar and Sulinder Pal are one person; Pawan Sharma is
    Pawan Kumar Sharma. Resolve through the registry's own aliases, never on
    a raw string.

DO NOT touch secondary_sku_line, sale_line or any other table in this phase.
Populate person_registry only.

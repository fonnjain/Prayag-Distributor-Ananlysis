## Section A — the two surviving flat run-rates

Your A6 sweep found two, both labelled:

**Sales Deep Dive** — `YTD OB ÷ elapsed months × 12`
**Momentum** — a flat comparator alongside its preferred seasonal projection

Labelling is honest. But on a business with **34.56% of its year in Q4**, a flat
run-rate understates a full year by roughly **17 percentage points** — the exact
error just corrected on Overview, sitting two pages away under a different name.

```
Read-only. Change nothing.

A1. For a sample of five members, print the Sales Deep Dive flat run-rate
    beside the seasonal projection of the same figure. Show the gap in
    rupees and percent.

    If the gap is material, a reader comparing a run-rate on one page with
    a projection on another is comparing two different things.

A2. Same for Momentum. Which of the two does a reader see first, and which
    is emphasised?

A3. Does anything CONSUME the flat run-rate - an alert, a target proposal,
    an AI narrative, an export - or is it display only?

    A flat run-rate that only displays is a labelling question. One that
    feeds a threshold is a defect.

A4. RECOMMEND one of three:
      - show the seasonal projection as primary, flat run-rate secondary
      - remove the flat run-rate entirely
      - keep as is, if the label genuinely suffices

    My position: a reader does not reliably distinguish "flat run-rate"
    from "projection" when both are rupee figures on a card. If both are
    shown, the seasonal one should lead.

Report only.
```

## Section B — primary and secondary targets are not additive

Your B10 answer was clear:

> *"Primary growth proposals use company-dispatch data from `sale_line_current`.
> Secondary People proposals use retailer-booking data from `secondary_sku_line`.
> These engines measure different commercial flows and must not be added
> together."*

That is settled. The question is whether anything in the app already does it.

```
Read-only. Change nothing.

B1. Print every surface where a primary target and a secondary target
    appear for the SAME person, head or period.

    Sales Deep Dive shows both - "Secondary Target (to date)" and "Primary
    Target (to date)" - and a "Total Target (to date)".

    Is that total the SUM of the two? For Rajan Srivastava the page showed
    secondary Rs 1.63 Cr, primary Rs 26.66 L and total Rs 1.90 Cr.
    1.63 Cr + 26.66 L = 1.8966 Cr, which rounds to 1.90 Cr.

    So it appears to add them. Confirm.

B2. If it does add them, is that wrong given B10, or is "Total Target"
    a legitimate combined commitment even though the two are measured on
    different flows?

    That is a business question, not a code one. State the case both ways
    so it can be decided.

B3. What else reads that total? "Total OB Achievement" is computed as
    (Sec OB + DD) / Total Target - so the combined total is already
    driving an achievement percentage and, through it, alert thresholds.

B4. Does anything sum primary and secondary at HEAD or COMPANY level -
    State Head Dashboard, Company Reports, Targets, AI Targets, any export?

B5. If the sum is legitimate, say so plainly and note it where both appear,
    so nobody re-raises this. If it is not, print what changes and which
    achievement percentages move.

Report only. Do not change any figure.
```

## Notes

- **B1 is the specific instance.** Sales Deep Dive shows a "Total Target" that
  appears to be the arithmetic sum of a dispatch-based primary target and a
  booking-based secondary target. Given B10 says those must not be added, either
  the page is wrong or "must not be added" needs qualifying.
- **B3 is why it matters beyond presentation.** That total is the denominator of
  Total OB Achievement, which drives alert thresholds. If the denominator is
  questionable, so is every achievement percentage built on it.
- **A3 is the same distinction one level down.** A flat run-rate that only
  displays is a labelling question; one feeding a threshold is a defect.
- Neither section is urgent. Both are worth settling before anyone builds further
  on top of these figures.

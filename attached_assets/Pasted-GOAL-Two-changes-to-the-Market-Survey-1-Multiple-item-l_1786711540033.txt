GOAL
Two changes to the Market Survey.

1. Multiple item lines per survey. Today one submission captures one product.
   A salesperson standing in a shop sees several, and should not re-enter the
   retailer four times.

2. Three tabs, because three different questions are being asked and they need
   different fields and produce different actions.

============================================================
THE THREE TABS
============================================================

TAB 1 — EXISTING CUSTOMER, EXISTING SKU
  The retailer buys from Prayag AND buys this same item from a competitor.
  The question: we are losing share on an item we already sell him.
  Required: Prayag item code, our MRP shown, competitor brand, their price.
  This is the most actionable tab - the relationship and the shelf space
  already exist.

TAB 2 — EXISTING CUSTOMER, NEW SKU
  The retailer buys from Prayag but buys THIS item from a competitor only.
  The question: a range gap with a named competitor attached.
  Required: Prayag item code (the equivalent we could sell him), our MRP,
  competitor brand, their price.
  Feeds the push-list conversation with evidence.

TAB 3 — NEW CUSTOMER
  The retailer buys nothing from Prayag.
  The question: what is a prospect currently paying, and to whom.
  Required: competitor brand, their product, their price, segment.
  Prayag item code is OPTIONAL here - offer "our nearest equivalent" as a
  link, not a requirement.

Store the tab on every row as survey_type. Do not infer it later from whether
fields are populated.

============================================================
VALIDATE THE TAB CHOICE FROM DATA, DO NOT JUST TRUST IT
============================================================
When a retailer and an item code are both selected, look up the secondary
register for the last 12 months and show plainly:

  "This retailer bought 451 from you in the last 12 months - 340 pcs"
     or
  "No purchase of 451 recorded in the last 12 months"

On TAB 1, if there is no recorded purchase, warn - it may belong in Tab 2.
On TAB 2, if there IS a recorded purchase, warn - it may belong in Tab 1.
Warn, do not block. The register may be incomplete and the salesperson is
standing in the shop.

On TAB 3, show whether the retailer exists in customer_master at all. If they
do, they are not a new customer and the survey belongs in Tab 1 or 2.

============================================================
MULTIPLE ITEM LINES
============================================================
Section 1 (retailer) and the recorder are captured ONCE per survey.
Section 2 becomes a repeating block: segment, item code, competitor brand,
competitor product, price, unit, pack size.

  - "+ Add another item" adds a line
  - Each line can be removed
  - Each line validates independently - one bad line does not block the others
  - Our MRP shows per line as the item code is chosen
  - On save, write ONE row per item line, all sharing the same retailer,
    recorder, survey_type and timestamp, linked by a survey_id

A salesperson recording four competitor items at one shop enters the shop
once and the products four times. That is the shape of the visit.

============================================================
MAKE THE TABS SELF-EXPLANATORY
============================================================
Each tab carries one line of plain text under its title saying who it is for.
Something like:

  Tab 1: "He buys this item from us AND from someone else."
  Tab 2: "He buys from us, but buys this item from someone else."
  Tab 3: "He buys nothing from us yet."

Do not rely on the tab label alone. Somebody using this for the first time in
a shop should not have to guess.

============================================================
DO NOT
============================================================
- Do not create customer_master rows. New retailers still go to the Review
  Queue as pending prospects.
- Do not feed any survey price into MRP calculations or pricing
  recommendations.
- Do not lose existing market_survey rows. Backfill survey_type on them -
  mark them 'unclassified' rather than guessing.

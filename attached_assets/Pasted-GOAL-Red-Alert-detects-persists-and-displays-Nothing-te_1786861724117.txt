GOAL
Red Alert detects, persists and displays. Nothing tells anyone. Build routing.

The destocking alert would have fired on Aradhya Kedia in January 2026,
three months into his primary gap and six months before the retailers beneath
him went silent. Everything is in place to have raised it, persisted it and
marked it OPEN 3 PERIODS by March. Nobody would have seen it.

============================================================
THE ROUTING RULES - decided, build to these
============================================================
  A-category (salesperson)     -> the State Head
  B, C and S categories        -> the State Head AND the CEO

  Severe alerts                -> on raise, immediately
  Salesperson alerts           -> weekly digest

Severe means: any S-category (destocking), any C-category, and B3 distributor
rollups. Everything else digests. Make this configurable rather than
hardcoded - the line between "tell me now" and "tell me weekly" will move
once people use it.

Not one notification per alert. A notification per fire becomes noise within a
fortnight and then nothing is read at all.

============================================================
TWO ESCALATION LEVELS, CONTACTS ENTERED BY THE USER
============================================================
alert_recipient
  id
  alert_code_pattern    'A*' | 'B*' | 'C*' | 'S*' | 'B3' | '*'
  scope_type            state_head | all
  scope_value           the head's person_id, or null for all
  escalation_level      1 (first recipient) | 2 (escalates to)
  name
  channel               whatsapp | email | in_app
  contact               phone number or email, entered by the user
  cadence               on_raise | weekly
  is_active

alert_delivery
  alert_id · recipient_id · channel · escalation_level
  sent_at · delivered_at · opened_at · acknowledged_at
  status                pending | sent | failed | skipped

ESCALATION
  Level 1 receives the alert when it is raised or in the digest.
  If it is not ACKNOWLEDGED within the escalation window, level 2 receives it
  with the original raise date and how long it has been open.

  Escalation window: configurable, default 7 days for severe alerts, 14 for
  digest alerts.

  Escalating does not stop level 1 seeing it. Both hold it until it clears.

A SETTINGS PAGE FOR THE CONTACTS
The user enters names, phone numbers and email addresses per rule. Do not
seed contacts from the HR file - phone numbers there are unverified and 62
employee codes in that file are already implausible. The user types them,
knowingly.

For each rule show: which alerts it covers, which scope, who is level 1, who
is level 2, and the escalation window.

============================================================
CHANNEL - WHATSAPP IS THE TARGET, NOT YET CONFIGURED
============================================================
Build the channel as an interface with three implementations:

  in_app     works now - a notification bell and an unread count
  email      works now - digest and on-raise
  whatsapp   INTERFACE ONLY. Store the number, render the message body,
             record the delivery row with status = pending, and log that no
             provider is configured.

Do NOT integrate a WhatsApp provider in this task. When one is chosen the
implementation drops in behind the same interface with no change to the rules,
the recipients or the digest.

A recipient set to whatsapp must still be visible in the delivery log with a
readable reason - "no provider configured" - never silently skipped.

============================================================
THE DIGEST
============================================================
Weekly, one message per recipient, covering their scope.

  what is NEW since the last digest
  what is STILL OPEN, with how many periods
  what CLEARED since the last digest
  what is ESCALATING to them from level 1

If a recipient has nothing, send nothing. An empty digest teaches people to
ignore the next one.

The digest is generated from stored alerts, never recomputed. It must match
the page exactly - if the page says 33 and the digest says 31, nobody trusts
either.

============================================================
DELIVERY LOG ON THE PAGE
============================================================
Every alert card shows who was told, by which channel, and when. This is not
decoration.

Without it, "nobody told me" and "I was told and did not act" are
indistinguishable - and that distinction is the whole reason an escalation
page exists.

Show acknowledged-by and acknowledged-at beside it.

============================================================
DO NOT
============================================================
- Do not integrate a WhatsApp provider.
- Do not send anything to a real contact during verification. Use a dry-run
  mode that writes delivery rows and renders the message body without
  transmitting.
- Do not seed contacts from HR data.
- Do not change detection, thresholds, guards or the alert store.

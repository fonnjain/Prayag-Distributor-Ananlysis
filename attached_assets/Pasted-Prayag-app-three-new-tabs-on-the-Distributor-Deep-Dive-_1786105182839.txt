Prayag app - three new tabs on the Distributor Deep Dive. Same global rules.
All three respect the geography -> distributor -> state head filter chain.

0. RECONCILE THE TWO DISTRIBUTOR VOCABULARIES FIRST. Report before building.
   Distinct names in each source, exact matches, matches after normalisation,
   and names appearing in only one.
   Apply the identity rule - same only if the name matches AND the state agrees
   AND they do not appear as separate rows for the same period. NEVER auto-merge
   on similarity. A wrongly merged distributor produces a concentration figure
   that is simply false and the numeric guard cannot catch it.

TAB 1 - SECONDARY SALES
   What retailers bought FROM this distributor, from the FY2026-27 secondary
   register. Apr-Jul only, Rs 81.36 Cr company-wide. The coverage note must
   derive from the loaded months, never be hardcoded.

   Show: secondary NET, gross, effective discount; retailers buying and how many
   active; codes and segments moved; month distribution; top retailers with
   concentration.

   THEN THE FLOW GAP AT ITEM-CODE LEVEL - the point of the tab.
     primary in (bought from Prayag) vs secondary out (sold to retailers)
   STATE BOTH READINGS EVERY TIME: stock building at the distributor, OR
   business moving outside the attributed channel. No stock statements exist, so
   they cannot be distinguished. NEVER phrase it as an accusation.
   Flag any CODE where primary in is large and secondary out is near zero -
   stock sitting still, far more actionable than a total.

TAB 2 - EXISTING vs NEW SKU
   Reuse the Targets engine's three populations. Do not write a second
   definition.

   Per distributor, on BOTH primary (what they buy) and secondary (what their
   retailers buy):
     EXISTING SKU  in the baseline period AND this period - value, codes, growth
     NEW SKU       this period, NOT in the baseline - value, codes, segments
     LOST SKU      in the baseline, absent now - ranked by baseline value

   Baseline is the same fiscal months of the prior year, like months only. For a
   closed prior year, state the frozen anchor used.

   THE READING THAT MATTERS: a distributor growing on EXISTING SKU alone is
   buying more of the same and is exposed if those lines soften. One growing on
   NEW SKU is widening. Report both as a SHARE OF GROWTH, not just totals.

   Show real terms beside nominal using that distributor's own segment mix - the
   index runs CPVC 1.007 to water tank 1.181.

TAB 3 - WHERE AND HOW TO PUSH
   CHECK THE FLOW GAP BEFORE RECOMMENDING ANYTHING. A distributor whose primary
   in far exceeds secondary out is already carrying stock; pushing more SKU at
   them is wrong. Lead the tab with the verdict: PUSH, or CLEAR STOCK FIRST.

   Then the push list - reuse the K3 engine, do not rebuild it:
     TIER 1 RANGE   codes in a segment they already buy - 67 per cent of one
                    real list, and the cheapest route
     TIER 2 LAPSED  bought before, stopped - proven demand from this customer
     TIER 3 ACTIVE  peers buy it, in a segment they are active in
     TIER 4 NEW     peers buy it, in a segment they do not touch
   Top 10 by default, ranked within tier by peer penetration x median peer net.
   NAME THE PEERS - "13 of 27 peers your size in your state buy this and you do
   not" is arguable in a way a target never is.

   THE "HOW", not just the "where". Each recommendation carries:
     the segment's PEAK QUARTER on territory-only curves - 13 of 14 peak Q4, so
       a Q4 code pushed in August is January groundwork and must say so
     WHICH RETAILERS beneath this distributor could take it
     THE DISCOUNT POSITION - flag any code already 5 points or more above its
       own territory norm. Volume on an over-discounted code is a margin
       question first
     THE SALESPERSON covering those retailers, so the list reaches someone who
       can act

   ALSO SHOW COVERAGE, a different kind of push:
     unassigned retailers in their districts - 96.0 per cent of assigned
       retailers are active against 5.0 per cent of unassigned, and this is an
       ADMINISTRATIVE fix, the fastest available
     dormant retailers beneath them, ranked by prior-year value
     districts they serve with no distributor coverage at all

Acceptance - show the rendered result:
  the two vocabularies reconciled and the match rate reported
  Tab 1 shows secondary NET with a derived coverage note, and the flow gap per
    segment and per code
  a code with high primary in and near-zero secondary out is flagged
  Tab 2 reports existing, new and lost on BOTH primary and secondary, with
    growth split between existing and new
  real terms use that distributor's own segment mix
  Tab 3 leads with PUSH or CLEAR STOCK FIRST
  the push list is tiered, capped at 10, with named peers and peak quarters
  unassigned retailers in their districts are listed
  no figure appears without its source named

Calendar months are the wrong unit for this. Distributors stock BEFORE Diwali,
so the buying month and the festival month are often different. Use windows
defined relative to the festival date.

DIWALI DATES - verified
    24 Oct 2022 · 12 Nov 2023 · 1 Nov 2024 · 20 Oct 2025 · 8 Nov 2026
A 23-day spread between 2023 and 2025.

FOR EACH COMPLETE FISCAL YEAR IN THE REGISTER, print territory-channel net and
invoice count for:

  a. WEEKS RELATIVE TO DIWALI - the analytically correct unit
     weeks -8 to -1 (the eight weeks before), week 0 (festival week),
     weeks +1 to +4 (after). One row per week per year.
     If the pattern holds, the peak week should sit in the same RELATIVE
     position each year even though the calendar date moves.

  b. MONTHS RELATIVE TO DIWALI - what people actually think in
     D-2, D-1, D (the month containing Diwali), D+1, D+2.
     For FY2025-26 that is Aug, Sep, Oct, Nov, Dec.
     For FY2024-25 that is Sep, Oct, Nov, Dec, Jan.
     Print each as a share of its own fiscal year.

  c. THE SAME WINDOWS BY CALENDAR MONTH, side by side, so the distortion is
     visible. October 2024 was a stocking month; October 2025 was a
     post-festival month. If the app compares them like for like, that
     comparison is wrong and I want to see by how much.

  d. Segment split for the four largest - PTMT, CP, CPVC, SWR. Sanitaryware and
     taps should follow the festival; pipe may follow monsoon and construction

  e. HOLI, the same treatment. 8 Mar 2023 · 25 Mar 2024 · 14 Mar 2025 ·
     3-4 Mar 2026. All in March, so less disruptive - but confirm whether
     March shows a pre-Holi effect at all, or whether its strength is purely
     financial year-end.

THE QUESTION THIS ANSWERS
Does the sales peak MOVE with the festival date, or stay in the same calendar
month? If it moves, the fixed monthly seasonal curve is wrong and every
October and November year-on-year comparison is distorted. If it stays,
monsoon and the construction season dominate and the current curve is right.

Do not adjust anything. I want the evidence first.

GOAL
When someone leaves, the app must not silently rewrite the hierarchy beneath
them. Their team moves to a clearly-marked holding head, a warning appears,
and someone decides where the team goes.

Two capabilities:
  1. Record that a person has left the company.
  2. Handle the team they leave behind, without breaking attribution.

============================================================
1 - MARKING SOMEONE AS LEFT
============================================================
Deactivation already exists. Extend it to record WHY and WHEN.

  person.left_date            the date they actually left, not today
  person.departure_reason     resigned | terminated | retired | transferred |
                              other
  person.departure_note       free text

Never delete a person. History depends on them existing - eleven months of
sales may be attributed to them.

The UI asks for the leaving date, not the date of entry. Someone recording a
departure a fortnight later must be able to say so, because everything
downstream is effective-dated from that date.

============================================================
2 - THE HOLDING HEAD
============================================================
When a person with direct reports is marked as left, the app does NOT
reassign their team automatically and does NOT leave them dangling.

Create a system person per departed head:

  name            "Unassigned — <departed head's name>"
  is_holding      true
  is_person       false     so they never appear in headcounts, ratios,
                            concentration alerts or person-level reports
  created_from    the departed person's id
  created_at

Their direct reports move to this holding head. Everyone BELOW those reports
keeps their own manager - only the top layer moves.

A holding head is not a state head. It exists so nobody is orphaned while a
decision is pending.

WHY A NAMED HOLDING HEAD RATHER THAN A SINGLE DUMMY
One shared dummy would merge two departures into one bucket, and nobody could
tell whose team was whose. Naming it after the departed head keeps the
question answerable: "who used to report to Sandeep."

============================================================
3 - EFFECTIVE DATING - THIS IS THE PART THAT MUST NOT BREAK
============================================================
Moving a team to a holding head is a reassignment like any other. It is
effective-dated from left_date.

Historical attribution stays with the departed head for every period they
actually held it. Reports read the assignment IN FORCE during the period
being reported.

That guarantee was verified in Phase 3: reassign a customer, and FY2025-26
value by head must be IDENTICAL before and after. The same must hold here.
If marking Sandeep Dadheech as left changes his FY2025-26 total, effective
dating is not working and that is a stop condition.

Their customers do NOT move automatically either. A customer assigned to a
departed person stays assigned, effective-dated, and appears in the existing
reassignment queue.

============================================================
4 - THE WARNING
============================================================
A holding head with people under it is a visible, persistent warning - not a
toast that disappears.

  On the Organisation page, at the top:
      "3 teams have no head. 47 people report to a holding head."
      naming each departed head and the count beneath them.

  On each holding head in the tree: a marker showing how many people and how
  long it has been unresolved. Days open, prominently, after 7 days.

  It does not clear until every person under it has a real manager.

Do not route this into Red Alert. That page is for commercial escalation;
this is an organisational task with a named owner.

============================================================
5 - RESOLVING IT
============================================================
Two paths from the holding head, both with the impact preview that already
exists on moves:

  APPOINT A NEW HEAD
    Pick an existing person or create a new one. Everyone under the holding
    head moves to them in one transaction. The holding head is then retired.

  DISTRIBUTE THE TEAM
    Assign each person individually to different managers. The holding head
    retires when the last one leaves it.

Both are effective-dated from the date of the decision, NOT from left_date.
The gap between someone leaving and a replacement being appointed is real and
the data should show it.

A holding head with nobody under it is retired automatically and kept in
history.

============================================================
DO NOT
============================================================
- Do not delete a person, ever.
- Do not auto-assign a team to the departed person's own manager. That is a
  decision, not a default.
- Do not move customers automatically.
- Do not count holding heads in headcounts, team sizes, ratios or the C6
  concentration denominator.
- Do not change sale_line, secondary_sku_line or any attribution table.

============================================================
VERIFY - produced values, and a hash that resolves
============================================================
1. Print FY2025-26 total net BY HEAD. Save it.
2. Mark a person WITH direct reports as left, with a left_date two weeks ago.
   Print the holding head created, who moved to it, and confirm people two
   levels down did NOT move.
3. Print FY2025-26 total net by head AGAIN. It must be IDENTICAL to step 1.
   If any head's total moved, stop and report - effective dating is broken.
4. Print the warning as rendered on the Organisation page.
5. Confirm the departed person's customers did NOT move, and that they appear
   in the reassignment queue.
6. Appoint a new head. Confirm the whole team moves in one transaction, the
   holding head retires, and the warning clears.
7. Repeat with the distribute path - assign people individually and confirm
   the holding head retires only when the last one leaves.
8. Confirm holding heads are excluded from: headcount, team size, the
   Organisation page ratios, and the C6 denominator. Print each count with
   and without.
9. Confirm change_log records the departure, the holding-head creation, and
   every move, with reason and operator in the correct columns.
10. Confirm sale_line, secondary_sku_line and customer row counts unchanged.
11. Push. Print git rev-parse HEAD and git rev-parse origin/main.
12. git cat-file -t <hash>, then git merge-base --is-ancestor <hash> main.

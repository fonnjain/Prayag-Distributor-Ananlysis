## Section A — the decision, unchanged and still first

There are two pending measures on this page and they are **not the same
population**:

| | Measure | Source | Value |
|---|---|---|---|
| Factory pending | **unit quantity** | REPORT 2 sheet | 2,98,679 pcs |
| Derived pending | **rupee value** | OB minus Sale sheets | ₹16.41 Cr |

The page says it plainly: *"They are in different units and cannot be added or
directly compared."*

```
Decide before building.

OPTION A - PRICE THE FACTORY PENDING QUANTITIES
  Both tabs describe the SAME balance through the SAME attribution
  hierarchy. A party's quantity and its amount are one order in two units.
  Cost: the price basis is a choice we make.

OPTION B - SHOW DERIVED PENDING
  A value measure from real sheets rather than a price assumption.
  Cost: a DIFFERENT population from the quantity tab, with no party-level
  or member-level detail - so it cannot drill at all, and the two tabs
  could not reconcile.

MY RECOMMENDATION: OPTION A, and more strongly than before.

  Prompt 96 built a hierarchy that reconciles to zero at every level.
  Option B would have no member or party breakdown to hang on it, so the
  Amount tab could not drill and the two tabs would disagree by design.

  Derived pending stays where it is, in the cross-check block, as the
  independent check it already is.
```

## Section B — read-only, the price basis

```
Read-only. Change nothing.

B1. GRAIN FIRST - this may change everything.

    The A1 audit found REPORT 2 carries: B state head, C party name,
    D balance qty, E-AB product-group quantities. Twenty-three product
    groups - GARDEN PIPE, WATER TANK, C P, CISTERN and so on.

    THERE IS NO ITEM CODE. Pricing must therefore use a GROUP-LEVEL
    average price, not an item price.

    Confirm that, and say what a group average would be built from -
    FY2026-27 sale_line amount over quantity, per group_canon? And over
    what period?

    A group average across a group as wide as "C P" spans very different
    products. Print the price dispersion within each group so the weakness
    is measurable, not just acknowledged.

B2. THE PRICE CANDIDATES. Compute the company pending total under each:

      group average realised price x pending qty - from sale_line,
        FY2026-27
      group average MRP x pending qty
      group median realised price x pending qty

    MRP will overstate badly - measured realisation runs 45-62% below MRP.
    Print all three so the gap is visible.

B3. SANITY CHECK AGAINST DERIVED PENDING. Derived pending is Rs 16.41 Cr.

    2,98,679 pieces at Rs 16.41 Cr implies about Rs 550 per piece. Is that
    plausible against measured group averages?

    If priced pending lands at Rs 30 Cr or Rs 5 Cr, find out why before the
    tab ships.

B4. UNPRICEABLE PENDING. Which of the 23 product groups have no usable
    price, and how many pieces sit in them?

    Note WT LID, QUAA, TEFELON TAPE and FLOOR TRAP are small groups where
    a reliable average may not exist.

B5. WATER TANKS. The page states they are pieces in this source, not
    litres - which is correct. Confirm the price applied is per tank.

    A litre price on a piece quantity would understate by 500 to 5,000x.

B6. THE DISPUTED AND UNASSIGNED BUCKETS carry 41,888 and 58,839 pieces for
    Sandeep alone, and 1,32,491 unassigned company-wide.

    Those price exactly as any other pending does - the pricing is by
    product group, and the attribution bucket does not affect it. Confirm.
```

## Section C — the build

```
Code change, after A and B.

Two tabs: QUANTITY and AMOUNT.

1. SAME HIERARCHY, SAME BUCKETS, SAME RECONCILIATION.

   State Head -> Team Member -> Party -> product group, with named,
   unassigned and disputed buckets on both tabs.

   Amount must reconcile to zero difference at every level, exactly as
   quantity does. Print Sandeep's amount reconciliation alongside his
   quantity one.

2. SWITCHING TABS KEEPS POSITION. A user drilled into Sandeep -> a member
   -> a party stays there. Switching the measure must not reset the view
   or collapse the tree.

3. THE HEAD-ROW COVERAGE LINE STAYS on both tabs. "42.82% has exactly one
   assignment candidate; 40.20% safely mapped" is about attribution, not
   about units, so it is true on both.

4. STATE THE PRICE BASIS PROMINENTLY on the Amount tab. Not a footnote:

     "Pending value = pending quantity x average realised price per product
      group, FY2026-27. Estimated worth of outstanding orders, not an
      invoiced amount. The source carries product groups, not item codes,
      so this is a group-level estimate."

   Quantity is a measured fact. Amount is a calculation on a coarse grain.
   Both facts belong on the tab.

5. UNPRICEABLE IS EXPLICIT, never zero. A party whose pending sits in an
   unpriceable group shows blank-grey with the reason. A party with
   genuinely zero pending shows zero.

   Tab header states the split: "Rs X Cr priced, Y pieces unpriceable."

6. THE CROSS-CHECK BLOCK gains one line - priced factory pending against
   derived pending, noting they are independent measures which should
   agree in magnitude. That is the block's stated purpose.

7. THE EXPORT, built in Prompt 96, gains amount columns beside quantity at
   every hierarchy level, with the price basis in the Info sheet.

DO NOT CHANGE any quantity figure. 2,98,679 pieces, 147 parties, 15 heads,
23 groups, and every Prompt 96 bucket total must be identical before and
after. The regression tests from Prompt 96 must still pass unchanged.

AFTER APPLYING, print:
  - company totals on both tabs
  - Sandeep on both, with both reconciliations
  - priced versus unpriceable split
  - the cross-check line against derived pending
  - confirmation the Prompt 96 tests pass
```

## Notes

- **B1 is now the load-bearing question**, and the A1 audit already half-answers
  it: the source has product groups, not item codes. So this is a group-level
  estimate and must be labelled as one. B1's dispersion check is what makes the
  weakness measurable rather than merely disclosed.
- **Option A is a stronger recommendation than before.** Prompt 96 built a
  hierarchy that reconciles to zero at every level; derived pending has no member
  or party detail to hang on it, so Option B could not drill at all.
- **Requirement 1 is the new constraint.** The Amount tab must reconcile through
  the same three buckets. If it reconciles only at head level, switching tabs
  changes the shape of the answer rather than its units.
- **B3 remains a free check.** If priced factory pending lands near ₹16.41 Cr,
  two independently sourced measures agree and the cross-check block earns its
  keep.

## The two checks that matter most

**The distinct list of column K values.** That is the ground truth for the sheet's state vocabulary, and it has already cost two rounds of investigation — my read of the FY2026-27 sale sheet said `W-BENGAL` and `MAHARASTRA L/R`; the database audit said those spellings exist nowhere. **These files will settle which layer holds what.**

**Zero state disagreements.** The earlier validation found none across 376 customers. If these fifteen files produce disagreements where that comparison did not, something in the parsing differs — and it is better to find that before ₹44 Cr is reattributed on the strength of it.

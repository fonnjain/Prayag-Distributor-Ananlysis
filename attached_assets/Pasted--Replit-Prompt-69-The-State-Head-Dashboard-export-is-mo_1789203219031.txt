## Notes

- **Section A is the one that matters most.** An export telling twelve state heads
  that nobody on their team sold anything is not a formatting issue. If it reached
  them it would be read as fact.
- **C1 is likely the root of half the empty cells.** If the export searches Drive
  for a 2026-27 order booking workbook, it will never find one — the data lives in
  `secondary_order_line` now. Forty-seven fields are suppressed by a check looking
  in the wrong place.
- **D1 matters for the formatting work that follows.** A blank cell that means
  "genuinely unknown" deserves the light-grey treatment the tab describes. A blank
  cell that means "we looked in the wrong place" is a bug wearing the same
  clothes.

## Formatting, once the sheets have content

Worth doing and straightforward at that point: freeze panes and repeating headers,
Indian number formats, percentages as percentages, consistent column widths, no
99-column sheets at 9% fill, and an Info tab stating FY, period, filters and
provisional months.

The Info tab already does part of that — it correctly carries "Jun–Sep 2026 are
provisional and may change." That is the provisional labelling working, and it is
the one thing in this export doing exactly what it should.

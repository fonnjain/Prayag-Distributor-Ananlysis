## Notes

  "Unrecorded" currently means both *an open month that will fill in* and *a row
  that does not exist*. The correction treats them identically, and they are not
  the same thing. Anywhere else that distinction is collapsed will have the same
  problem.
- If B0.3 comes back close to 140.72%, ship B1. If it comes back materially
  lower, the correction is trading one distortion for another and needs a
  condition on missing rows before it goes in.

  change, five colour bands change (Lalan, Pawan, Nasir and Sulinder from orange
  to amber; Sunil Patel from yellow to emerald), and nothing else moves — no Low

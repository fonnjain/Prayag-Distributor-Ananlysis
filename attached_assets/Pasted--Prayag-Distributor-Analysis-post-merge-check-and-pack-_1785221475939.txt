*Two parts. Part A verifies that the five head merges did not break anything. Part B closes PA3 by producing the pack that was verified but never generated.*

## Why now

Five `head_canon` UPDATEs have just rewritten **19,480 invoice rows** across four fiscal years. Every year-on-year figure in the application that groups by head has changed as a result — correctly, but changed. Nothing has been checked since.

Separately, **PA3 verified the Anant Singh payload but never produced a pack.** The zip in circulation still contains a full member report and a forward visit plan for Jagdev, who is marked LEFT. That is the oldest known-wrong artifact outstanding.

For each of the five merged heads, report FY2025-26 and FY2026-27 side by side and confirm the prior year is **no longer zero**. Sandeep Dadheech should show roughly ₹163 Cr against ₹41 Cr, not ₹0.

Confirm the `CROSS_FY_KEY_SPLIT` flag has **cleared** for those five and **remains active** for Pawan Sharma.

### A4. Pawan stays paused — but correct the stated reason

The recorded justification is a 6× customer contraction, 152 → 21. That compares **three fiscal years cumulative against one quarter**. Like-for-like, FY2025-26 to FY2026-27 Q1:

| Head | Customers retained | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Syed Aqil Rizvi | 82% | merged |
| Sandeep Dadheech | 78% | merged |
| Lalan Kumar | 76% | merged |
| Nasir Hussain Khan | 56% | merged |
| **Pawan Sharma** | **47%** | **paused** |
| Biju C.O | **45%** | merged |

Bijju retained less and was merged. **Pawan is not a numerical outlier.**

Keep the pause, but record the real reason: **the surname**. Five pairs are honorific-to-full-name, a spelling variant, or an added surname. `Kumar` to `Sharma` is none of those, and only the business can settle it.

Also note in the record: Pawan Sharma has 11 members of whom **3 are marked LEFT** — the highest departure rate of any head — which would explain a customer drop with no identity question involved.

## What to watch

**A2 is the check that matters most.** The merges rewrote 19,480 rows. If a fiscal-year total has moved even slightly, the UPDATE touched something beyond `head_canon`, and that would be far more serious than the problem it was fixing.

**Do not let the Pawan pause quietly become permanent.** It needs a business answer, not more analysis — the numbers have already been examined and do not settle it.

**The regenerated pack should be compared against the old one field by field**, and every difference should trace to one of the four changes in B1. A difference that traces to none of them is a regression.

## What Prompt 41 A4 found

while `ordered_amount` sums to **₹0.00**. September through March carry the flag
correctly.

So the system currently treats August secondary order booking as a **measured
zero**, not a missing month. That is the empty-versus-unloaded problem, this time
in the source data rather than the display layer.

## Why it may already be live

The head-row denominator shipped this afternoon uses `secondaryPlanRecorded` —
the sum of plan for months where `not_yet_recorded = false`. If August now counts
as recorded, August plan has entered the denominator with zero achievement
against it, and every head's percentage falls.

The Prompt 28 and 30 labels derive their range the same way. They read "Apr–Jul
recorded-month plan" when they shipped. If they now read "Apr–Aug", the change
arrived through data, not code.

## Notes

- Section A is the urgent part. A change shipped this afternoon may already have
  been altered by data that arrived afterwards. If the labels now read Apr–Aug
  and every head's percentage has dropped, that happened without anyone deciding
  it.
- C4 is the one that could bite operationally. If A1 fires on a recorded-but-zero
  August, every member goes below pro-rated target at once, and the alert volume
  would look like a business collapse rather than a missing sheet.
- B3 is the root question. A calendar-driven flag means this recurs on the 1st of
  every month until the sheet is filled — which makes it a standing monthly
  hazard, not an incident.
- Nothing here should be fixed in this prompt. If the flag is wrong, changing it
  changes ten head percentages and possibly the alert volume, and that needs to
  be a decision rather than a correction.

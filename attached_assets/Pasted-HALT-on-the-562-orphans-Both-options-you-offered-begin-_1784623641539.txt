EVERY ORPHAN IN YOUR SAMPLE IS A WATER TANK

  WCT-3LL-05  qty 500      WCT-3LL-10  qty 1000
  WCT-3LL-07  qty 750      WT-3LL-10   qty 1000

WCT / WT prefix, quantities in round hundreds. Those are tanks, measured in
LITRES. Tanks are the ONE product where the DB and the sheet use different
units - the sheet converts pieces to litres via the WT-LTR lookup, and the
identity key is (invoice, code, colour, QTY). If a tank line sits in the DB at
one quantity and in the sheet at another, the identity keys do not match even
though it is the SAME billed line present in both.

That is a far more likely explanation for a 100%-tank orphan set than 562
genuine deletions. And tombstoning a unit mismatch erases a real line - the
overshoot again. The blast-radius guard halted at 14.8% precisely to stop an
unverified removal. Do not override it until these are proven to be real
deletions.

# Replit Prompt 47 — April to July 2026 order booking, from files already held

**Date:** 2 September 2026
**Scope:** Read-only and scoping only. Build nothing, load nothing, persist
nothing.

**Standing rule:** every figure states which database or source it came from.

**Scope: the whole of FY2026-27 to date** — April to July from the PSCode 3
archives, plus 1–19 August from the Product-Wise file. Two source formats, one
fiscal year. The five historical Segment Wise years are a separate decision and
are not in scope here.

## What Prompt 45 established

The PSCode 3 archives carry the **identical header** to the Segment Wise files:

```
Sr.No., Date, Retailer Id, Retailer, Order ID, Segment, Cat. No., Qty, MRP,
Order Value, Distributor, Discount, Sub Total, Order Total
```

And the same `SORD-` order references — April `SORD-144316`, July `SORD-155203`,
against FY2025-26 Segment Wise `SORD-90527`. Same namespace, same booking-line
fields, not a dispatch or invoice reference.

So the old CRM did produce April–July order booking. It was exported per member
rather than assembled into one workbook, and both archives are already in
`attached_assets`.

**The gap is that `secondary_sku_line` has no `order_id` column.** Those 123,326
rows were loaded from these archives with the SORD dropped. The data exists in
the files; it does not exist in the database.

## Notes

- A2 is the check that matters most. Both the SKU rows and a booking load would
  come from the same archives, so they must reconcile exactly. If they do not,
  something in the parse differs and that needs finding before anything is built.
- C1 is what makes April–July materially easier than the historical years. The
  existing identity key — `order_id + product_code + occurrence` — is fully
  available here. The pre-FY2024 Segment Wise files have no Order ID at all, and
  that is the hard part of the historical work.
- Section D is worth asking even though it does not fill the page. The rows are
  already loaded; only the reference was discarded. Recovering it may be smaller
  and useful in its own right — though it touches frozen months, which needs its
  own justification.
- Section 0 is new and it is the one I would read first. The new CRM restarted
  the SORD counter at single digits while the old CRM was at 155,203. The
  identity key carries no era marker. That is a collision waiting to happen.
- B3 is the sharpest design question. Status exists for August and not for
  Apr-Jul. A user filtering to APPROVED would silently lose four months, and a
  wrong result that looks right is worse than a blank page.
- Nothing here should be built off the reply.

## Two things from Prompt 45 worth recording separately

**FY2023-24's Segment Wise workbook covers April 2023 only** — 16,274 rows,
₹10.53 crore, against a full-year primary register of ₹349 crore. That workbook
is not a fiscal year and nothing has said so.

**The FY2025-26 order-booking anchor spans 4 January 2025 to 3 December 2026** —
23 months inside a 12-month workbook — because the reader includes every dated
row to reconcile to the printed total. Distinct order IDs come to 52,515,
matching the configured anchor exactly. So "FY2025-26 order booking: ₹231.09 Cr,
verified" is reconciled to its source but describes a population running eleven
months past the fiscal year end.

Neither is urgent. Both should be written down before someone relies on those
figures as fiscal-year totals.

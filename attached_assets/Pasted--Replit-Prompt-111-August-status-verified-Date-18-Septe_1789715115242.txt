## Notes

- **A3 is the whole prompt.** Two reports have said different things about the same
  table and I want one verified answer with the environment named.
- **B2 matters because the obsolete freeze was found in development and cleared** —
  nobody has confirmed production was clean, only that it "was already correctly
  unfrozen". Worth seeing the value.
- **C4 avoids re-investigating a correct failure.** If August genuinely has no
  rows, the canary firing is right and should be recorded as expected.
- **D3 is the forward-looking part.** August is recoverable whenever the export
  arrives. September is the one where the same gap can still be prevented.

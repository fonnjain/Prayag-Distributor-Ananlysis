## Corrections carried in

**The grain block was mine and it was wrong.** PSCode 3 Sub Total is line-level.
All 123,326 FY2026-27 rows carry non-null `net_amount`, and SORD-155203 proves
it — ₹1,378 and ₹1,225 across two lines summing to the ₹2,603.04 order total.
The v1 comment describes the older consolidated files and does not cover this
source. Prompt 48's scope was sound; only that premise was not.

**Order ID was dropped everywhere.** `secondary_register_line` never mapped it.
`secondary_sku_line` has no column for it, and the loader comment claiming to
carry it is stale. That is why five committed years cannot populate the page.

**The historical years are out of scope.** No Order ID in the database, none at
source for FY2021-22 through FY2023-24, no safe full-FY replace path, and
FY2021-22 and FY2022-23 carry net on only 26.8% and 28.0% of rows. Two of five
could theoretically be re-parsed. That is a separate, larger decision.

**FY2026-27 is unaffected by all of that.** The Apr–Jul archives carry Order ID on
every one of 123,326 rows plus Cat.No, and August is separately validated.

## Four findings from the last report that are not part of this change

These are real and should be recorded, but none blocks FY2026-27.

**The documented re-ingest path is unsafe.** The config says re-ingest requires
moving an entry back to `registers` and rebuilding. But the loader inserts by
`line_uid` with conflict avoidance and **no full-FY delete-and-replace route
exists in production**. Re-running could append new rows while leaving stale ones,
silently inflating a frozen year. The comment does not say so.

**Two years are barely line-level.** `secondary_register_line` carries net on
26.8% of FY2021-22 rows and 28.0% of FY2022-23. Their ₹121.14 Cr and ₹155.59 Cr
come from about a quarter of their rows. FY2024-25 and FY2025-26 are 100%. The
"one v1 format across five years" is not uniform in practice.

**The two loaders disagree on the same source.** FY2023-24 SKU has 26 more rows
and ₹1,56,427 more gross than the register; FY2024-25 has one fewer row and
₹17,200 less. FY2021-22 and FY2022-23 have no SKU rows at all. Gate 2 never
compared the tables — equality was outside its contract.

**The Apr–Jun register gap is a pipeline asymmetry, not a failure.** July's one-off
loader wrote both `secondary_sku_line` and a `pscode3_brand_rollup` mirror in one
transaction. The April–June path wrote only the SKU table. Nothing was lost; the
two paths simply differ. That closes the question Prompt 12 raised.

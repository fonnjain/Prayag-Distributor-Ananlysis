GOAL
Prayag's team must be able to correct any assignment: move a salesperson to a
different head, change a head's territories, change who owns a customer, and
mark anything unassigned. Selecting a person opens a page offering the choices
that apply to THAT person's role.

============================================================
THE RULE THAT MAKES THIS SAFE
============================================================
Coverage is DERIVED from register evidence today. Editing must not silently
replace that.

  - Derived stays the default and stays visible
  - A person may OVERRIDE it. The override is recorded with who, when and why
  - Both are shown: "Register evidence: Lalan Kumar, 29 customers" beside
    "Set by Nishant, 20 Aug: Rizvi"
  - A divergence is a stated decision, never an accident

Every change is EFFECTIVE-DATED from the date of the decision. Historical
attribution never moves. FY2025-26 by head must read the same before and after
any edit made through this system - that is the stop condition on every phase.

============================================================
PHASE 1 - THE PERSON PAGE, ROLE-AWARE
============================================================
Selecting anyone opens a detail page. What it offers depends on who they are.

IF THEY ARE A STATE HEAD
  Territories        multi-select from state_hierarchy. The dropdown ALWAYS
                     shows split leaves - UP ( A ), UP (AS), UTTAR PRADESH,
                     DELHI A, DELHI NCR, JAMMU, KASHMIR, KARNATAKA (B),
                     RAJASTHAN (N) - indented under their parent. Never
                     collapse a split into its parent.
  Their team         add or remove people; move a person to a different head
  Their customers    view, with a route into bulk reassignment
  Reports to         a more senior person, or none

IF THEY ARE A MEMBER
  State Head         dropdown of all heads, PLUS "Unassigned"
  Reporting manager  dropdown of any active person, PLUS "None"
  Designation        dropdown, plus add-new
  Territories        same split-aware picker
  Their customers    view and reassign

"UNASSIGNED" IS A REAL OPTION EVERYWHERE
Not a blank. A selectable sentinel that renders as "Unassigned" and is
countable. Reuse the Unassigned coverage sentinel already built for the
null-head register buckets. A person or customer parked as Unassigned appears
in the unassigned queue by design, not by omission.

MOVING A PERSON
Mandatory impact preview before saving:
    "Moving X from Head A to Head B.
     Affects N people below them and M customers.
     Historical attribution before <date> is unchanged."
Nothing saves until confirmed. This already exists for deactivation - reuse it.

============================================================
PHASE 2 - THE CUSTOMER PAGE
============================================================
Selecting a customer offers:
  Type            distributor · direct_dealer · retailer · sub_dealer ·
                  project · govt · other
  Salesperson     any active person, PLUS "Unassigned"
  State Head      any head, PLUS "Unassigned"
  Territory       the same split-aware state picker
  Distributor     for a retailer: add or remove links, many allowed

TYPE CHANGES NEED A REASON
Changing a customer from distributor to project is what broke year-on-year
comparison before - Rs 35.73 Cr read as a total collapse. Require a reason,
record it in change_log.reason with the operator in changed_by, and
effective-date it.

============================================================
PHASE 3 - ADDING AND UNASSIGNING
============================================================
  Add a person       creates an active person. Employee code OPTIONAL - 62 of
                     179 have implausible codes and it must never be the key.
  Add a customer     goes to the Review Queue as pending, not straight to the
                     master. Keep that gate.
  Unassign          available on both, from the same dropdown. Sets the
                     sentinel, dates it, logs it.
  Bulk              select several customers, reassign in one transaction with
                     one impact preview and one change_log row per customer.

============================================================
NON-NEGOTIABLE ON EVERY PHASE
============================================================
- Effective dating on every assignment change
- Impact preview before anything affecting more than one record
- change_log per edit: operator in changed_by, reason in reason, SEPARATE
  columns. Never overload one into the other.
- Admin gate, matching the People tab
- Never delete a person or customer. Deactivate or unassign only.
- The state dropdown always shows splits, on every screen

============================================================
VERIFY - each phase, stop and report
============================================================
1. Move a member from one head to another. Paste the impact preview. Confirm
   the counts are right.
2. Print FY2025-26 net by head BEFORE and AFTER that move. Identical.
   Rs 3,609,953,808.51. If any head moves, stop.
3. Add a territory to a State Head from the dropdown. Confirm the picker shows
   UP ( A ), UP (AS) and UTTAR PRADESH as three separate selectable leaves.
4. Confirm an overridden coverage row shows BOTH the derived value and the
   override, with who and when.
5. Set a person to Unassigned. Confirm they appear in the unassigned queue and
   are countable, not merely blank.
6. Change a customer's type. Confirm a reason is required and lands in
   change_log.reason with the operator in changed_by.
7. Bulk-reassign 5 customers. Confirm one impact preview and five change_log
   rows.
8. Add a person with no employee code. Confirm it saves.
9. Confirm sale_line, margin_fact and mrp tables unchanged.
10. Push, print both refs.

    (party/secondary OB + direct-dealer OB) / elapsed months x 12 and

## Notes

- **Section A first, without exception.** A correct Batch 4 change tested in
  development would show every rupee as Unmapped. Somebody would revert a working
  change.
- **A4 is the durable fix.** Five false findings in one month from environment
  divergence is a pattern. A parity check that reports rather than passes silently
  would have caught all five.
- **B2 is worth doing even alone.** A projection whose numerator includes
  direct-dealer booking compared against a plan labelled secondary is wrong before
  seasonality enters.
- **C1's recommendation is to label, not reconcile.** Three populations forced into
  one number would hide a real measurement difference. Naming the sources is
  honest; making them add up would not be.

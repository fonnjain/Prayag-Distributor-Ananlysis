## Notes

- Section A is the one that matters. Two heads each holding half a state, with
  neither page showing any sign of it, is worse than a wrong number because it
  looks correct from every angle.
- Section B is the same principle you already applied to the Growth cards. A head
  seeing 0 distributors reads it as "I have none", not "we cannot count them".
- Section D3 is worth reading alongside the is_territory work. A university
  account with no DIST# is exactly the shape of an institutional customer, and
  the two problems may share a cause.
- Do not let Section E lead to a canonicalisation change. It is a question for
  Prayag first, and it is in the note going to them.

SCHEMA
============================================================
alert_recipient
  id · name · escalation_level (1|2|3)
  scope_type (state_head | all_india) · scope_value (person_id or null)
  alert_code_pattern ('A*' | 'B*' | 'C*' | 'S*' | 'B3' | '*')
  channel (whatsapp | email | in_app) · contact · cadence (on_raise | weekly)
  is_active

alert_delivery
  alert_id · recipient_id · channel · escalation_level
  sent_at · delivered_at · opened_at
  status (pending | sent | failed | skipped) · skip_reason

alert_escalation_config
  level · window_days     defaults: L1->L2 after 7 days severe / 14 digest
                                    L2->L3 after 7 days

ESCALATION BEHAVIOUR
Level 1 receives on raise or in the digest. If NOT ACKNOWLEDGED within the
window, level 2 receives it with the original raise date and days open. If
still unacknowledged, level 3.

Escalating never removes it from the level below. Everyone holds it until it
clears.

With level 2 blank, an unacknowledged alert must SKIP level 2 with a logged
reason and go to level 3. Do not stall silently at an empty level.

============================================================
CHANNEL - WHATSAPP IS THE TARGET, NOT YET CONFIGURED
============================================================
Build the channel as an interface with three implementations:
  in_app     works now - notification bell and unread count
  email      works now - digest and on-raise
  whatsapp   INTERFACE ONLY. Store the number, render the message body, write
             the delivery row with status = pending and skip_reason = "no
             WhatsApp provider configured".

Do NOT integrate a provider. When one is chosen it drops in behind the same
interface with no change to rules, recipients or the digest.

A whatsapp recipient must remain VISIBLE in the delivery log. If those rows
were silently skipped, nobody would notice for weeks that the channel everyone
assumed was working had never sent anything.

============================================================
THE DIGEST
============================================================
Weekly, one message per recipient, covering their scope:
  NEW since the last digest
  STILL OPEN, with periods open
  CLEARED since the last digest
  ESCALATING to them from a level below

If a recipient has nothing, send nothing. An empty digest teaches people to
ignore the next one.

Generate from STORED alerts, never recompute. The digest must match the page
exactly - if the page says 33 and the digest says 31, nobody trusts either.

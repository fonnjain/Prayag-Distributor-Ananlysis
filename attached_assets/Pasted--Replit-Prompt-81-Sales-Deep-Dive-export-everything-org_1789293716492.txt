## Notes

- **A1 and A2 are real defects, not formatting.** A date shown as ₹41,004, and two
  cost ratios a hundred times apart on the same page. Both would export as they
  stand.
- **A4 matters because those fields are already visible to users.** `DIFFERENCEIJCZ`
  and `TARGETCALCULATEDBGBF` are internal column names with generated suffixes.
  Either they mean something and deserve a label, or they should not be on the
  page at all.
- **Sheet 1 is the point of the whole request.** Everything else is organisation.
  The summary answers five questions and shows both sides of every percentage —
  which is the fix for the recurring problem of a figure that cannot be
  reconciled from what is beside it.
- **A5 is the honest exception.** Segment spread runs on brand segments from the
  secondary register, which has no item code and cannot join the registry. That is
  a real constraint, not an oversight — and the sheet should say so rather than
  look inconsistent.

## One thing the page already does well

The banner *"FY2026-27 secondary register covers Jul-26–Jul-26 (1 month) only;
later months are not loaded yet"* is exactly right. It states the limitation
rather than showing a small number without explanation. Carry that wording into
the export.

## The bigger finding from Prompt 57, not part of this change

**Three real weekly digests ran — 17, 24 and 31 August. 110 email attempts, all
failed. 110 WhatsApp attempts, all pending. Zero messages delivered.**

The scheduler works. Recipients are configured — 13 WhatsApp routes, an all-India
email route, four on-raise routes. The providers are not: `RESEND_API_KEY` and
`SMTP_HOST` are both unset.

So ten A1 alerts covering **₹3.44 crore of shortfall** are open, unacknowledged,
and have never reached a person.

**And the sequencing was fortunate.** Had delivery been configured before
yesterday, those three digests would have carried the 114 false August A2 alerts
to the CEO. The gap that looks like a failure prevented a worse one. Now that the
arrears fix is live and the false alerts are cleared, connecting delivery is safe
— and overdue.

# M1 — Momentum page rebuild

*The page currently shows levels. Momentum is about rate, direction and what to do about it.*

---

## The problem in one line

**₹111.98 Cr is a level, not momentum.** A bar chart of monthly order value is monthly totals, not direction. Neither tells anyone whether the business is accelerating, decelerating, or simply following its own seasonal shape.

## What the page should be saying today

| | |
|---|---|
| Apr–Jul FY2026-27 | ₹101.31 Cr |
| Apr–Jul FY2025-26 | ₹100.89 Cr |
| Nominal | **+0.4%** |
| **Real** (index 1.069) | **−6.1%** |

And the two years before it: FY2024-25 **−8.6% real**, FY2025-26 **−1.0% real**.

**Three consecutive years of real decline.** That is the headline this page should lead with, and nothing on it currently says so.

## The flag I would build first

**Falling from high.** Every alert in this app is threshold-based — the Warning System fires below 50%, low performers below 50%.

**A member who has gone from 85% to 70% triggers nothing.** They are above every line, and they are the most actionable person in the company because the decline is early and reversible.

Nothing in the app can currently see them. That single flag would justify the page on its own.

---

## And the honest headline

Three years of real decline is uncomfortable, and it is what the data says. **A momentum page that shows ₹111.98 Cr and a rising bar chart is telling the opposite story** — the bars rise because prices rose, not because more product moved.

If this page is going to inform decisions, that is the first thing it has to say.

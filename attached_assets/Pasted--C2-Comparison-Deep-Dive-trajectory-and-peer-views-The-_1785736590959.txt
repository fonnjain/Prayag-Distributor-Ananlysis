# C2 — Comparison Deep Dive, trajectory and peer views

*The contract is proven. This puts a screen on it — for the two modes people actually use.*

---

## What C1 established, and must not be weakened

Twelve guards, verified with live responses. **C2 adds no logic.** It renders what `/api/comparison` returns, including its refusals.

**The `channelLabel` sits above the data, not below it.** Territory-only figures are legitimately different from all-channel figures on other pages, and that line is the only thing preventing the next investigation.

## The design point worth holding

**A refusal is a result.** The instinct will be to style a block as an error — red, apologetic, something to be got past. It should read like a finding: *this comparison would mislead, here is why, here is what you could compare instead.*

That framing is what makes people trust the numbers that do render.

## `Sheet11` — a scratch tab that would corrupt the total

It has **completely different columns**: `Bill From`, `Item Code`, `Quantity`, `Taxable Value`, `GROUP`, `MAIN GROUP`, **`WEIGHT`, `TOTAL WEIGHT`** — plus a second block of `GROUP / QTY / VALUE / WEIGHT`.

**No invoice number. No date. No month column.** It is somebody's weight and tonnage working area.

> **A loader that sums every tab would silently add ₹26.77 Cr of non-sales data** — a 20% overstatement. This is exactly the case for detecting new tabs and refusing to guess.

## Why detection matters more than the August load

August itself is routine — the tabs already exist and the nightly replace should pick them up.

**`Sheet11` is the reason to build detection now.** It appeared without warning, holds a plausible-looking `Taxable Value` column, and would inflate the year by a fifth if summed. Nobody would have caught it except by noticing the total looked wrong — which is how the ₹33 Cr July inflation was found, several days late.

**A tab that is new should be reported, not read.**

## B — the YTD decision, now with figures

Two problems in one: YTD is applied across sources that have different data, and it is offered on years where it has no meaning.

```
Prayag app - resolve YTD properly. Same global rules. Two separate faults.

FAULT 1 - YTD IS OFFERED ON CLOSED FISCAL YEARS, WHERE IT MEANS NOTHING.
"Year to date" only has meaning for the CURRENT fiscal year. FY2023-24,
FY2024-25 and FY2025-26 are complete - for them, year to date IS the full year.

CHECK FIRST, AND REPORT: what does the app currently return for FY2024-25 with
YTD selected? If the resolver computes "April to the current calendar month" and
applies that range to whichever FY is chosen, a closed year is being shown as a
QUARTER of itself with nothing to indicate why. FY2024-25 would read roughly
Rs 85 Cr instead of Rs 341.14 Cr.

FIX: for any fiscal year that is not the current one, either hide the YTD option
or make it resolve to Full Year and label it as such. Never let a closed year
return a partial range.

  FY2023-24 + YTD  ->  Rs 349.02 Cr   (= Full Year)
  FY2024-25 + YTD  ->  Rs 341.14 Cr   (= Full Year)
  FY2025-26 + YTD  ->  Rs 359.52 Cr   (= Full Year)
  FY2026-27 + YTD  ->  Apr to latest data

The same applies to any quarter beyond a closed year's data and to the
"Last 7 days" and "Today" shortcuts, which are meaningless on a closed year.

FAULT 2 - YTD ON THE CURRENT YEAR IS TRUNCATED TO THE SECONDARY CUTOFF.
It resolves to Apr-Jun everywhere, because June is the last complete SECONDARY
month. That range is then applied to the PRIMARY cards, which do have July. The
two pages now disagree:

   Overview     Orders YTD              Rs 102.25 Cr   (Apr-Jul)
   State Head   Order Booking (Primary) Rs  77.76 Cr   (Apr-Jun)
   difference   Rs 24.49 Cr - exactly July's Rs 24.50 Cr

Adding July to the secondary side is NOT the fix. July carries a Rs 26.57 Cr plan
and no recorded actuals, so including it would drop achievement from 76.2 per
cent to 57.6 per cent - a 19-point fall caused by a month with no data.

So YTD must resolve PER SOURCE for the current year:

   PRIMARY cards    Apr-Jul   sale_line and the Order Sheet both hold July
     Sale (Dispatched)       Rs 90.16 Cr
     Order Booking (Primary) Rs 102.26 Cr
     Pending                 Rs 12.10 Cr
   SECONDARY cards  Apr-Jun   last month with recorded actuals
     Target Rs 82.65 Cr, Sales Received Rs 62.95 Cr, Achievement 76.2 per cent

Label each card with the range it actually covers. Every card already names its
source, so two ranges can sit on one page honestly. What cannot happen is the
primary cards being silently truncated to the secondary cutoff, or the two pages
disagreeing on the same measure.

Acceptance:
  report what FY2024-25 + YTD returns TODAY, before any change
  after the fix, a closed FY with YTD returns its Full Year total
  FY2024-25 Rs 341.14 Cr, FY2025-26 Rs 359.52 Cr, FY2023-24 Rs 349.02 Cr
  State Head primary cards read Rs 90.16 Cr and Rs 102.26 Cr on current-year YTD
  Overview and State Head agree on Orders YTD
  secondary cards stay at Apr-Jun and say so
  every card shows the range it covers
```

# YTD belongs to the current fiscal year only

*And it must move on its own when the fiscal year turns, with no code change.*

---

```
Prayag app - restrict YTD to the current fiscal year, derived from today's date.

"Year to date" only has meaning for the fiscal year currently in progress. For a
closed year, year to date IS the full year - so offering it either misleads or
duplicates Full Year.

1. DERIVE THE CURRENT FY FROM TODAY'S DATE. Do not hardcode it, do not read it
   from config, do not maintain a list of closed years.
   Indian fiscal year runs 1 April to 31 March, so:
      on 31 Jul 2026  -> current FY is 2026-27
      on 31 Mar 2027  -> current FY is still 2026-27
      on  1 Apr 2027  -> current FY becomes 2027-28

   This is the whole point of the change. When the year turns, FY2026-27 must
   lose YTD automatically. Nobody should have to remember to edit anything.

2. YTD IS OFFERED ONLY FOR THE CURRENT FY.
   For any other fiscal year, hide the YTD control. If a stored URL or saved view
   still carries ytd for a closed year, resolve it to Full Year and label it
   "Full Year" so the figure shown matches the label.

   REPORT WHAT IT DOES TODAY BEFORE CHANGING ANYTHING. If YTD currently resolves
   as "April to the current calendar month" and applies that to whichever FY is
   selected, then a closed year is being shown as a QUARTER of itself right now,
   with nothing on screen to say so. FY2024-25 would read roughly Rs 85 Cr
   instead of Rs 341.14 Cr.

3. THE SAME APPLIES TO "Last 7 days" AND "Today".
   Both are meaningless on a closed year - they would return nothing, or worse,
   silently return a range that falls outside the year entirely. Hide them
   whenever the selected FY is not the current one.

4. QUARTERS BEYOND THE CURRENT ONE, WITHIN THE CURRENT FY.
   Q3 and Q4 of FY2026-27 have not happened. They carry a plan but no actuals.
   They must read "no actuals recorded yet" rather than 0 per cent, and must not
   contribute a low-performer count. Same rule already agreed for July.

5. A FUTURE FY, if selectable at all, shows "no data for this fiscal year"
   rather than zeros.

Acceptance - report each:
  what FY2024-25 + YTD returns TODAY, before any change
  after the change, YTD is not offered for FY2023-24, FY2024-25 or FY2025-26
  a saved ytd URL for a closed FY resolves to Full Year and is labelled so:
     FY2024-25   Rs 341.14 Cr
     FY2025-26   Rs 361.00 Cr
     FY2023-24   its frozen total
  FY2026-27 + YTD still resolves to April through the latest data, unchanged
  "Last 7 days" and "Today" are hidden on any FY that is not current
  Q3 and Q4 of FY2026-27 read "no actuals recorded yet", not 0 per cent

  THE AUTOMATIC TEST - this is the one that matters:
  simulate today's date as 1 April 2027 and confirm, with NO code edit:
     FY2027-28 becomes the current year and offers YTD
     FY2026-27 no longer offers YTD and resolves it to Full Year
  If that requires touching a config file or a list, the rule is not derived
  from the date and the change is not complete.
```

---

## Why the automatic test is the acceptance criterion

Everything else here is a one-off correction. **The date-derived rule is the part that keeps working.**

A hardcoded list of closed years would pass every check today and quietly break on 1 April 2027 — showing YTD on a year that has ended, on a dashboard nobody would think to re-verify. That is the same shape as several faults already found in this project: correct when written, wrong later, and silent about it.

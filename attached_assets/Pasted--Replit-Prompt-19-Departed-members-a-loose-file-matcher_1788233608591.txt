**Date:** 1 September 2026
**Scope:** Read-only. No code change, no connection, no load, no rename.

## Notes

- A2 is the question that matters. Year-to-date accumulation that stops at a
  leaving date is normal. Figures continuing after someone left is not, and the
  snapshot cannot tell the two apart.
- A6 could collapse two open investigations into one. If the 58 unmatched names
  and the leaver population overlap heavily, the ₹13.51 crore is a roster-history
  problem rather than unidentified money.
- B2 is a hazard rather than a current fault. The folder holds five files, none
  for 2026-27, so nothing is being mismatched today. But the matcher would accept
  a draft dropped in by anyone.
- Section C is small and cheap, and worth doing before the pack reaches Prayag —
  if we ask them about sources while our own screen misreports two of them, that
  is confusing for everyone.
- D3 is the useful part of Section D. Whatever the label says, we should know
  which sheets are actually being read for the closed years.

## Notes

- Question 2 first, and it is probably the answer. `sale_line` is a current-only
  view while `sale_line_all` holds versions; a predicate on `state_raw` rather
  than `state_canon` would also give a different population. A methodology
  difference between two reports is far more likely than a frozen year losing
  rows.
- Question 4 is the one that matters if question 2 comes back clean. A frozen
  year that can lose 2,094 rows without a guard firing is a much larger problem
  than the rows.
- The instruction not to restore is deliberate. The standing pattern in this
  project is that discrepancies get resolved by removing or superseding data
  rather than preserving both versions and escalating. If rows have gone, the
  evidence of how needs to survive the investigation.

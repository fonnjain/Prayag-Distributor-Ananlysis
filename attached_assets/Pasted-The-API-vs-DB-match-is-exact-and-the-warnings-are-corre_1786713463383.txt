The API-vs-DB match is exact and the warnings are correctly advisory. But
your last paragraph describes a defect, not a design note.

THE PROBLEM
secondary_register_line has no item_code, so the lookup answers "has this
customer bought ANYTHING from us in 12 months" - but the warning text says
"this ITEM may belong in Tab 2".

Consequence: Ayan Enterprise has 415 lines across 11 months, so Tab 2 will
warn on EVERY item code - including ones he has genuinely never bought, which
is precisely what Tab 2 exists to record. The warning fires on the correct
answer.

Tab 1 only warns for customers who have bought nothing at all, which the tab
already knows.

Both warnings are inverted in practice. A salesperson using Tab 2 correctly
gets told they are wrong every time, which is the fastest way to train people
to ignore a warning.

REPORT FIRST, do not change anything yet
  a. Does ANY table hold retailer-level purchases at ITEM CODE granularity?
     Check secondary_sku_line - it carries item_code and retailer. Print its
     columns and confirm whether a retailer + item_code lookup is possible.
  b. If it is, print for RET#9690 (Ayan Enterprise) the count of distinct item
     codes bought in the last 12 months, and whether CP-101 is among them.
  c. If secondary_sku_line does not link to a named retailer, say so plainly.

THEN, depending on (a):

IF item-level data exists:
  Repoint the lookup at it. The warning then means what it says. Keep the
  customer-level count as secondary context - "bought from us in 9 of 12
  months, but never this item" is a more useful sentence than either fact
  alone.

IF it does not:
  Change the WORDS to match the check. Drop the tab recommendation entirely.
  Replace with a neutral statement:
      "This customer purchased from Prayag in 11 of the last 12 months
       (415 lines). Item-level history is not available."
  A warning that cannot distinguish the two tabs must not recommend a tab.

Either way, print the new text verbatim before you ship it.

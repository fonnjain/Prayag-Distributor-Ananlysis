**Date:** 31 August 2026
**Adds to:** Prompts 1–12
**Scope:** Read-only. No code change, no config change, no data change, no load,
no anchor added.

## Notes

- Section A is the one that matters most. A green verification badge that covers
  only a frozen year is worse than no badge, because it reads as assurance about
  the live year.
- Section D could close one of the three red workflows cheaply. If
  `costRatioSales` fails because CTC was never connected, the guard is behaving
  correctly and nothing needs fixing in the guard. D5 is there because the
  tempting move is to adjust the expectation until it goes green, and that would
  destroy the signal.
- Section C4 connects to the Red Alert completeness question you have been
  carrying since the calibration work. Empty targets may account for some of the
  nine inert categories.
- Nothing here should result in an FY2026-27 anchor being written off the back of
  the reply. An anchor invented from the app's own figures verifies nothing — it
  needs an external reference, which is what A4 is asking about.

## Credit worth noting

The SFA and CTC sources report themselves as not connected and name exactly which
columns stay blank as a result — visits, working days, GPS kilometres, lead
counters, CTC, TA bills, cost ratio. That is the pattern the six surfaces in
Prompt 12 lack, and it already exists in this codebase.

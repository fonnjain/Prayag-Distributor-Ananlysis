## Notes

- **Prompt A question 6** and **Prompt B section A3** are the same question from
  two directions: is any year-on-year comparison being computed against a
  baseline that is materially incomplete? One asks about missing states, the
  other about a missing monthly table. Either would produce movement that looks
  like performance and is not.
- **Prompt B question B2** is the whole of section B in one ask. Six members' raw
  cells side by side should show immediately whether the cumulative column is a
  formula or hand-typed. If it is typed by team members, the ₹10.51 crore is not
  a defect and the question becomes which number anyone should be reading.
- Both prompts forbid backfilling, deliberately. FY2025-26 is frozen, and the
  reflex on both findings will be to fill in what is missing.

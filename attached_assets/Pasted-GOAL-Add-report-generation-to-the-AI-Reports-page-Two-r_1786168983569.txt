GOAL
Add report generation to the AI Reports page. Two report types, one
pipeline. The user picks a type, picks a subject, picks a period, and
gets a rendered report they can read on screen and download.

These are NOT the same report at two scopes. A distributor report names
items and retailers. A state-head report names distributors and people.
Do not build one template with a scope switch - the output is padded and
unreadable at one end or the other.

REPORT TYPE 1 - DISTRIBUTOR
Sections in this fixed order. Every section states its own period.

  1  Headline        net, quantity, realised per piece, codes bought,
                     active retailers - all vs the SAME months prior FY
  2  Five levers     Activate / Widen / Recover / Read / Close, each with
                     its current position and a priority of High, Medium,
                     Low or None
  3  Reach           retailers on book, active, activation %, unassigned
                     count, top-5 retailer share, effective retailers
  4  Range           per segment: net, share, codes he buys, peer median,
                     gap. Then the peer group definition and his rank.
                     Then tier counts. Then the top 8 recommendations with
                     peers-buying and peer median quarterly net.
  5  Recovery        At Risk retailer count and their prior-year value,
                     lost codes, reactivated
  6  Rhythm          orders this period, orders per month, days since last
                     order, both years
  7  Scheme          billed, current slab and rate, next slab and rate,
                     gap, Extra Earn, Extra ROI, and the split of Extra
                     Earn into new-purchase and re-pricing components
  8  Tier            score with all four components and their basis, plus
                     the recommended cadence
  9  What to do      This week, and an ordered visit plan
  10 Not available   what is missing and why

REPORT TYPE 2 - STATE HEAD
  1  Headline        team net, quantity, achievement against target,
                     active member count
  2  Team table      one row per member: net, growth, retailers declared
                     vs sheet, achievement. Name the source of each count.
  3  Coverage        distributors, retailers, districts with no distributor,
                     unassigned retailers by district
  4  Distributor mix distributors by tier, concentration flags above 60%,
                     the largest single-customer dependency in the state
  5  Range           state-level codes bought vs the national peer picture,
                     and the five distributors with the widest range gaps
                     BY VALUE - named
  6  Attention       hidden shrinkers in the state (value up, quantity
                     down), narrowers, and distributors silent 6+ weeks -
                     all named
  7  Schemes         live nudges in the state ranked by Extra Earn with
                     Extra ROI beside each, and total gap to close
  8  What to do      ranked actions with names attached
  9  Not available   what is missing and why

HARD RULES - these are why the reports are trusted
- Every figure names its period. Comparisons are the SAME months in the
  prior FY, never a rolling window and never a different length.
- Where a count has two sources (retailers declared vs retailers from
  sheets), print BOTH and label each. Never silently pick one.
- Never print 0 for a missing value. Print the reason: "no target
  recorded", "not recorded yet", "no prior year". A zero reads as a fact.
- Territory figures exclude the project channel. State that in the header
  of any section carrying a territory figure.
- No margin, profit or cost-derived figure anywhere. It does not exist in
  the system. Section 10 / 9 must say so explicitly.
- Two decimals, truncated not rounded, using the existing trunc helper.
  The shift or delta columns must be computed from the SAME truncated
  values that are displayed, so the printed numbers reconcile.
- No emojis.

GENERATION
- The narrative sentences (headline reading, lever priorities, the visit
  plan) may be model-generated. Every NUMBER must come from the existing
  query layer - never from the model. Pass computed figures in and have
  the model write around them.
- If a section has no data, render the section with its reason line
  rather than omitting it. A missing section reads as an oversight.

OUTPUT
- Render on screen first.
- Download as PDF. If the user also asks for slides, produce a PPTX.
- When more than one file is produced, deliver a single ZIP.
- Filename pattern: Subject_Period_ReportType, no spaces.

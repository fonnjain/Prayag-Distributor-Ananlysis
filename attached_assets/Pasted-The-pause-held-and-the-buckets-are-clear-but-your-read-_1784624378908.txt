THE B1 RATIOS ARE CONSISTENT - YOU DIVIDED THE WRONG WAY

You called 2x, 3x, 10x, 15x, 19x "inconsistent, rules out a single conversion."
It is not a conversion at all - it is the PIECE COUNT, and it varies per line by
nature. Watch:

  DB qty = litres of ONE tank (from the WT-LTR size code)
  Sheet qty = TOTAL litres across all tanks on that line
  sheet / DB = number of tanks on the line

  WCT-3LL-05  DB 500   sheet 9,500   = 500 x 19 tanks
  WCT-3LL-10  DB 1,000 sheet 15,000  = 1,000 x 15 tanks
  WCT-3LL-07  DB 750   sheet 1,500   = 750 x 2 tanks
  WT-3LL-10   DB 1,000 sheet 10,000  = 1,000 x 10 tanks
  WT-3LL-20   DB 2,000 sheet 6,000   = 2,000 x 3 tanks
  WT-3LL-02   DB 200   sheet 600     = 200 x 3 tanks

Every single one is size-litres x an integer piece count. The rule is uniform:
the DB stored litres-per-tank; the sheet stores total-litres-dispatched. The DB
is UNDER-representing tank quantity. The sheet is correct - it is the real
volume dispatched.

So these 286 are the SAME billed lines, present in both. They are a
unit-representation bug, not deletions. The fix is to re-match on
(invoice, code, colour) and ADOPT THE SHEET QTY. No tombstone.

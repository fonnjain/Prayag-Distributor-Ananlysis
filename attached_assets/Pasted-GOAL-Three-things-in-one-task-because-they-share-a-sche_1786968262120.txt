GOAL
Three things in one task, because they share a schema change:
  1. Capture more detail on the entry form - in values, not tick-boxes
  2. A Submissions tab
  3. An Analytics tab

The existing three entry tabs (Existing SKU / New SKU / New Customer) keep
their structure. This adds fields, not steps.

============================================================
1 - RICHER CAPTURE
============================================================

THE PRINCIPLE: EVERYTHING NEW IS OPTIONAL
Required stays: retailer, segment, competitor brand, net price.

A salesperson standing in a shop with a phone abandons a form that demands
fifteen fields. Optional fields improve the record when they are available and
never block a submission when they are not.

PRIORITY SET - visible by default, under section 4
  credit_days_competitor      integer. How many days the competitor gives.
  credit_given_by             distributor | competitor_company | unknown
                              A distributor funding 60 days from his own
                              working capital is a different problem from a
                              competitor funding it centrally. The first is a
                              service gap; the second is a structural cost.
  credit_days_prayag          integer. What we give the same retailer.
                              The COMPARISON is the finding, not either number.

  competitor_scheme_type      percentage | free_goods | slab | none | unknown
  competitor_scheme_value     text - "6%", "10+1 free", "5% above Rs 2L"
                              "Better scheme" is unactionable. "6% against our
                              5%" can be answered.

  delivery_days_competitor    integer
  delivery_days_prayag        integer
                              Availability is usually about SPEED, not stock.
                              Two days against seven is a logistics answer, not
                              a pricing one, and the cheapest gap to close.

  shelf_share                 mostly_prayag | even_split | mostly_competitor |
                              only_competitor
                              With monthly volume this turns a survey into a
                              sized opportunity: "Rs 40,000 a month in CPVC,
                              80% going elsewhere."

MORE DETAIL - collapsed by default, expands on click
  payment_terms_note          text - cash discount, part-payment, cheque cycle
  competitor_visit_frequency  weekly | fortnightly | monthly | rarely | never
                              Sometimes the answer is not price at all.
  competitor_moq              text - a competitor accepting smaller orders is a
                              real advantage for a small shop and costs nothing
                              to match
  buying_since                text or year - six months is recoverable, six
                              years usually is not, and it changes whether to
                              spend time there
  would_switch                yes | no | maybe | unknown
  switch_condition            free text - "what would it take?"
                              The most useful sentence on the form and the one
                              nobody thinks to ask.

DO NOT ADD
  - The competitor's own MRP. Retailers rarely know it accurately and a
    guessed number becomes data. The net price he pays is the honest figure.
  - Anything about the end consumer. Out of scope, deliberately.

Every new field is nullable. Existing rows keep NULL and must render as
"not recorded", never as zero.

============================================================
2 - SUBMISSIONS TAB
============================================================
Everything recorded, grouped by survey type in this order:
  Existing customer + existing SKU · Existing customer + new SKU ·
  New customer · Unclassified (pre-rebuild rows)

Group header shows the count.

Columns: submitted date · recorder (labelled SELF-DECLARED, it is not
authenticated) · retailer, marked where it is a pending prospect · state ·
district · segment · Prayag code · our MRP at the time · competitor brand ·
competitor product · net price · unit · pack size · entry mode · the priority
fields above · reasons · monthly volume · note

MULTI-LINE SURVEYS
Rows sharing a survey_id were one visit. Group them under one retailer heading
rather than repeating the retailer per line.

Filters: state head, state, segment, competitor brand, recorder, date range.
Export to xlsx.

A row stays editable by its recorder for 24 hours, then locks. Show the state.

============================================================
3 - ANALYTICS TAB
============================================================
Read-only, computed from stored survey rows only.

THE SAMPLE-SIZE RULE - build this first, it governs every figure
Every number carries n. Below n=5, label INDICATIVE ONLY and render it visibly
weaker. Never show a median from one or two responses as a market price.

There are 7 surveys in the system today. Almost everything will start below
n=5 and the tab SHOULD look sparse until the field has done the work. Do not
pad it.

PANELS

  1. PER ITEM CODE
     our MRP · surveyed competitor net, median · n · spread (min-max) · gap %

  2. BY COMPETITOR BRAND
     surveys · segments · states · price range · median net
     Which brands are actually met in the field, not which are in the
     competition database.

  3. WHY RETAILERS BUY ELSEWHERE
     Reason counts, overall and by segment. The most actionable panel here -
     if credit terms outrank price in a state, that is a different
     conversation from a pricing one.

  4. CREDIT COMPARISON            (new)
     Median competitor credit days vs median Prayag credit days, by state and
     by segment, with n. Plus the split of credit_given_by - distributor
     versus competitor company.

  5. SCHEME COMPARISON            (new)
     Competitor scheme values recorded, grouped by type. Where the retailer's
     distributor has a live Prayag scheme, show our Extra Earn beside it.
     Do not compute a winner - show both and let a person read it.

  6. DELIVERY SPEED               (new)
     Distribution of competitor delivery days against ours, by state. Median
     and range.

  7. SIZED OPPORTUNITY            (new)
     From shelf_share and monthly volume: estimated monthly value going to
     competitors, by segment and by state. State the assumption on the panel -
     it rests on a rough share band and a recalled volume, and both are
     estimates.

  8. COVERAGE GAPS
     Segments and states below n=5, named. A salesperson should see where to
     go next.

  9. NEW SKU OPPORTUNITY
     From survey_type = new_sku: Prayag codes being bought from a competitor
     by customers who already buy from us, with the retailers named. A push
     list from field evidence rather than peer inference.

 10. COMPETITOR PRICE VS OURS, WHERE BOTH EXIST
     Show the competition-app figure labelled DERIVED - it is MRP less an
     assumed 40%, not observed. The survey figure is observed. Never present
     them as equivalent.

============================================================
DO NOT
============================================================
- Do not feed any survey figure into the MRP calculator or any pricing
  recommendation. That remains separate and reviewed.
- Do not compute a median, average or gap without n beside it.
- Do not make any new field required.
- Do not write to customer_master, mrp_history, margin_fact or sale_line.

Phase D7 - tiering, cadence and credit terms, and two-way concentration
PROMPT - Phase D7
Prayag app - Distributor Deep Dive, Phase D7. Same global rules. Send after D4;
this replaces and extends the tiering section of D4.

Goal: turn the analysis into an action, and expose dependency risk in both
directions.

1. Distributor tiering A / B / C. Classify from NET, growth against the same
   period last year, retailer breadth (active retailers and districts covered),
   and effective discount. Show every input behind a tier so the classification
   can be argued with, and allow a manual override that is recorded with a reason
   rather than silently applied.

2. Each tier must carry an ACTION, not just a label:
     visit cadence      - visits per month for the distributor and for its
                          retailers, feeding the Sales Deep Dive visit plan
     credit posture     - recommended terms and exposure limit
     range focus        - which whitespace segments to push first, from D3
   A tier with no action attached is a report, not a decision.

3. Concentration risk in BOTH directions - these are different risks:
     PRAYAG'S EXPOSURE   - Prayag's dependence on one distributor within a state
                           or district. Show each distributor's share of that
                           territory's business and flag any single distributor
                           above a set threshold.
     DISTRIBUTOR'S OWN   - each distributor's dependence on a few retailers.
                           Show top-5 and top-10 retailer share of that
                           distributor's NET.
   Report both on the same panel, clearly separated.

Acceptance:
  every distributor carries a tier, the inputs behind it, and all three actions
    (cadence, credit posture, range focus)
  a manual tier override is stored with its reason and shown as an override
  Prayag exposure: Jagdamba Traders shows as 86.1 percent of party order booking
    in Anant Singh's Delhi territory (Rs 15,80,341 of Rs 18,34,503) and is flagged
    as single-point dependency
  distributor-side concentration renders top-5 and top-10 retailer share for each
    distributor
  the recommended cadence is consistent with the capacity model in Phase 3-C - if
    the tiers demand more visits than the salesperson can physically do, surface
    that shortfall rather than silently producing an impossible plan
What to watch
- Coverage gap and assignment gap must stay separate. Merged into one 'uncovered' figure they suggest appointing distributors when 29 of 30 cases just need a field filled in.
- AT RISK must never be rendered as 'lost' or 'churned'. Three months of data cannot support that claim, and a state head acting on it would write off live accounts.
- The two definitions of 'new' must not be merged. Prasun scores 1 newly onboarded retailer and Rs 0 new-party booking, yet 2 reactivated accounts worth Rs 4,76,320 - averaging them into a single 'new business' number hides both facts.
- Tier cadence must be reconciled against the visit capacity model. Tiers that demand 1,300 visits from someone who can do 1,000 are how a plan becomes fiction.
- Direct dealers appear in the top-5 list. Ensure they are not also counted under a distributor's concentration - the same double count that corrupted the '2026-2027' TOTAL row.

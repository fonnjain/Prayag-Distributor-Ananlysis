# Replit Prompt 55 — Load FY2026-27 order booking, April to August

**Date:** 2 September 2026
**Scope:** Section A is design confirmation. Section B is implementation.
**Decision taken:** FY2026-27 only — April to July from the PSCode 3 archives,
plus 1–19 August from the Product-Wise file. **The historical years are out of
scope for now.**

**Standing rule:** every figure states which database or source it came from.

## Why this scope is clean

FY2026-27 avoids every complication the historical years carry.

**No date correction needed.** The 56,029 locale-misparsed rows are confined to
the FY2024-25 and FY2025-26 **Segment Wise Google Sheets**. The PSCode 3 archives
are XLSX files that never went through Sheets, and Prompt 51 T6 confirmed they
hold day-above-12 serials and zero out-of-fiscal-year rows. The Product-Wise file
is likewise clean.

**No frozen-year re-ingest.** Both sources are files already in
`attached_assets`, not registered workbooks.

**Order ID present throughout.** 17,951 distinct SORDs across Apr–Jul, 1,361 in
August. The identity key `order_id + product_code + occurrence` is fully
available.

**Basis proven equivalent.** 378 matched pairs across the 1 August changeover —
same retailer, product, quantity, discount — give identical values either side,
with GST added on top in the new format.

## Notes

- **A2 exists to prevent a mistake later.** The date correction is fresh and
  someone will reasonably ask whether it applies here. It does not, and the
  reason should be recorded rather than rediscovered.
- **The ₹0 per-month reconciliation is the hard control.** Both the booking load
  and the existing SKU rows come from the same accepted source lines. Any
  difference means the two parsers diverge, and that must be found before loading
  rather than after.
- **The canary warning matters.** The instinct on a red guard is to adjust the
  floor. That would remove a control that is working correctly to accommodate a
  file that is legitimately partial.

## What the page will show afterwards

**₹87.00 crore across 19,312 orders**, April to 19 August 2026.

April to July will show orders, retailers, distributors, quantity and value, with
status unavailable. August adds order status, CP codes and GST.

## Deliberately not in this load

**FY2024-25 and FY2025-26** — readable and worth doing, but they come from the two
Segment Wise workbooks carrying the locale-misparsed dates. That load must apply
the same transpose correction or the page would show wrong months for those years
while the Reports page shows right ones. A separate piece of work with its own
design step.

**FY2021-22 to FY2023-24** — no Order ID column at source, and FY2023-24's
workbook covers April 2023 only. Not possible at order level from anything held.

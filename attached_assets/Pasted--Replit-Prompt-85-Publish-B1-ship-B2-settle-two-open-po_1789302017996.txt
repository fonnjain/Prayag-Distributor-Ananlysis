## Section D — completeness threshold

```
Your recommendation of 75% minimum coverage is accepted, with the basis
always shown - "142 of 189 members".

ONE REFINEMENT, and it matters.

For COST roll-ups, weight the coverage by COST, not by headcount.

  142 of 189 members is 75% by headcount.
  Those same 142 could be 95% of total CTC, or 60%, depending on who is
  missing.

  A company cost ratio built on 75% of people but 60% of cost would be
  materially wrong at a headline that reads acceptable.

D1. Implement coverage as TWO figures wherever cost is rolled up:
      headcount coverage    142 of 189
      cost-weighted coverage  x% of known total CTC

    The 75% gate applies to the cost-weighted figure.

D2. Same principle for margin: the assessment already proposes
    revenue-weighted coverage for margin claims. Confirm that is
    revenue-weighted and not row-count weighted.

D3. For coverage roll-ups - visits, retailers - weight by what the metric
    measures. A member with 400 retailers missing matters more than one
    with 12.

    Recommend the weighting basis for each roll-up type.
```

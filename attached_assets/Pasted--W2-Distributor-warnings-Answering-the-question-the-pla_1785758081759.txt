# W2 — Distributor warnings

*Answering the question the placeholder asks, then correcting the proposed rule set.*

---

# The answer: there is no sub-dealer level

**The channel is two levels deep.**

```
Prayag
 ├── Distributor ──→ Retailer          the main route
 └── Direct Dealer                     PARALLEL - no distributor above them
```

**Evidence, all from the data:**

1. **Column K "Total Dealer" sums to 11,536** — that is the Secondary Retail Reach figure on the Overview. It counts **retailers**.
2. **Member sheets carry "Assigned Distributor" against each retailer.** One join, one level. There is no third column pointing at anything between them.
3. **"Direct Dealers Order" is column J**, a separate measure from secondary OB — business bought **direct from Prayag**, not through a distributor.

## But "dealer" means two different things, and that needs fixing

| Term | Level | Source |
|---|---|---|
| **Direct Dealer** | **same level as a distributor** | column J, blank Assigned Distributor |
| **Total Dealer** (col K) | **retailer / outlet level** | column K, a non-deduplicated sum |

**A direct dealer is not a dealer beneath a distributor. It is a customer of Prayag with no distributor above it.** That collision is worth renaming before more is built on top of it — `Total Dealer` should read `Total Retailers` or `Retail Outlets`.

---

# The proposed rule set needs correcting

**A1–A4 do not apply to distributors.** They are target warnings, and **Prayag sets targets for salespeople, not for distributors.** There is no distributor target anywhere in the data. Applying A1 to a distributor would require inventing one.

The distributor relationship is commercial, not a performance-managed one. **The warnings should be about the health of the relationship**, not about whether someone hit a number nobody gave them.

```
Prayag app - Warning System, Distributors tab. Same global rules.

FIRST, THE ANSWER TO THE QUESTION IN THE PLACEHOLDER:
There is NO sub-dealer level. The channel is Prayag -> Distributor -> Retailer,
with Direct Dealers as a PARALLEL branch buying straight from Prayag with no
distributor above them. Column K "Total Dealer" counts RETAILERS - it sums to
11,536, the Secondary Retail Reach figure. Rename it "Total Retailers" so it
stops colliding with "Direct Dealer", which sits at the distributor level.

DROP A1-A4 FROM THIS TAB. They are target warnings and distributors have no
targets. Do not derive one.

BUILD THESE INSTEAD - all computable from data already loaded.

RECENCY AND FLOW
  R1  DAYS SINCE LAST ORDER
      today - MAX(invoice date). Red / Orange / Yellow banded against that
      distributor's OWN historical order interval, not a fixed number of days.
      A distributor who orders monthly and a distributor who orders weekly are
      not comparable on a single threshold.
  R2  FILL RATE FALLING
      dispatch / order booking. Falling means orders are being taken and not
      served - a supply signal, not a distributor problem.
  R3  PENDING BUILD-UP
      pending as a share of that distributor's booking, and its direction.
  F1  FLOW GAP
      primary in-flow minus secondary out-flow, same period.
      STATE BOTH READINGS EVERY TIME: stock building at the distributor, OR
      business moving outside the attributed channel. No distributor stock
      statements exist, so they cannot be distinguished.
      NEVER phrase this as an accusation.

RANGE AND VALUE
  B1  BREADTH NARROWING
      distinct codes bought falling versus the prior comparable period.
      Rank BY VALUE, not by code count - losing 5 high-value codes matters
      more than losing 20 marginal ones.
  B2  LOST CODES
      bought in the prior period, absent now. Ranked by prior-period value.
      Proven demand, known customer - the warmest recovery list available.
  D1  DISCOUNT ABOVE NORM
      this distributor's effective discount against the territory norm for the
      same codes. The VARIANCE is the finding - one distributor materially
      below another on the same item is a commercial conversation.
  D2  DISCOUNT CREEP
      effective discount rising while volume is flat or falling.

CONCENTRATION - BOTH DIRECTIONS
  G2  PRAYAG'S DEPENDENCE
      this distributor's share of party order booking within their state.
      A single distributor carrying most of a state is Prayag's risk.
  G3  THE DISTRIBUTOR'S OWN CONCENTRATION
      their top-3 and top-5 retailer share, and effective retailers
      (10,000 / HHI). Their fragility becomes Prayag's one step later.

THE RETAILER BASE BENEATH THEM
  E1  AT-RISK RETAILERS
      bought last year, nothing yet this year, beneath this distributor.
      Sized by prior-year value. NOT churn on a partial year.
  E2  UNASSIGNED IN THEIR DISTRICTS
      retailers in districts this distributor serves who are assigned to
      nobody. 96.0 per cent of assigned retailers are active against 5.0 per
      cent of unassigned - this is an ADMINISTRATIVE fix and the fastest
      available.
  E3  ACTIVE SHARE FALLING
      active retailers beneath them as a share of total, over time.

REAL TERMS
  V1  REAL DECLINE
      growth below that distributor's own segment-weighted price index.
      The company index is 1.069 but the spread runs CPVC 1.007 to water tank
      1.181 - use their own mix, never one company figure.
      Three consecutive years of real decline company-wide makes this the
      warning most likely to fire, and the most honest.

GUARDS - the same discipline as the member tab:
  a distributor with too few orders to establish an interval is NOT flagged on
    R1 - report "insufficient history"
  concentration and correlation suppressed below a stated sample size
  project and institutional customers EXCLUDED from territory baselines and
    shown as their own channel
  every cross-year money figure shows real terms with the index named
  a partial period is excluded from any rate
  frozen years state the anchor used

SUPPRESSION - a root cause hides its symptoms, as on the member tab:
  R1 at Red (no orders for a long time) hides B1, D2, E3 - a dormant
    distributor will trivially show narrowing breadth and falling activity
  insufficient history hides everything derived from a trend

Acceptance - show the computed result:
  the Total Dealer column is renamed and the direct-dealer collision is gone
  A1-A4 do not appear on this tab
  R1 bands against each distributor's own order interval, not a fixed day count
  the flow gap states both readings and is not phrased as an accusation
  breadth narrowing is ranked by value
  G2 names the largest distributor by state share
  E2 links to the assignment gap list
  V1 uses each distributor's own segment mix, not the company index
  a distributor with insufficient history is reported as such, not flagged
  suppression works - a dormant distributor shows R1 and not five symptoms
```

## The two flags I would build first

**E2 — unassigned retailers in their districts.** It is the strongest relationship in the project, it is an administrative fix rather than a selling one, and it is the fastest thing anyone can act on.

**V1 — real decline.** Three consecutive years of real decline company-wide means this will fire widely, and it is the honest reading that nominal figures hide.

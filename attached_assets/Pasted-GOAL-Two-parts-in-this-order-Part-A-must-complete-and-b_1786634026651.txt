THE RULE
A month freezes on the 7th of the following month, computed from the load
date, never from a config list.
    June 2026 data  -> frozen from 7 July 2026
    A load on 6 July still picks up June changes. A load on 8 July does not.

A frozen month is never re-read from source. The loader skips it and logs the
skip: segment, month, freeze date.

Store on the row: frozen_at, and source_file_at_freeze, so it is always
possible to say which version of a sheet a frozen figure came from.

NO UNFREEZE PATH
There is deliberately no way to re-open a frozen month. Not a parameter, not
an environment variable, not an admin endpoint. Late corrections to a closed
month are accepted as lost - the business has judged that impact negligible.

If a frozen month must ever be corrected, that is a deliberate database
operation done knowingly, not a feature of the loader. Do not build one.

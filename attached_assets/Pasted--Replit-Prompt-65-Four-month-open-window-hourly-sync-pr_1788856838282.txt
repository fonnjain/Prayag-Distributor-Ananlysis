# Replit Prompt 65 — Four-month open window, hourly sync, provisional labelling

**Date:** 8 September 2026
**Scope:** Section A read-only. Sections B–E are code changes, in order.

**Standing rule:** every figure states which database or source it came from.

---

## The reasoning, in one paragraph

The app's own reconciliation shows **April and May match exactly**, while
**June, July and August are ahead by 63 rows and ₹10.72 lakh**. The months that
match are the ones Prayag have finished editing; the months that differ are the
ones they were still working on when we froze them.

**The drift is not tampering. It is a premature freeze.** We lock at day 7;
Prayag edit for about two months.

September is a separate thing — 13 rows behind on a single date, 7 September.
That is sync lag on an open month and it self-corrects.

## Why not nightly unfreeze and refreeze

A freeze lifted every night is not a freeze. Worse, each refreeze writes a **new
anchor** that claims finality and does not have it — a series of
authoritative-looking anchors, each superseded the next night. That is weaker
than no anchor, because it looks like a control while behaving like a rebuild.

**You do not need to unfreeze if you never freeze too early.** Keep the month open
until Prayag have finished with it, then freeze once, permanently.

## Notes

- **A5 is the evidence for the whole change.** April and May match exactly because
  Prayag have finished editing them. That is what a settled month looks like, and
  it is why freezing at three months rather than seven days is the right boundary.
- **C1 before anything else in Section C.** Those 63 rows are about to be deleted.
  Printing and archiving them first is the difference between a reconciliation and
  a quiet loss.
- **C5 matters for people, not code.** The FY total drops ₹10.17 lakh. That is
  small, but someone has quoted ₹136.11 Cr and should hear why it became ₹136.01
  Cr from you rather than noticing it.
- **E4 is worth checking.** Growth and the freeze already use different clocks —
  the 8th versus the 7th. Widening one and not the other makes them diverge
  further, and the labels should not contradict each other.

## What this costs, honestly

**Four months open at once**, and anchors settling roughly 92 days after month end
instead of seven.

Against that: the anchor was being set on unsettled data, which is what
manufactured the drift we have spent a week investigating. An anchor that has to
be explained every month is not protecting anything.

**And two things make it safe now that were not true a week ago.** The ledger
records every change including rejections, so nothing moves silently. And
provisional labelling means a reader knows which figures can still move.

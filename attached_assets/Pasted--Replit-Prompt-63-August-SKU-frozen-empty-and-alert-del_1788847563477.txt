## Section B — alert delivery. A decision, not a build.

**Three real digests ran — 17, 24 and 31 August. 110 email attempts, all failed.
110 WhatsApp attempts, all pending. Zero messages delivered.**

The scheduler works. Recipients are configured: 13 WhatsApp routes, an all-India
email route, four on-raise routes. The providers are not — `RESEND_API_KEY` and
`SMTP_HOST` are both unset.

**Ten A1 alerts covering ₹3.44 crore of shortfall are open, unacknowledged, and
have never reached a person.**

The timing was fortunate. Had delivery been configured a week earlier, those
digests would have carried the 114 false August A2 alerts to the CEO. The gap that
looks like a failure prevented a worse one.

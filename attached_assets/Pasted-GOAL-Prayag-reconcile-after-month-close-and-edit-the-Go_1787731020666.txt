GOAL
Prayag reconcile after month close and edit the Google Sheets. Frozen months
are never re-read, so the app and the sheet diverge silently - June and July
already differ by Rs 4.83 L and Rs 4.73 L against 18 invoices deleted after
freeze, and nobody found it until someone counted by hand.

Detect the drift automatically. Never act on it automatically.

Open months already re-read fully on every 6-hourly sync - the whole month is
replaced, so any changed day is picked up. This closes the frozen-month gap
only.

============================================================
1 - THE WEEKLY CHECK - read-only, always
============================================================
Once a week, for the LAST THREE FROZEN MONTHS in the current fiscal year:

  read the sheet tab read-only
  compare row count and net against what the app holds frozen
  record the result

It NEVER writes, never unfreezes, never reloads. It only reports.

Three months, not all. A month frozen six months ago is settled; drift that
old is a different conversation.

frozen_drift_check
  id · fy · month · checked_at
  app_rows · app_net · sheet_rows · sheet_net
  row_delta · net_delta
  status        match | drift | sheet_unreadable
  detail        JSON - the invoices present in one and not the other
  resolved_by · resolved_at · resolution   refreshed | accepted | ignored

Store the invoice-level detail at detection time. The sheet may change again
before anyone looks, and the operator needs to see what was found THEN.

============================================================
2 - WHAT COUNTS AS DRIFT
============================================================
Any difference in row count, or a net difference above Rs 1,000.

Below that, log as `match` with the delta recorded - a few rupees of rounding
is not worth a notification, but it should still be visible in history.

If the sheet cannot be read - quota, permissions, tab renamed - record
`sheet_unreadable` and say so. Do NOT record it as a match.

============================================================
3 - HOW THE OPERATOR SEES IT
============================================================
On the Data page, only when drift exists:

    2 frozen months differ from the source sheet

    Jun-26   frozen 1 Aug     app 12,868 rows  Rs 31,42,78,773
                              sheet 12,848     Rs 31,37,95,963
                              -20 rows  -Rs 4,82,810.94
                              [ view detail ]  [ refresh this month ]

    Jul-26   frozen 8 Aug     app 13,803 rows  Rs 32,10,28,905
                              sheet 13,769     Rs 32,05,55,470
                              -34 rows  -Rs 4,73,435.00
                              [ view detail ]  [ refresh this month ]

**view detail** lists every invoice present in one and not the other, with
date, lines and value. The operator must see WHAT changed before deciding.

Nothing happens until someone clicks. **The app never refreshes on its own.**

============================================================
4 - THE REFRESH - one month, reason required
============================================================
Same as a manual refresh:
  a. Preview - what would be added, removed and changed. Nothing written.
  b. Reason required, minimum 10 characters, plus operator name.
  c. In one transaction: archive the current frozen rows, delete the month,
     reload from the sheet, re-freeze immediately, write the record.

The wipe guard STAYS ARMED. Below 0.60x existing rows it aborts even with a
valid reason. A reconciliation removing 40% of a month is not a
reconciliation.

============================================================
5 - THREE OUTCOMES, ALL RECORDED
============================================================
  refreshed  the app now matches the sheet
  accepted   the app value is correct and the sheet is wrong - keep frozen,
             record why
  ignored    known and not worth acting on

"Accepted" matters. Sometimes the sheet is the thing that changed wrongly, and
the operator should be able to say so rather than being forced to refresh or
leave it flagged forever.

A resolved item stops appearing but stays in history.

============================================================
6 - CLOSED FISCAL YEARS ARE NOT CHECKED
============================================================
Only the CURRENT fiscal year. FY2025-26 is anchored at Rs 3,609,953,808.51 and
must not move. If drift is somehow detected in a closed year, report it as a
finding and refuse the refresh.

============================================================
DO NOT
============================================================
- Do not refresh automatically, ever. Detection and action stay separate.
- Do not check or refresh a closed fiscal year.
- Do not check more than the last three frozen months.
- Do not remove the wipe guard.
- Do not let a sheet read failure record as a match.

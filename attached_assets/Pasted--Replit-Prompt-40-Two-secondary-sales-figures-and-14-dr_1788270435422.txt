# Replit Prompt 40 — Two secondary sales figures, and 14 dropped workbooks

**Date:** 1 September 2026
**Scope:** Read-only. Change nothing, reload nothing, drop nothing.

**Standing rule:** every figure states which database or source it came from.

## What prompted this

There are two figures called secondary sales for Apr–Jul FY2026-27, from two
different sources, **4.0% apart**, and nothing in the codebase compares them.

| Figure | Source | Value |
|---|---|---:|
| Sales Received, Secondary Performance | live Google Sheets, `IMPORTRANGE` into member workbooks | **₹84.63 Cr** |
| `secondary_sku_line` net | CRM export files, manual load | **₹81.36 Cr** |
| | | **gap ₹3.27 Cr** |

And I found a mechanism that explains at least half of it.

`pscode3Jul26.ts` carries a hardcoded `DUP_DROP` set of **14 member workbook
names**. 163 files found, 14 dropped, 149 loaded — all three asserted, so the
loader refuses to run if the count changes.

For July that removes **2,658 rows and ₹1,58,74,958** — 6.6% of the month.

Two of the fourteen are not obviously junk: **SANDEEP DADHEECH**, a state head
with ₹54.83 crore of sales, and **O.P. KALRA**, one of the six unrecognised State
Head folders.

## Notes

- A1 is the whole of Section A. `DUP_DROP` asserts duplication and shows no
  evidence for it. Fourteen names, ₹1.59 crore in one month, and a loader that
  refuses to run if the count changes — that is a strong control built on an
  unproven premise.
- B4 is the payoff. If the Apr–Jun drops sum with July to around ₹3.27 crore, the
  two secondary sales figures are reconciled and the difference has a name.
- C3 could make this a documentation problem rather than a data one. If "Sub
  Total after discount" and "Sales Received" are genuinely different measures,
  the gap is expected — but nobody has written that down, and two pages show
  different totals for the same period with no explanation.
- Section D is not a defect. Including flagged rows in totals while excluding
  them from rankings is a reasonable choice. The question is only whether anyone
  reading ₹84.63 crore knows ₹7.2 crore of it is flagged.

## Not asked here, deliberately

Nothing in this prompt proposes changing the drop list, reloading a month, or
adjusting either figure. April through July are frozen. If the drops turn out to
be wrong, that is a decision about a closed period and needs its own
conversation.

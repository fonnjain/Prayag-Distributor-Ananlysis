Query 1 omitted the Color column. Your output header reads
(invoice_no, code, amount). My spec included colour and it matters.

Colour variants are REAL duplicates on that key. The documented case:
invoice 12600285, item 188, qty 88, Rs.25,867 - serial 1802 WHITE, serial 1803
IVORY, both genuine. Without colour, every variant counts as a duplicate.

RE-RUN QUERY 1 WITH COLOUR, for all four months:

  SELECT month_label, COUNT(*) AS raw,
         COUNT(DISTINCT (invoice_no, item_code, color, taxable_value)) AS distinct
  FROM sale_line WHERE fy='2026-27' GROUP BY month_label;

Then the same grouped by (invoice_no, item_code, color, qty, taxable_value).

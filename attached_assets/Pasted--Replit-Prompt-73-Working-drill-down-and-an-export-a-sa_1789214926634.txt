# Replit Prompt 73 — Working drill-down, and an export a sales head can use

**Date:** 12 September 2026
**Scope:** Section A is the UI. Section B is the export. Section C is a
correction found while reviewing.

**Standing rule:** every figure states which database or source it came from.

---

## Two problems

**Drill-down does not work.** Reports 1–7 render as tabs on
`/company-reports`, but selecting a state does not open its parties, a party
does not open its segments, and so on. The requirement is that **every report
drills down in the UI**.

**The export carries data-integrity columns, not analysis.** A sales head opening
`Company_Reports_2026-27_2026-09-12.xlsx` finds twelve sheets including two raw
dumps, a metadata snapshot, raw-vs-canonical columns and seven empty future-month
column pairs.

## Notes

- **Section A is the substantive work.** The export can be trimmed in an hour; the
  drill-down is the feature that was asked for.
- **The Info sheet is the best thing in this export** and should not be touched. It
  names the source of every figure, states the like-month rule, discloses the
  1,000-party cap and carries the provisional-month line. That is exactly what an
  Info sheet is for.
- **The future-month columns in Report 2** are the same problem as elsewhere:
  September to March 2027 render as empty pairs because the year has not happened.
  Showing only months in the period fixes the width and the confusion together.
- **C2 matters more than C1.** If R7 is still on the old grouping, other surfaces
  probably are too, and it is better to find them in one pass.

## The Deep Dive export, for contrast

`SalesDeepDive_Surojit_Mondal_...xlsx` is seven sheets, all modest — Summary 15
rows, Targets 16, Coverage 11, Cost 6, Monthly 14, Missing Data 7. Nothing
extraneous, and the Monthly sheet is present because Surojit has monthly rows.

That is the right shape. The Company Reports export should look more like it.

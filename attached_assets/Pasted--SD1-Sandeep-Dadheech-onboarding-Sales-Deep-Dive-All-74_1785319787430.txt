## What to watch

**The zero-target members are the largest arithmetic risk.** Ten of them, carrying ₹2.01 Cr. A division error breaks the page; treating them as 0 per cent makes real producers look like total failures.

**Odisha at 107 per cent will be quoted as a success story within a week** if the like-for-like figure is not beside it.

**Expect the reconciliation to fail for some members.** Anant Singh's eleven all reconciled exactly, but that was eleven. Seventy-four sheets across four folders, several created in 2020, will not all be clean. Report failures rather than tolerancing them.

**Do not carry Anant Singh's ratios across.** His territory has an unusually high direct-dealer share and a concentrated distributor base. Sandeep has one member with direct-dealer business out of 74. Carry the method, not the numbers.

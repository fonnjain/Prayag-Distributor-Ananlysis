1. THE TWO MISREADINGS

(a) Invoice prefix = BRANCH, not billing system.

You called 12xxx "SAP billing" and 22xxx/32xxx/42xxx "non-SAP invoice
families". The prefix is the branch. From the SAP file itself:

  52600001 = Gujarat
  72600001 = Andal

  12600002 = Bhiwadi 1

So 1=Bhiwadi, 4=Delhi, 5=Gujarat, 7=Andal, and 2/3/6 are three more branches.
All of them are SAP invoices. There is no second billing system. A SAP tab

2. PIPELINE DIRECTION - SAP IS THE MASTER

The evidence is the SAP file's own column names:

  Posting Date, Document Number, Dscription, PriceBefDi

"Dscription" is SAP Business One's own column name, misspelling included.
"PriceBefDi" is its truncated "Price Before Discount". These are raw ERP field
names. No operator hand-types a column called Dscription.

So SAP bills, and SALE SHEET 26-27 is derived from it. SALE SHEET adds Serial
no, City, Destination, STATION, STATE and STATE HEAD; drops Segment,
Dscription, Total and Branch; and converts tank quantity from pieces to litres.

Why SAP nonetheless shows FEWER July rows: the Drive copy of SAP is a periodic
export and it lags. SAP last modified 18 July 07:54. SALE SHEET last modified
20 July 05:06. Two days apart. Your 139 "sale-only" rows sit in a single
invoice dated 18 July - they are newer than the last SAP export, not
unauthorised.

Consequence: "saleOnly" means newer than the last export. "dbOnly" means
removed from the sheet after ingestion, which is the real issue and is what
db-gap now recovers.

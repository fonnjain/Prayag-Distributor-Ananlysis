# Replit Prompt 115 — AI Analyst should cover the whole app

**Date:** 18 September 2026
**Follows:** the Prompt 114 audit, accepted in full.
**Scope:** A is enforcement and must ship first. B–E are graph expansion, in
releases. F is verification.

**Standing rule:** every figure states which database or source it came from.

---

## The target

**Every question a surface can answer, AI Analyst should answer** — Sales Deep
Dive, SKU Deep Dive, Distributor Deep Dive, Company Reports, Pending Orders,
Momentum, Growth, Targets, Coverage, Alerts, Data Health, and the Resolution
register.

**Keep the bounded resolver architecture.** No unrestricted SQL. The audit is
right that query access would bypass `blocks_api`, effective-period MRP rules,
not-offered exclusions, source seams, availability semantics and frozen-period
rules.

**Expand the graph, not the permissions.**

---

## Section A — enforcement first. Nothing else ships before this.

The audit found the numeric guard **appends a warning and does not reject**. And
the six operating rules are prompt-enforced only.

```
Code change. The resolver layer, not the prompt.

A1. DISCRIMINATED AVAILABILITY ON EVERY MEASURE. Not a nullable value:

      measured | partial | unavailable | held | not_applicable

    A held measure returns availability "held", the hold code, the
    category and a safe reason - and NO VALUE. The blocked number must not
    be in the payload at all, because a model cannot narrate what it never
    received.

A2. PERCENTAGES CARRY THEIR BASIS OR DO NOT EXIST. Numerator, denominator,
    population, period and source, as structured fields. A percentage
    without them is not constructible.

A3. TYPED MEASURE KINDS WITH FIXED LABELS.
      order_booking never renders as "sales"
      dispatch never renders as "booking"
      The label is a property of the measure kind, not the prompt.

A4. PROJECTIONS ONLY FROM AN APPROVED SEASONAL SERVICE. A projection node
    carries the calibration basis and observed months, or it does not
    exist. Linear annualisation must be unconstructible - Q4 is 34.56% of
    this business's year and flat pacing understates by roughly 17 points.

A5. CATEGORY IS ASSIGNED OR UNMAPPED. Never inferred from prefix, series,
    division or price.

A6. EVERY NODE CARRIES ITS CUTOFF AND READ TIME. The audit found the graph
    returning Rs 140.54 Cr where production held Rs 140.64 Cr - a cache
    difference of Rs 0.10 Cr quoted with no cutoff.

A7. THE NUMERIC GUARD MUST REJECT, NOT WARN. An answer citing a figure not
    present in the resolved nodes does not reach the user.

These survive a model change. Prompt instructions do not.
```

## Section E — what it should say when it cannot answer

```
E1. A gap must be as informative as an answer. Not "I don't have that" but:

      what is missing
      why - not loaded, held, source absent, out of period
      whether it is tracked, and under which register item
      what would make it available

E2. WORKED EXAMPLE. "What is PTMT margin for April 2026?" should return
    roughly:

      "Held. PTMT margin for January to April 2026 is under Resolution
       hold H1 - factory cost recorded at about 40% of its true level,
       overstating margin by roughly 21 points. Open since 9 September,
       owner Prayag. The underlying figure is not shown."

    That is more useful than the number would have been.
```

## Notes

- **Section A before anything.** Every capability added without it is another
  surface a model can narrate wrongly. Held measures must be absent from the
  payload, not merely flagged.
- **B is the release that changes the most.** The app's best knowledge — what is
  unreliable and why — is currently invisible to the thing people ask.
- **C3 is now buildable** because per-month source metadata exists. It did not a
  week ago.
- **E is the difference between a tool and an oracle.** An analyst that explains a
  gap, names the hold and says who owns it is more useful than one that produces a
  number it cannot support.

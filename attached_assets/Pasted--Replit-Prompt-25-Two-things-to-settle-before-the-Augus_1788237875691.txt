## Notes

- Section A is the gate. The load itself is small and isolated, but putting 19
  days on screen labelled August recreates the exact problem that started this
  thread. A5 asks for the smallest fix; that becomes a separate, narrow change.
- B4 and B5 are the interesting ones. If ADARSH GAURAV is in neither the roster
  nor the LEFT list, he is a third category, and he has now failed to resolve
  across two fiscal years and two different source formats.
- Section C matters when the next file arrives rather than now. C3 in particular:
  the table has no FY column and no freeze check, so the only thing preventing a
  historical file from being loaded is operator care.

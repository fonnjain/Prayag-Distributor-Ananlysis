Every verification this project has run was at the database level — achievement equals received ÷ plan, row counts reconcile, totals match anchors. None of it confirmed what a State Head actually sees on screen. A correct value can sit under the wrong label, for the wrong period. That gap is what this closes.

1. The achievement label

FY2026-27 · company / overview

The single most important thing to get right. Two correct numbers exist; only one belongs on screen.

✓

Q1 achievement reads roughly 74%

Not 15.86%. Not any mid-teens figure labelled “achievement.”

Look forThe headline FY2026-27 achievement / attainment figure on the company or overview view.
Pass~74% (three closed months of sales against three months of plan).
Fail~15.86% shown as achievement. That is annual progress — 3 months of sales against a 12-month plan — a different metric wearing the wrong name.
Why it matters: 15.86% and 74% are both arithmetically correct. This is the exact confusion the whole labelling effort was about. If you see 15.86% called achievement anywhere, that is a real fail to send back.
✓

If annual progress is shown too, it is labelled differently

15.86% is fine to display — but as “YTD vs annual plan,” never as “achievement.”

Look forAny second percentage near the achievement figure.
PassIt carries its own clear label (“annual progress,” “YTD of full-year target”) distinct from the ~74% achievement.
FailTwo percentages both called “achievement,” or the annual one unlabelled and ambiguous.

2. July and the future months

FY2026-27 · monthly view

An in-progress month and an unstarted month must not read as zero — zero looks like failure, not “no data yet.”

✓

July reads “in progress” / “partial” — not 0%

July is open and mid-collection. It must not show a hard 0% or a red miss.

Look forThe July cell / row in the FY2026-27 monthly breakdown.
Pass“In progress,” “partial,” a provisional marker, or the live figure clearly flagged as not-yet-final.
FailJuly shows 0% or reads as a completed month that missed. That misrepresents an ongoing month as a failure.
Bonus: if July primary dispatch is marked provisional, that is the disputed-rows flag working. Note whether you see it.
✓

Aug through Mar are blank — not 0%

Months that haven’t happened should be empty or “not yet,” never zero.

Look forThe Aug–Mar cells in the FY2026-27 monthly view.
PassBlank, dash, or “not yet recorded.”
Fail0% or 0 values, which drag down any average and read as real misses.
✓

The “not yet recorded” vs “zero sales” distinction

A month awaiting data must not read the same as a month with genuinely no business.

Look forWording / tooltip on empty or partial months, and on the secondary side where the sheet updates in arrears.
Pass“Not yet recorded” (neutral, awaiting entry) is visibly different from “zero sales” (real).
FailA data-entry lag looks identical to a performance collapse.
This is the arrears guard’s whole purpose. On 1 Aug, if July secondary isn’t updated, a State Head must not see every salesperson at 0%. Confirm the wording distinguishes the two states.

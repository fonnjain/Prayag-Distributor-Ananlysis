# C1 — Comparison Deep Dive, the contract layer

*API only. No UI in this phase. If the guards work, the rest is presentation; if they do not, no interface will save it.*

---

```
Prayag app - Comparison Deep Dive, Phase C1 of 4. Same global rules as every
earlier phase. NO UI IN THIS PHASE. Build the selection schema, the measure
catalogue and all twelve guards, and prove them with an API.

The page exists because every comparison that has gone wrong in this project
went wrong the same way - the two sides were not on the same basis. Nine real
examples, all encountered here:

   Prasun 73 retailers vs Shivam 136        different sources
   Anant Singh 51.7% vs 58.4%               before/after excluding 3 leavers
   Rahul Singh 11 days vs Amey 77 days      pace not normalised
   Jul Rs 27.36 Cr vs Jun Rs 31.43 Cr       13 of 14 segments peak Q4
   FY25-26 Rs 361 Cr vs FY24-25 Rs 341 Cr   +5.8% nominal, -1.0% REAL
   HDPE gap Rs 32.65 Cr vs Rs 0.01 Cr       with vs without project
   Q1 FY26-27 vs full FY25-26               partial vs complete
   Sandeep 100% active vs Anant 96%         different retailer definitions
   Sulinder Pal 45.6%                       3 of 5 have no target

This phase must make each of those IMPOSSIBLE, not merely documented.

1. THE SELECTION SCHEMA.

   comparison = {
     entityType : company | head | member | distributor | retailer | segment | code
     entities   : [id, ...]        1..N, or a cohort rule
     periods    : [period, ...]    1..N
     measures   : [measure, ...]
     basis      : primary | secondary          NEVER mixed silently
     normalise  : absolute | perElapsedMonth | perWorkingDay | perRetailer
                | perVisit | realTerms
     channel    : territory | project | all    default territory
     population : activeOnly | includeLeft     default activeOnly
   }

   PERIOD GRAMMAR: month (Jul-26), quarter (Q1 FY2026-27), fiscal year,
   YTD, samePeriodLastYear (auto-derived, like months only), custom range.
   EVERY PERIOD CARRIES A COMPLETENESS FLAG: complete | partial |
   noActualsRecorded. Derive it from the data, not a config list.

2. THE MEASURE CATALOGUE - only measures VALID for that entity type are
   offered. This is what prevents most category errors before they happen.

   MEMBER and HEAD: secondary OB (I+J), sales received (AY), target (BM),
     achievement (recomputed), retailers (THREE sources, each named),
     visits (THREE sources), pace ratio, cost and cost ratio, unassigned
     share, customer states, SKU breadth
   DISTRIBUTOR: primary sale, primary OB, pending, fill rate, SKU breadth,
     gap value, effective discount, tier A/B/C, retailers served, effective
     retailers, flow gap
   RETAILER: secondary OB, codes bought, assigned distributor, active/dormant,
     last order date. LIVE YEAR IS APR-JUN ONLY - the register has no July
   SEGMENT and CODE: NET, quantity, customers buying, breadth, discount and
     its variance, seasonal curve, gap value, peer penetration

   Requesting a measure invalid for the entity type returns an error naming
   the entity type and the valid measures. It does not silently return null.

3. THE TWELVE GUARDS. Each either BLOCKS the comparison or ANNOTATES it.
   Report which fired on every response.

    1 SOURCE       both sides must use the same source. Mixing sheet row
                   counts with Data-tab declared counts is BLOCKED, not
                   footnoted. Prasun reads 62 declared and 73 from his sheet.
    2 LIKE MONTHS  a partial period compares only to the same months of
                   another year. Q1 against a full FY is BLOCKED.
    3 SEASONAL     comparing different quarters shows each segment's seasonal
                   index beside the figure. 13 of 14 peak Q4 on territory
                   curves.
    4 REAL TERMS   any cross-year money comparison shows nominal AND real
                   using that segment's own Laspeyres index - CPVC 1.007 to
                   water tank 1.181, company 1.069. Never one company figure
                   across segments.
    5 CHANNEL      territory by default. Project shown as its own panel,
                   never blended into a territory baseline. Project was 6 per
                   cent of revenue and 78 per cent of apparent opportunity.
    6 POPULATION   LEFT members excluded from current-period comparison,
                   history preserved. If the roster differs between the
                   periods being compared, NAME the members who joined or
                   left and state the effect. Excluding three leavers moved
                   one team 51.7 to 58.4 per cent.
    7 TENURE       members whose elapsed months or working days differ
                   materially are compared ONLY on normalised measures.
                   Working days run 8 to 79 company-wide. Absolute visit
                   counts are suppressed and per-day shown instead.
    8 ZERO TARGET  "no target recorded", never 0 per cent. Where a group
                   contains untargeted members, report headline AND
                   like-for-like. Sulinder Pal has 3 of 5 untargeted.
    9 NO BUSINESS  a period with a plan and no actuals reads "not recorded
                   yet". Sunil Mohanty and Anuj Sharma have no target and no
                   business - undefined, not zero, and excluded from ranking.
   10 IDENTITY     two people are named Ashutosh Kumar. An ambiguous entity
                   selection returns an error naming both candidates with
                   their head and headquarter. Never take the first match.
   11 SAMPLE       correlations, concentration and effective-retailer figures
                   are suppressed below a stated minimum and say so. Sunil
                   Mohanty has one retailer; that is not a distribution.
   12 FROZEN       a comparison against a closed year states the frozen anchor
                   used: FY2023-24 Rs 349.02 Cr, FY2024-25 Rs 341.14 Cr,
                   FY2025-26 Rs 361.00 Cr.

4. EVERY RESPONSE CARRIES A BASIS BLOCK - source, period with completeness,
   channel, population, normalisation, and the guards that fired. A consumer
   that ignores it still gets correct numbers; a consumer that reads it can
   explain them.

5. ROUTE: POST /api/comparison
   Body is the selection schema. Response is the basis block, the guard
   results, and the value matrix. No rendering, no charts, no suggestions -
   those are C2 to C4.

Acceptance - prove each by showing the response, not by asserting success:
  a member-vs-member comparison on retailers where one side would use sheet
    rows and the other declared counts is BLOCKED with the reason
  Q1 FY2026-27 against full FY2025-26 is BLOCKED as partial vs complete
  Q1 FY2026-27 against Q1 FY2025-26 is ALLOWED and labelled like-months
  a cross-year money comparison returns nominal AND real, with the segment
    index named - confirm water tank uses 1.181 and CPVC 1.007
  comparing Rahul Singh (11 working days) with Amey Deodhar (77) suppresses
    absolute visits and returns per-day figures with the tenure guard noted
  a comparison including Sulinder Pal returns headline AND like-for-like with
    the three untargeted members named
  a comparison including Sunil Mohanty or Anuj Sharma returns "no target and
    no recorded business", not zero, and they are excluded from ranking
  entity "Ashutosh Kumar" with no head returns an error naming both, with
    their heads and headquarters
  a correlation requested on fewer than the minimum sample is suppressed with
    the sample size stated
  a comparison against FY2025-26 names the frozen anchor Rs 361.00 Cr
  requesting a distributor measure on a member entity returns an error listing
    the valid member measures
  every response carries the basis block

DO NOT BUILD ANY UI IN THIS PHASE. C2 adds trajectory and peer views, C3 the
matrix and cohort modes, C4 the suggestion layer. If a guard cannot be proven
by an API response here, it will not hold once there is a chart in front of it.
```

---

## Why C1 alone

**Every guard is a rule that has already been broken once.** Guard 1 cost three separate investigations. Guard 4 is why a −1.0% real year read as +5.8% growth. Guard 5 is why HDPE sat at the top of a push list it had no business being on.

A chart makes those failures harder to see, not easier. Proving the contract against an API — where the response either says *blocked, and here is why* or returns a basis block — is the only way to know it holds.

**C2 to C4 are presentation.** They are worth building, and they are not worth building first.

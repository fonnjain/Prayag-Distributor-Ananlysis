GOAL
Customers > Schemes > Nudge List currently shows a column called "They earn"
and one called "ROI". Two problems.

First, the names are wrong. "They earn" does not say what the number is, and
"ROI" reads like a company return when it is the distributor's extra earning
per rupee of extra purchase. Rename them to "Extra Earn" and "Extra ROI".

Second, a salesperson cannot reproduce either figure from what is on screen.
The table shows Billed, Next slab and Gap, but not the current slab rate, the
current earning, or the earning at the next slab. So the two most important
numbers arrive as assertions the salesperson has to take on trust, in front of
a distributor who will ask how it was worked out. Expose every variable.

PART 1 - RENAME
In artifacts/api-server/src/lib/schemes/nudge.ts and
artifacts/prayag/src/components/customers/SchemeNudgeEngine.tsx:

  NudgeRow.theyEarn  ->  NudgeRow.extraEarn
  NudgeRow.roi       ->  NudgeRow.extraRoi

Both fields appear only in those two files - rename the field, not just the
label. Also rename the sort key "theyEarn" to "extraEarn", the roiColor helper
to extraRoiColor, and every visible label:

  "They earn"          ->  "Extra Earn"
  "They earn (total)"  ->  "Extra Earn (total)"   (Sales Head tab)
  "ROI"                ->  "Extra ROI"

DO NOT rename the `roi` query parameter on GET /api/schemes/nudges in
routes/schemes.ts. That is the suppression threshold, not this column, and
renaming it would break existing links. Leave it exactly as it is.

PART 2 - EXPOSE THE VARIABLES
Add two fields to NudgeRow so the arithmetic is fully derivable from the
response:

  currentSlab: number | null    the threshold of the slab the distributor
                                is currently in (getCurrentSlab already
                                computes this - it is simply not returned)
  nextEarnings: number | null   nextSlab * nextRate

currentRate and currentEarnings already exist on NudgeRow and are already
computed. They are just not rendered. Render them.

The Nudge List table should carry these columns, in this order:

  Distributor | Scheme | Current slab | Current rate | Current earn |
  Next slab | Next rate | Gap | Extra Earn | Extra ROI | Status

Keep the table horizontally scrollable rather than shrinking the font below
readable size.

PART 3 - THE WORKED CALCULATION
Clicking a row expands an inline strip underneath it showing the calculation
in that distributor's own numbers, using the real values, not a template.
For B R ASSOCIATES the strip must read as:

  Gap          = 6,00,000 - 4,24,920 = Rs 1,75,080
  Extra Earn   = (6,00,000 x 6.00%) - (4,24,920 x 5.00%)
               = 36,000 - 21,246 = Rs 14,754
  Extra ROI    = 14,754 / 1,75,080 = 8.42%

Above the table, add one line of explanation, because this mechanic is the
whole reason the numbers are large and nobody guesses it:

  "A slab pays its rate on the distributor's ENTIRE quarterly billing, not
   just the amount above the threshold. Crossing a slab re-prices everything
   already bought - which is why Extra Earn is far bigger than the margin on
   the gap alone."

Tooltips:
  Extra Earn - "What the DISTRIBUTOR earns extra by reaching the next slab,
                after re-pricing his whole quarter. Not Prayag's cost."
  Extra ROI  - "Extra Earn per rupee of extra purchase needed to get there."

PART 4 - NULLS
Rows with status AT_MAX (no next slab) and TRIP_ZONE (reward is a trip, no
rate) have no Extra Earn or Extra ROI. Render the status word in those cells.
Never render 0, and never render a dash that could be read as zero. The same
applies to currentSlab and nextEarnings when they are null.

CONVENTIONS
- Two decimals, truncated not rounded, using trunc2 from
  artifacts/prayag/src/lib/trunc.ts. No emojis.
- Add all new columns to the Export output as well.
- Never console.log in server code - use req.log in handlers, logger elsewhere.

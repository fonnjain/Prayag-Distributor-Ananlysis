   Between the 1st and the 6th, TWO months are unfrozen and both must be
   attempted: the prior month still inside its edit window, and the current open
   month. Deepak confirms the sheet is edited for 3 to 5 days into the following
   month, which is exactly why July must stay in scope until it freezes.

     1-6 Aug    replace Jul-26 AND Aug-26
     7 Aug      Jul-26 freezes, then Aug-26 only
     8-31 Aug   Aug-26 only
     1-6 Sep    replace Aug-26 AND Sep-26
     7 Sep      Aug-26 freezes

   Log every month attempted, including no-ops, so the scope is visible rather
   than inferred from which months happened to change.

3. AN EMPTY MONTH IS NOT A SHORT READ.
   August will read zero rows every night until invoices are raised. The read
   guard must not treat that as truncation and abort.

   Specifically:
     a month with no baseline yet AND an empty read is NORMAL - record a
       baseline of 0 and continue
     a month whose baseline is 0 and reads 0 is a no-op - log it, do not abort
     the guard should fire only when a month with a POSITIVE baseline reads
       materially fewer rows

   This matters this week: if the guard trips on an empty read, August's first
   real sync could halt on the day invoices start landing, and nobody would
   notice until the figure looked wrong.

4. AUGUST'S FIRST REAL DATA IS THE TEST.
   When the Aug tab receives its first invoices, confirm:
     the baseline rises from 0 to the new count without the guard firing
     the month replaces cleanly and rows written equals rows read
     a second tick on unchanged data produces zero changes

Acceptance:
  the nightly log lists BOTH Jul-26 and Aug-26 as attempted, with Aug-26 a no-op
  Apr, May and Jun continue to show frozen-skipped
  an empty August read does not abort and does not halt the tombstone
  July freezes automatically on 7 August with its anchor recorded at whatever
    the sheet holds at that moment
  after 7 August the log shows only Aug-26 attempted

# C2b — More measures on the Comparison page

*Six requested, three of which need care. Plus the ones I would add alongside.*

---

## Three of the six are period-pair measures

**New SKUs to existing customers** and **New Customers** cannot be computed for a single period. "New" only means anything against a baseline.

In Mode A (trajectory) that is natural — each period compares to the one before. In **Mode B (peer, one period) they need an explicit baseline selector**, or they must be disabled with the reason stated.

## And "Coverage" means five different things here

| Reading | Formula |
|---|---|
| Retailer activation | active retailers ÷ total retailers |
| Visit coverage | visits done ÷ visits required |
| Segment coverage | segments sold ÷ segments available |
| SKU breadth | codes bought ÷ codes ever sold |
| **Secondary ÷ primary** | **not a valid ratio — different populations** |

The last one is Correction 1 in the State Head glossary. **Name the specific coverage rather than offering one ambiguous measure.**

## The one I would prioritise

**Unassigned share is not on this page**, and it is the strongest relationship the project has found — **96.0% active with an assigned distributor against 5.0% without**.

For a comparison tool meant to support decisions, that is the measure most likely to explain why one member's numbers differ from another's. Everything else describes the outcome; that one points at a cause.

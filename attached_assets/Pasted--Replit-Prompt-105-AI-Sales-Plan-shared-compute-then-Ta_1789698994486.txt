## Section C — Tab 1: what they buy

Ships alone and is immediately useful. A salesperson who knows what a customer
bought last year and has stopped buying is better prepared than by any
recommendation.

```
Code change. New page /ai-sales-plan, Tab 1 only.

SELECT a retailer directly, or a member and then one of their retailers.

SHOW every SKU this customer bought:
  FY2025-26 quantity and value
  FY2026-27 to date, quantity and value
  the change, in both

THREE FLAGS, computed:

  STOPPED     bought last year, nothing this year
  DOWN 30%+   slipping
  UP 30%+     working - do more of it

  STOPPED is the strongest pitch on the page. The Prompt 101 sample found
  Nirman Sales with 236 stopped SKUs, Srikrishna 166, Ayan 92 - with
  individual lines worth Rs 3.04 L, Rs 2.08 L and Rs 1.33 L.

  "You took 8,000 of 3272 last year across six months and none this year"
  is a different conversation from a recommendation.

ALSO SHOW, separately: which top-80 SKUs they ALREADY buy, with their
quantity against the peer median.

  "You buy C62 at half what your peers buy" is a better opening than a new
  product.

MINIMUM HISTORY. Prompt 101 found 4,279 of 9,078 retailers meet 10 SKUs
and 3 months - 47%.

  Below that threshold: show known purchases, label limited history, and
  suppress change-based flags. A retailer with two SKUs in one month has no
  trend to read.

  Print how many retailers fall each side.

COVERAGE STATEMENT on the tab: FY2025-26 complete; FY2026-27 to July;
August partial through 19 August; September unavailable.
```

## Section D — Tab 5: top 80%, state versus India

Needs no customer history, so it works for every new prospect from day one.

```
Code change. Tab 5.

THE VERSIONED TOP-80 LIST with TWO demand columns side by side:

  what this SKU does IN THIS STATE      value, quantity, rank
  what it does ACROSS INDIA             value, quantity, rank

Sorted by state demand, so a new customer is pitched what moves where they
are.

THE GAP BETWEEN THE COLUMNS IS THE INSIGHT. A SKU ranked 12th nationally
and 180th in this state is either an opportunity or a product the market
rejects. Flag large divergences BOTH ways:

  strong nationally, weak here    -> possible opportunity
  strong here, weak nationally    -> local strength, protect it

Use primary sale_line for the state ranking - it has state_canon and does
not carry the secondary geography gap.

STATE THE SNAPSHOT VERSION AND DATE on the tab. A salesperson comparing
two customers a week apart must know whether the list changed.
```

## Notes

- **Section B is the part that must not be rushed.** If AI Sales Plan computes peer
  penetration differently from SKU Deep Dive, two pages will disagree about one
  customer — and the audit was explicit that the existing measures use different
  sources, entities and periods and must not be conflated.
- **B1's peer-size floor matters more than the algorithm.** A peer signal from three
  retailers is noise presented as evidence. Better to return unavailable.
- **C's STOPPED flag is the single most valuable output on this page**, and nothing
  in the app surfaces it today.
- **D ships without any customer history**, so it is useful the day it lands — and
  it is the only part of this page that works for a prospect who has never bought
  anything.

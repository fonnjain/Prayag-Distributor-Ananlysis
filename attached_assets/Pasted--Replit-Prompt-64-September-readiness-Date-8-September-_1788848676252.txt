## Notes

- **A4 is the one that shows why this matters now.** If a September sale resolves
  an August MRP, then every discount figure for the month so far is computed on
  the wrong price, and it compounds daily.
- **The "report, do not remove" instruction in Section B is not boilerplate.** The
  development load deleted five rows because they lacked provenance. Against
  production that instinct is exactly wrong — a row with no provenance is the
  evidence, not the problem.
- **C4 is the lesson from August in one question.** A month approaching freeze with
  an empty dataset should be visible before the freeze, not after. That single
  check is worth more than recovering August would have been.

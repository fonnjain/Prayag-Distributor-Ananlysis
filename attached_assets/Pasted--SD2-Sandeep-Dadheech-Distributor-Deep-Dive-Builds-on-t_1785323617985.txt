## Why this build matters more than the last

Anant Singh's territory produced the strongest relationship found in this project:

- Retailers **with** an assigned distributor: **96.0% active**
- Retailers **without** one: **5.0% active**
- A retailer with a distributor is **19 times more likely** to be ordering
- Unassigned share correlated with achievement at **−0.90**

That was **854 retailers, one territory, ten members.** Sandeep has **6,248 retailers, eight states, 71 members** — seven times the base and a completely different geography.

**If it holds here, it is a company-level finding. If it does not, that is equally worth knowing.** Carry the method, never Anant Singh's ratios.

## What to watch

**Direct dealers are almost absent here.** One member of 71, ₹1,40,628. Anant Singh had three of ten and ₹14.49 L. If the blank-distributor group comes back large, the classification is wrong — do not accept it as newly discovered business.

**Distributor name collisions are more likely than member ones.** Members are typed once into a roster; distributor names are typed by every salesperson independently, into 6,248 rows. Expect near-duplicates and do not let them be merged silently.

**West Bengal is 30 members and 2,707 retailers** — larger than Anant Singh's entire territory three times over. If anything times out, it is there.

**Do not let the correlation be assumed.** The value of running this on a second territory is precisely that it might not hold. A result of −0.30 would be as useful as −0.90, and reporting a weak correlation honestly is the point of the exercise.

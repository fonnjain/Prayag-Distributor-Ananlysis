## Notes

- **Block 1 is the whole request.** What the person costs, what they returned, and
  the ratio between them — against a benchmark, at the top, in one line anyone can
  act on.
- **The peer comparison is what turns a number into a decision.** 36.1% means
  nothing alone. 36.1% against a company norm of around 3% is a conversation with
  a state head.
- **B1 is a genuine inconsistency** and would undermine the sheet if anyone checked
  it. 46 + 18 = 64 against a stated total of 59.
- **The data-integrity columns are not wasted** — they should exist, on a working
  sheet. The objection is to them sharing space with a decision.
